# Six Layers for AI Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/six-layers-for-ai-governance-2.png)

## Summary
A six-layer operating model organizes AI governance from inventory and responsible deployment through security, testing, human oversight, and compliance or audit. The model links adoption with defense and governance activities across the AI lifecycle.

## Key points
- AI inventory covers system risk, ownership, classification, model and tool usage, and registry visibility.
- Responsible deployment includes use-case selection, architecture, model choice, safety practices, change control, and DevSecOps integration.
- AI security and access plus testing and monitoring address permissions, evaluation, threats, detection, and response.
- Human oversight and compliance or audit add approval, escalation, alignment, evidence, auditability, and reporting.
