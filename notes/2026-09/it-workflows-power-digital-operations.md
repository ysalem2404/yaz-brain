# IT Workflows That Power Digital Operations

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/it-workflows-power-digital-operations.png)

## Summary
An IT-operations workflow map shows how strategy and demand, service experience, change and release, cloud FinOps, identity lifecycle, endpoint workplace operations, and AIOps incident automation connect.

## Key points
- Digital operations spans service desk and experience, change and release flow, cloud FinOps and platform, identity access lifecycle, and endpoint workplace operations.
- The workflows make business decisions explicit: estimate effort, escalate when needed, plan releases, allocate spend, match access roles, and deploy patches.
- AIOps and incident automation correlate events across the operating flows to improve response and reduce manual handoffs.

## Why I saved this
A useful systems view of IT as interconnected workflows rather than a set of isolated tools or queues.
