# Vibe Coders Think Full-Stack Production Is Reality

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/vibe-coders-think-full-stack-production-is-reality.png)

## Summary
A production-readiness infographic challenges the idea that a generated frontend is a finished product, highlighting backend, security, row-level security, rate limiting, caching, CDN, and error tracking concerns.

## Key points
- The capture contrasts frontend work with the broader full-stack responsibilities required for production.
- Production concerns include backend services, security, row-level security, rate limiting, caching and CDN, and error tracking and monitoring.
- The implied lesson is that a prototype can look complete while reliability, authorization, performance, and observability remain unfinished.

## Why I saved this
A sharp checklist for separating “the UI works” from a secure, scalable, observable production application.
