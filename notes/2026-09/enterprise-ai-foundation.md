# Enterprise AI Foundation

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/enterprise-ai-foundation.png)

## TL;DR
An enterprise architecture turns isolated AI solutions into reusable capabilities through shared technical foundations, governance, lifecycle controls, and an interoperability layer.

## Key points
- AI experiences span apps, copilots, assistants, agents, chat and Q&A, employee productivity, business-process automation, and domain agents.
- The reusable foundation includes AI and agent orchestration, enterprise knowledge, model and AI services, tools and integration, evaluation and observability, architecture standards, an AI registry, audit and evidence, and lifecycle management.
- Governance and lifecycle controls include identity and access, AI governance, risk management, human-in-the-loop, evaluation and testing, auditability, cost and FinOps management, and guardrails.
- The interoperability layer connects data, APIs, events, MCP, A2A, and standard connectors to data platforms, enterprise SaaS, OT/IoT, and external partners.
