# Human Analyst Impact on Threat Hunting

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/human-analyst-impact-on-threat-hunting.png)

## Summary
A threat-hunting slide separates deterministic telemetry, intelligence, semantic layering, and risk presence from the AI analyst's probabilistic assessment of impact.

## Key points
- Telemetry and intelligence are treated as facts rather than AI-generated claims.
- Semantic layering and determination of risk presence rely on deterministic algorithms and structured inputs.
- The AI analyst is presented as the only nondeterministic variable, responsible for opining on the impact of risk.
- A human analyst reviews the AI analysis for correctness, preserving a human-in-the-loop control point.

## Why I saved this
A crisp boundary model for using AI in security operations without allowing probabilistic interpretation to redefine the underlying evidence.
