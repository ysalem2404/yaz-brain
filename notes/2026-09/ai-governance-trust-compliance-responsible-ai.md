# AI Governance: Trust, Compliance, and Responsible AI

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/ai-governance-trust-compliance-responsible-ai.png)

## Summary
An AI Frontier infographic frames AI governance as the policies, roles, processes, controls, and oversight needed to build trustworthy, secure, ethical, and sustainable AI. It connects Responsible AI, ISO/IEC 42001, and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework to accountable enterprise adoption.

## Key points
- AI governance asks who is accountable, what risks a system creates, what data it can access, whether it is appropriate for its use case, how behavior is monitored, and what happens when something goes wrong.
- Responsible AI emphasizes fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, reliability, and human oversight; it is presented as enterprise risk and cybersecurity work, not only ethics.
- ISO/IEC 42001 provides a management-system approach, while NIST AI RMF organizes work around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.
- The central thesis is that governance alongside AI enables safe innovation and trusted, sustainable enterprise AI.
