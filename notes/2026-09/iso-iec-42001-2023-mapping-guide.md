# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Mapping Guide

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/iso-iec-42001-2023-mapping-guide.png)

## Summary
An ISO/IEC 42001:2023 mapping guide connects AI management-system clauses to lifecycle activities and controls spanning context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement.

## Key points
- The guide maps an AI management system to context and scope, leadership accountability, planning and impact assessment, competence and awareness, documented information, operation, monitoring, and improvement.
- Lifecycle considerations include human-centered values, transparency, accountability, fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and data governance, security, and performance evaluation.
- Control activities include AI system lifecycle management, bias detection and mitigation, human oversight, monitoring and measurement, internal audit, management review, corrective action, and continual improvement.
- The framework is positioned alongside information-security and quality-management systems as part of a broader control environment.
