# Six AI Strategy Frameworks: Match the Method to the Decision

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/six-ai-strategy-frameworks-decision-map.png)

## Summary
A decision-led comparison of six AI strategy frameworks spans use-case selection, business-model redesign, operating-model change, value capture, and lifecycle risk governance.

## Key points
- BCG's Deploy, Reshape, Invent distinguishes task automation, workflow redesign, and entirely new offerings.
- Gartner's AI Opportunity Radar maps possible use cases across business functions and levels of ambition.
- Bain's AI-Era Operating Model focuses on roles, accountabilities, processes, leadership, and ways of working.
- BCG's 10-20-70 heuristic puts 10% of emphasis on algorithms, 20% on technology and data, and 70% on people and process.
- McKinsey's six value-capture dimensions are strategy, talent, operating model, technology, data, and adoption/scaling.
- NIST's Govern, Map, Measure, Manage cycle organizes AI risk across the lifecycle.
