# NIST AI RMF 1.0 Core Functions

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/nist-ai-rmf-1-0-core-functions.png)

## TL;DR
A visual summary of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework organizes trustworthy-AI work around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage, with assurance and readiness as a recurring outcome.

## Key points
- Govern sets direction through risk appetite, principles, roles, accountability, policy alignment, and a responsible-AI culture.
- Map builds a clear view of risks and opportunities by identifying use cases, assessing impacts, mapping dependencies, and understanding the supply chain.
- Measure validates and tests systems, monitors performance and safety, assesses bias, tracks incidents, and compares results against defined metrics.
- Manage acts on findings through risk treatment, vulnerability mitigation, model-lifecycle management, incident response, supplier management, documentation, continuous monitoring, and audit preparation.
