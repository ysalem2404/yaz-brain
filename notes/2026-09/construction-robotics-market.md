# Construction Robotics Market

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/construction-robotics-market.png)

## Summary
A market infographic groups construction robotics applications and estimates growth from USD 6.55B in 2025 to USD 15.39B in 2030 at an 18.6% CAGR.

## Key points
- Application areas include autonomous construction equipment, demolition, bricklaying and masonry, 3D construction printing, concrete finishing, robotic welding, rebar installation, material handling, surveying and inspection, and autonomous excavation.
- The infographic attributes growth to skilled-labor shortages, rising construction and labor costs, safer and more productive jobsites, AI and computer vision, autonomous systems, BIM and digital twins, and remote monitoring.
- The visual lists a broad vendor landscape including Caterpillar, Komatsu, Built Robotics, Dusty Robotics, Hilti, Siemens, Trimble, Topcon, and Leica Geosystems.

## Why I saved this
Market sizing and a vendor landscape for construction robotics in one place — useful for the construction-AI thread in the vault, though the figures are a market-research projection and should be attributed rather than treated as fact.
