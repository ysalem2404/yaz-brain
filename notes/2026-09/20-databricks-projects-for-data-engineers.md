# 20 Databricks Projects for Data Engineers

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/20-databricks-projects-for-data-engineers.png)

## Summary
A project menu for data engineers groups hands-on Databricks builds across real-time analytics, IoT and digital-twin data, lakehouse and medallion pipelines, streaming, Unity Catalog, dbt, and anomaly detection.

## Key points
- The top row covers a real-time point-of-sale pipeline with DLT, an IoT digital-twin data platform, Databricks end-to-end lakehouse demos, data-engineer hands-on projects, and a Formula 1 modern data stack.
- The middle row expands into Spark declarative pipelines, e-commerce PySpark, scalable FMCG data, Kafka plus structured streaming with Delta Lake, and IoT streaming with anomaly detection.
- Additional projects cover streaming with Delta Live Tables, Azure Databricks plus Unity Catalog ETL, declarative medallion pipelines, Formula 1 data engineering, retail SCD Type 1 and 2, and Databricks with dbt.
- The final examples include production-style Azure Databricks, an FMCG medallion lakehouse, and an end-to-end Databricks lakehouse project.

## Why I saved this
A compact idea bank for turning Databricks capabilities into portfolio projects and for mapping data-engineering patterns to concrete business scenarios.
