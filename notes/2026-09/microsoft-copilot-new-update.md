# Microsoft Copilot New Update

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/microsoft-copilot-new-update.png)

## TL;DR
A Microsoft product update frames Copilot as a unified experience spanning Home, Office, Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and FinOps for AI.

## Key points
- Home brings Chat and Cowork together with recent activity, suggestions, and a way to resume work.
- Office in Copilot lets users create, edit, and iterate on Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files with context preserved.
- Cowork delegates end-to-end work such as RFP responses, launch kits, customer briefings, and financial packages.
- Code lets people build, test, and safely run their own solutions; Autopilot is a persistent proactive agent, while FinOps for AI tracks spend, measures value, and optimizes usage.
