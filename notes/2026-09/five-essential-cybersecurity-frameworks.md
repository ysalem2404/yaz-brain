# Five Essential Cybersecurity Frameworks

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/five-essential-cybersecurity-frameworks.png)

## Summary
A comparison of five security frameworks maps each to a different job: outcome-based risk governance, an auditable ISMS, prioritized safeguards, adversary behavior analysis, and reduced implicit trust.

## Key points
- NIST CSF 2.0 is presented for cyber-risk governance and outcome improvement across Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.
- ISO/IEC 27001 supports an auditable security management system through context, risk treatment, control operation, Annex A controls, and continual improvement.
- CIS Controls v8.1 prioritize practical safeguards through Implementation Groups 1–3, from cyber hygiene to advanced defense.
- MITRE ATT&CK maps adversary behavior from reconnaissance and initial access through execution, persistence, and impact to detection gaps and SOC tuning.
- Zero Trust reduces implicit trust across identities, devices, networks, applications, workloads, and data, supported by visibility, automation, and governance.
