# Enterprise AI Governance Model

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/enterprise-ai-governance-model-2.png)

## Summary
An enterprise AI governance model lists ten domains leaders should monitor across the AI lifecycle: data governance, model validation, bias and fairness, human oversight, regulatory compliance, security controls, vendor risk, audit logging, model monitoring, and incident response.

## Key points
- Data governance and model validation establish ownership, lineage, benchmarks, performance thresholds, and independent review.
- Bias and fairness, human oversight, and regulatory compliance address discriminatory outcomes, escalation and override paths, and policy or legal requirements.
- Security controls, vendor risk, and audit logging cover prompt attacks, data leakage, third-party dependencies, supply-chain exposure, and traceable AI actions.
- Model monitoring and incident response create feedback loops for drift, anomalies, failures, misuse, recovery, and continuous improvement.
