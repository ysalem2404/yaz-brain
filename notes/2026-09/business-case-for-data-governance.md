# How to Build a Business Case for Data Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/business-case-for-data-governance.png)

## Summary
A practical framework for making data governance fundable shifts from generic maturity language to a quantified business case. It ties material risk, value levers, governance design, decision authority, and expected impact to board-level outcomes.

## Key points
- Weak pitches ask for better metadata, lineage, or maturity; stronger cases quantify impact, focus on one high-value domain, define a 90-day measurable outcome, and secure an executive sponsor.
- Material risks include regulatory exposure, incorrect reporting, forecast errors, and AI instability; value levers include revenue leakage, pricing optimization, customer churn, and decision delays.
- Governance design should define metric ownership, change control, conflict resolution, decision rights, and approval structures.
- Expected outcomes include protected revenue, faster decisions, reduced rework, trusted numbers, scalable AI, board confidence, and audit evidence in minutes.
