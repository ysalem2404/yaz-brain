# AI in Project Management

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/ai-in-project-management.png)

## TL;DR
A workshop outline treats AI project management as both a leadership and delivery discipline, pairing governance and responsible AI with practical use across the project lifecycle.

## Key points
- Day 1 covers the global AI project landscape, lifecycle and value delivery, AI project-team leadership, human-AI collaboration, governance, regulation, accountability, data governance, privacy, cybersecurity, and quality assurance.
- Day 2 applies AI to project work through prompt engineering, scenario briefings, stakeholder analysis, requirements and system design, scheduling, budgets, risk, quality, communications, governance, monitoring, control, reporting, and closeout.
- The outline explicitly pairs AI output validation with professional judgment rather than treating generated output as self-validating.
- Tools explored include ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, with an integrated leadership challenge and real-world project scenarios.
