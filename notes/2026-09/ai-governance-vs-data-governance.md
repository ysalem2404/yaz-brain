# AI Governance vs. Data Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/ai-governance-vs-data-governance.png)

## Summary
An infographic contrasts data governance for the raw material with AI governance for model behavior, arguing that both must work together across the data-to-decision lifecycle.

## Key points
- Data governance covers data assets, quality, lineage, access, ownership, and usage rights; AI governance covers how models are developed, validated, deployed, monitored, and used.
- The disciplines mitigate different risks and use different controls, including metadata and lineage versus validation, bias testing, explainability, oversight, and monitoring.
- They involve different owners and operate on different clocks, but both are needed for clean inputs, explainable outputs, and accountable decisions.
- The visual cites a Gartner finding that successful AI initiatives invest substantially more in foundations such as data quality, governance, people, and change management.

## Why I saved this
A clear governance-gap diagnostic: pointing only to the data team or only to the model team does not answer why an AI decision happened.
