# AI Org Chart 2026

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/ai-org-chart-2026.png)

## Summary
An AI organization chart describes the roles needed to turn executive AI ambition into production systems, covering strategy, models, agents, data, governance, security, workflows, and measurable value.

## Key points
- A Chief AI Officer owns vision, governance, adoption, and the translation of executive ambition into production systems.
- AI engineering builds the stack, selects model vendors, and runs models, agents, evaluations, budgets, and incident drills.
- Data and governance leadership owns quality, access, privacy, compliance, guardrails, and whether launches are possible.
- The operating model ties AI work to productivity gains, cost savings, workflow integration, and weekly KPIs rather than experiments alone.
