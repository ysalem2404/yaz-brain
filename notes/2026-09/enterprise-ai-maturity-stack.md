# Enterprise AI Maturity Stack

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/enterprise-ai-maturity-stack.png)

## Summary
A six-level maturity ladder shows how organizations move from manual work to AI assistance, workflow automation, autonomous agency, AI-enabled workforce roles, and an enterprise-level partner model.

## Key points
- Level 0 is manual status-quo work; Level 1 uses chat prompts to accelerate individual tasks.
- Level 2 converts repetitive work into applications; Level 3 agents accept goals, plan, and run workflows autonomously.
- Level 4 assigns AI an end-to-end role alongside employees, while Level 5 imagines swarms of AI systems operating at department scale and aligning to team goals.
- The framework distinguishes the value delta and economic model at each step, from saved labor hours and fixed fees to per-outcome and subscription-like arrangements.
