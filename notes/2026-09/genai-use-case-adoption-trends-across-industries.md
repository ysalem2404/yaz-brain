# GenAI Use-Case Adoption Trends Across Industries

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/genai-use-case-adoption-trends-across-industries.png)

## Summary
A Gartner infographic maps GenAI use-case adoption across industries and frames domain-specific agents around a context layer, workflow intelligence, models, guardrails, observability, governance, FinOps, and security.

## Key points
- The map compares use cases including knowledge management and search, conversational language interfaces, analysis, text and translation, simulation and scenario planning, image/audio/video generation, and software development.
- Three pillars for domain-specific agents are shown as a context layer, models, and workflow intelligence, surrounded by guardrails, observability, governance, FinOps, and security.
- The context layer combines domain data sources, document types, domain-centric document processing, and domain-specific skills.
- Workflow intelligence connects traditional ML algorithms with domain-specific workflow templates and reusable skills.

## Why I saved this
A useful frame for evaluating whether an AI agent is truly domain-specific: it needs context, workflow understanding, and operating controls, not only a model.
