## Summary

## Key points
- The ecosystem spans model access, agent frameworks, retrieval and embeddings, tool connectivity through MCP, and vector data infrastructure.
- Security, observability, memory, and agent layers are treated as first-class parts of an application stack rather than add-ons.
- The infographic is useful as a vendor and capability map for comparing the components needed to build AI applications.
