# EPC Project Lifecycle: Opportunity to Handover

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/epc-project-lifecycle-opportunity-to-handover.png)

## Summary
A stage-gate map of the engineering, procurement and construction lifecycle from opportunity and bid through feasibility, FEED, detailed engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, and handover.

## Key points
- The lifecycle moves through eight phases: opportunity and bid; feasibility and concept; FEED and planning; detailed engineering; procurement; construction; pre-commissioning and commissioning; and handover and closeout.
- Decision gates include bid approval, concept freeze, design freeze, purchase release, ready for construction, ready for start-up, and final acceptance.
- Leadership controls span scope, schedule, cost, quality, HSE, risk, interfaces, changes, and stakeholders, with reviews and reporting flowing across every phase.

## Why I saved this
A clean stage-gate reference for EPC delivery — the named decision gates (bid approval, concept freeze, design freeze, purchase release, ready for construction, ready for start-up, final acceptance) are the useful part for mapping where AI or data controls can attach to an existing project lifecycle.
