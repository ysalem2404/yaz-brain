# ISO 42001 AI Governance Checklist

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/iso-42001-ai-governance-checklist.png)

## Summary
An ISO 42001 checklist frames an AI management system around organizational context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement, and Annex A reference controls.

## Key points
- The objective is an AI Management System (AIMS) that delivers responsible, trustworthy, beneficial AI while managing risk and maximizing opportunities.
- Auditors should test context of the organization, leadership, planning, and support, including scope, interested parties, policy, roles, risk assessment, objectives, resources, competence, communications, and documented information.
- Operation and performance evaluation cover lifecycle controls, AI risk assessment and treatment, impact assessment, monitoring, measurement, internal audit, and management review; improvement covers continual improvement and corrective action.
- Annex A reference controls span AI-specific policy, internal organization, resources, impact assessment, lifecycle, data, information, use, and third-party/customer relationships.
