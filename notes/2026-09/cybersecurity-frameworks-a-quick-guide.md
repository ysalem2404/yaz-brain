# Cybersecurity Frameworks: A Quick Guide

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/cybersecurity-frameworks-a-quick-guide.png)

## Summary
A side-by-side guide distinguishes five cybersecurity frameworks by their focus areas and best-fit uses, from risk governance to threat-informed detection.

## Key points
- NIST CSF 2.0 is framed around governing cyber risk, identifying critical assets, protecting services, detecting events, and responding/recovering.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 centers on ISMS scope, risk treatment, controls, audit evidence, and continual improvement; CIS Controls v8.1 prioritizes practical safeguards.
- CISA's Zero Trust Maturity Model focuses on identity, devices, networks, applications, data, and visibility; MITRE ATT&CK supports mapping adversary behavior and detection gaps.
- The infographic maps framework choice to needs such as board reporting, certification readiness, cloud hardening, hybrid-work access, threat hunting, and SOC tuning.
