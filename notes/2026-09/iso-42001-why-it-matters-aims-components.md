# ISO 42001: Why It Matters and AIMS Components

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/iso-42001-why-it-matters-aims-components.png)

## Summary
A concise ISO 42001 explainer links trust, risk reduction, regulatory alignment, and structured governance to the core components of an AI Management System.

## Key points
- The infographic frames ISO 42001 as a way to build ethical and transparent AI, mitigate bias/security/privacy risks, and support compliance with regimes such as the EU AI Act and GDPR.
- AIMS components include an AI risk-management framework, data and model security, bias assessment and mitigation, incident response and recovery, transparency and explainability, and ongoing improvement and audits.
- Its central promise is repeatable governance and a trusted signal rather than a one-time checklist.
