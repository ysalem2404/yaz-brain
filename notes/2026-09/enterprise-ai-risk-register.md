# Enterprise AI Risk Register

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/enterprise-ai-risk-register.png)

## Summary
A sample AI risk register links ten lifecycle risks to accountable owners, controls, inherent and residual ratings, and a recurring treatment workflow.

## Key points
- Risks include unfair outcomes, prompt/context injection, sensitive-information exposure, excessive agency, data/model/memory poisoning, supply-chain compromise, vector weaknesses, misinformation, unbounded consumption, and unsafe output handling.
- Controls include impact and bias testing, trust boundaries, input/tool validation, data minimization, least agency, human approval gates, provenance, vendor assurance, retrieval isolation, grounded evaluation, usage limits, and output validation.
- Each record carries an owner, inherent and residual rating, treatment response, lifecycle context, evidence, status, due date, regulatory mapping, and acceptance authority.
- The operating loop is capture, analyze, decide, control, verify, and reassess; assurance checks test oversight, traceability, least privilege, evaluation evidence, red teaming, incident linkage, and residual-risk approval.
