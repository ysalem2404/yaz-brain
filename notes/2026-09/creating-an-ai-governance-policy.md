# Creating an AI Governance Policy

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/creating-an-ai-governance-policy.png)

## Summary
A nine-part policy framework turns responsible-AI principles into expectations for organizational scope, accountability, risk, data, vendors, oversight, incidents, and review.

## Key points
- The policy defines its purpose and scope, who it applies to, roles and approval authorities, stakeholders, and the governance structure.
- It sets responsible-AI principles, impact-assessment triggers and reassessment frequency, and requirements for data handling, privacy, security, and confidential information.
- Third-party provisions address due diligence, contractual controls, vendor risk, data usage, and model-training practices; human-oversight provisions define review and intervention requirements.
- Incident management establishes reporting, escalation, investigation, and remediation; monitoring and review evaluate effectiveness and drive updates as systems, laws, and business needs change.
