# BIM vs Digital Twin

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/bim-vs-digital-twin.png)

## TL;DR
A built-environment infographic distinguishes BIM's structured, governed asset information from digital twins' connected operational context and feedback loops.

## Key points
- BIM provides a structured digital representation of geometry, properties, and asset information for design, delivery, coordination, handover, and whole-life information requirements.
- Digital twins connect a physical asset, system, or environment to operational data, decision feedback, synchronization, analytics, and forecasting.
- BIM workflows emphasize controlled collaboration, CDE approvals, revisions, traceability, and open exchange through IFC, BCF, and machine-checkable requirements.
- The central message is that a digital twin does not replace BIM: BIM can provide trusted structured information, while the twin connects it to operational context and feedback.
