# India AI Governance Framework: Principles to Practice

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/india-ai-governance-framework-principles-to-practice.png)

## Summary
A nine-layer operating framework translates responsible-AI principles into enterprise controls, with India-specific emphasis on data sovereignty, public-sector services, and the DPDP Act.

## Key points
- The framework layers AI strategy/value, inventory/discovery, risk/impact management, responsible development/deployment, security/access, testing/monitoring, human oversight/accountability, compliance/audit/reporting, and continual improvement.
- The side panel emphasizes alignment with the IndiaAI Mission, privacy and consent under the DPDP Act 2023, digital sovereignty, high-impact public services, and scalable implementation from ministries to field-level services.
- The operating sequence is organized as Adopt (build responsibly), Defend (test and protect), and Govern (assure and sustain).
- Emerging risks called out include bias, deepfakes, synthetic content, misinformation, and broader societal impact.
