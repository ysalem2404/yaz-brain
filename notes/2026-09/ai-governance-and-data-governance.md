# AI Governance and Data Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/ai-governance-and-data-governance.png)

## Summary
A governance comparison explains why AI governance and data governance address different objects and risks but must operate together across the data-to-decision lifecycle.

## Key points
- Data governance manages data quality, lineage, access, ownership, and usage rights; AI governance manages how models are developed, validated, deployed, monitored, and used.
- Their controls differ: metadata and access controls protect data, while validation, bias testing, explainability, human oversight, monitoring, and approvals govern AI behavior.
- Data governance often follows audits and policy updates, whereas AI governance moves with development, deployment, retraining, monitoring, and drift.
- The central recommendation is an integrated ecosystem that prepares and governs data, validates and deploys AI responsibly, and monitors and improves the full lifecycle.
