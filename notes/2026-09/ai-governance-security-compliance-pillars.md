# AI Governance vs. AI Security vs. AI Compliance

## Summary

A three-circle framework distinguishes AI governance, AI security, and AI compliance as complementary pillars of a trustworthy AI agent. Governance provides direction through strategy, policy, accountability, culture, and risk oversight; security provides protection for data, models, and infrastructure; compliance provides proof through mapped requirements, audits, evidence, and continuous monitoring.

## Key Points

- AI governance defines how AI should be used, who owns decisions, and what responsible use means inside the organization.
- AI security protects systems and access, detects threats and anomalies, responds and recovers, and builds resilience against prompt injection, data poisoning, and model theft.
- AI compliance maps requirements to AI systems, understands laws and standards, maintains evidence, passes assessments, and monitors continuously.
- The center of the overlap is responsible and trusted AI: safe, protected, provable, and aligned with business goals.
