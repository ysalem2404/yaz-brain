# The Obsidian and Databricks Advantage

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/obsidian-databricks-advantage.png)

## Summary
A security architecture combines curated production data and notebook workflows with a knowledge graph that normalizes identities, access, activity, threats, incidents, and attacks.

## Key points
- The knowledge graph correlates identity, session, token, activity, threat, incident, signal, and attack relationships into a live risk map.
- Inputs include identity providers, SaaS and AI integrations, endpoint browsers, and real-world incident data.
- The architecture positions curated data and notebook-based threat research alongside dynamic security investigations.
- The overall value proposition is seamless threat hunting through a unified graph and data foundation.

## Why I saved this
A strong example of contextual security analytics: the graph is useful because it connects identity, behavior, and threat evidence across systems.
