# 8 Ways to Build AI Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/eight-ways-to-build-ai-governance.png)

## Summary
An eight-part AI governance model links data, models, responsible AI, risk, security and privacy, compliance, auditability, and monitoring into one operating system for trustworthy AI.

## Key points
- The eight dimensions are audit and accountability, data governance, model governance, responsible AI, risk management, security and privacy, compliance and regulation, and monitoring and observability.
- Auditability emphasizes audit logs, decision traceability, immutable records, governance reporting, and internal and external audits.
- Data governance covers ownership, quality and validation, metadata, catalogs, discovery, lineage, and traceability; model governance covers inventory, versioning, approvals, retraining, decommissioning, model cards, and datasheets.
- The guiding principles are trust and transparency, human-centric design, fairness and inclusion, privacy by design, continuous improvement, and accountability.
