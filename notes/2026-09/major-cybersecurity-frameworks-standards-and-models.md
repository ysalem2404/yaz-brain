## Key points
- The table differentiates cyber-risk programs, ISMS certification, security-control guidance, prioritized safeguards, security/privacy catalogs, zero-trust architecture and maturity, and secure software development.
- It also includes MITRE ATT&CK for threat behavior and detection validation; OWASP ASVS for application/API verification; and an OWASP LLM application security entry.
- Cloud Controls Matrix, PCI DSS, and ISA/IEC 62443 cover cloud assurance, payment-data protection, and operational-technology/industrial-control environments respectively.
