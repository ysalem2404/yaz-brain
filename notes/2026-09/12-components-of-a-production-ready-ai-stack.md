# 12 Components of a Production-Ready AI Stack

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/12-components-of-a-production-ready-ai-stack.png)

## Summary
A production AI stack checklist connects enterprise data, orchestration, integration, governance, guardrails, retrieval, human approval, model selection, gateways, observability, and lifecycle operations.

## Key points
- The stack connects operational and customer systems, moves data reliably, coordinates models and agents, and integrates AI with applications and workflows.
- Governance and safety layers cover ownership, quality, lineage, privacy, enterprise access, unsafe inputs and outputs, policy violations, and human approval before sensitive actions.
- Retrieval and model layers ground responses in enterprise documents, support vector search, and select models based on workload and business requirements.
- AI gateways, monitoring, observability, and lifecycle management centralize routing, security, policies, costs, quality, latency, failures, drift, versions, deployments, audits, and improvement.

A useful production architecture checklist that places models inside the data, control, integration, and operating systems they depend on.
