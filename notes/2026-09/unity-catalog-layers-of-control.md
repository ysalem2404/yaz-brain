# Unity Catalog Layers of Control

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/unity-catalog-layers-of-control.png)

## Summary
A Unity Catalog control model combines task-fit permissions, attribute-based policies for scale, and role-based access for isolation.

## Key points
- Granular permissions grant only the privileges a task needs.
- ABAC policies use attribute-based classification to apply rules automatically at scale.
- RBAC uses roles to isolate active access by task.
- The slide presents the three layers as complementary rather than mutually exclusive choices.

## Why I saved this
A compact mental model for designing authorization as a layered system: narrow privileges, scalable policy conditions, and isolated task roles.
