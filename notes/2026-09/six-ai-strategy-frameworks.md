# 6 AI Strategy Frameworks

**TL;DR**
- A decision-oriented cheat sheet presents six AI strategy frameworks: BCG's Deploy, Reshape, Invent; Gartner's AI Opportunity Radar; Bain's AI-Era Operating Model; BCG's 10-20-70; McKinsey's Six Dimensions of AI Value Capture; and NIST's Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. Together they span use-case selection, operating-model change, value capture, and lifecycle risk control.

## Content
6 AI Strategy Frameworks. Start with the decision, then choose the framework. 1. Deploy, Reshape, Invent (BCG): separates productivity gains from workflow redesign and entirely new offerings. 2. AI Opportunity Radar (Gartner): maps potential AI use cases across the business. 3. Bain: AI-Era Operating Model: looks at roles, accountabilities, processes, leadership, and ways of working. 4. BCG: 10-20-70: shifts attention beyond algorithms toward technology, data, people, and process; 10% algorithms, 20% tech and data, 70% people and process. 5. Six Dimensions of AI Value Capture (McKinsey): connects strategy, talent, operating model, technology, data, and adoption. 6. Govern, Map, Measure, Manage (NIST): structures AI risk across the lifecycle through context, governance, measurement, and response/improvement.
