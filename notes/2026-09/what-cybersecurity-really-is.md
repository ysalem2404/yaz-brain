# What Cybersecurity Really Is

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/what-cybersecurity-really-is.png)

## TL;DR
A contrast infographic expands cybersecurity beyond familiar perimeter tools to a broader operating model focused on identity, exposure, cloud and data protection, detection, response, and resilience.

## Key points
- The familiar shorthand is firewalls, antivirus, and passwords.
- The broader model includes identity governance, exposure management, cloud and SaaS security, zero-trust access, AI security controls, data protection, detection engineering, incident response, and cyber resilience.
- The visual reframes cybersecurity as a lifecycle of prevention, visibility, detection, response, and recovery rather than a collection of isolated products.
- Its compact contrast is useful for explaining why modern security programs must cover identities, data, cloud systems, AI, and operational response together.
