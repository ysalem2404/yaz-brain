# Data Governance: A Complete Framework

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-09/data-governance-a-complete-framework.png)

## TL;DR
A framework links ownership and accountability to usable data, explicit decision authority, defensible analytics, and data that is ready for trusted AI.

## Key points
- The data foundation covers ownership registers, metric cards, data dictionaries, escalation playbooks, and quality scorecards so organizations know what they have and why it is reliable.
- Data management makes information usable through data products, data-mesh boundaries, automated policy checks, machine-readable definitions, and real-time lineage.
- Decision authority makes clear who decides what through an accountability chart and an explicit decision cadence.
- The target state is trusted analytics and AI-ready data: numbers that people can defend and machines can use.
