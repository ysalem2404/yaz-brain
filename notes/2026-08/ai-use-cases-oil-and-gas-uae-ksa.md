## Key points

- Reservoir optimization, seismic interpretation, drilling optimization, and production optimization apply AI to subsurface and field decisions.
- Digital twins and emissions monitoring connect operational models with asset visibility and sustainability goals.
- Supply-chain optimization extends AI beyond the field into materials, logistics, and planning.
- AI agents/agentic AI are positioned as an emerging operating pattern alongside established analytics and optimization use cases.
