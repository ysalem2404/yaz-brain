# Microsoft MAI Models: Enterprise Platform Pivot

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/microsoft-mai-models-enterprise-platform-pivot.png)

## Summary
A breaking-news infographic describes Microsoft’s MAI model family as a platform-level move toward owning model weights, controlling inference costs, and tuning models for Microsoft products and enterprise workflows.

## Key points
- The pictured lineup includes MAI-THINKING-1 for reasoning, MAI-CODE-1-FLASH for coding, MAI-IMAGE-2.5 for image generation, MAI-TRANSCRIBE 1.5 for speech-to-text, and MAI-VOICE-2 for multilingual voice
- The stated strategic benefits are lower inference cost, faster or product-specific performance, and more control over adaptation and fine-tuning
- The models are shown in or rolling out across Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, OneDrive, VS Code, GitHub Copilot, and Microsoft AI experiences
- The graphic should be treated as a dated snapshot of product claims rather than an independent benchmark

## Why I saved this
A time-stamped view of the platform economics behind a major model provider bringing more of the stack in-house.
