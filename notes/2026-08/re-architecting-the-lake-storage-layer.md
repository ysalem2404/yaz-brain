# Re-architecting the Lake Storage Layer

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/re-architecting-the-lake-storage-layer.png)

## Summary
A slide stating that the storage layer of the lake had to be re-architected, illustrated as compute separated from lake storage, with storage shown only partly filled.

## Key points
- The stated change is to the storage layer specifically, not the compute or query engine.
- Compute and lake storage are drawn as distinct stacked layers, reinforcing separation of the two.
- The framing is that scaling the lake was a storage-layer problem before it was a compute problem.

## Why I saved this
A compact reminder that lakehouse scaling limits often show up first in the storage layer — useful framing, though the slide is thin on specifics.
