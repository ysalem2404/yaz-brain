# Databricks Became Our Platform Team

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/databricks-became-our-platform-team.png)

## Summary
A platform-strategy slide contrasts rebuilding infrastructure with leveraging Databricks as the platform team, leaving the product team to focus on business logic, prompts, orchestration, and UX.

## Key points
- The leveraged substrate includes Workday identity, row-level ACL enforcement, secrets, Spark execution, scaling, model registry and versioning, tracing, audit logs, SQL warehouse/API, AI Gateway/routing, vector search, and Delta Lake governance.
- The built layer is intentionally narrower: business logic, prompts, agent orchestration, and frontend UX.
- The slide claims zero lines of authentication or authorization code on a governed enterprise-data product because ACLs are enforced in the substrate.

## Why I saved this
A strong visual argument for platform leverage and for measuring product differentiation above the governance substrate.
