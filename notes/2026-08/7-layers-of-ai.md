# 7 Layers of AI

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/7-layers-of-ai.png)

## Summary
A progression graphic places classical AI, machine learning, neural networks, deep learning, generative AI, agentic AI, and AGI on an expanding capability ladder.

## Key points
- The ladder moves from rule-based classical AI to machine learning, neural networks, deep learning, generative AI, agentic AI, and AGI.
- Each layer signals a broader capability: recognizing patterns, creating content, acting and planning, and ultimately general intelligence.

## Why I saved this
A concise orientation map for distinguishing the layers and ambitions commonly bundled under AI.
