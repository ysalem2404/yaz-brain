# Permissions Evolution — Granular Controls Across Privileges

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/permissions-evolution-granular-controls-across-privileges.png)

## Summary
A permissions-evolution slide extends the least-privilege idea across multiple privileges, showing how broad administrative access can be unbundled into narrower task-specific grants.

## Key points
- The framing is “the same unbundling, across more privileges,” suggesting a systematic redesign rather than a one-off permission split.
- An administrator is asked to ensure least-privilege access by assigning only the capability needed for a task.
- The slide complements earlier granular-control examples by emphasizing that the pattern should apply consistently across the permission model.

## Why I saved this
A concise design principle for auditing coarse permissions and converting them into a more defensible access model.
