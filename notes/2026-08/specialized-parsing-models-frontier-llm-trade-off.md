# Specialized Parsing Models — Frontier LLM Trade-off

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/specialized-parsing-models-frontier-llm-trade-off.png)

## Summary
A document-parsing slide contrasts general-purpose frontier LLMs with specialized parsing models, arguing that document parsing is output-token-heavy and can benefit from a lower-cost model built for the task.

## Key points
- Frontier LLMs may offer high accuracy but are large general-purpose models with significant cost.
- Document parsing produces substantial output and is therefore especially sensitive to token economics.
- Specialized models geared specifically to document parsing are presented as a route to high fidelity at a fraction of the cost.

## Why I saved this
A useful cost-and-quality lens for choosing a parser before adding a general LLM to the document workflow.
