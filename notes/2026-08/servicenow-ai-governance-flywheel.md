# ServiceNow AI Governance Flywheel

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/servicenow-ai-governance-flywheel.png)

## Summary
A ServiceNow AI governance flywheel frames enterprise oversight as a continuous loop: discover AI usage, assess risk and value, apply controls, and monitor and improve the estate.

## Key points
- Discovery and visibility establish an inventory of models, agents, prompts, and tools
- Governance applies policies, access controls, reviews, and human oversight where needed
- Risk and value monitoring feed improvements back into the control loop

## Why I saved this
A clear operating picture for governing a growing AI estate without treating governance as a one-time approval.
