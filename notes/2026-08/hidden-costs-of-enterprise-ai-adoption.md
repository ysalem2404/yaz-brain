# The Hidden Costs of Enterprise AI Adoption

## Summary

An Antriksh Gupta infographic presents enterprise AI adoption as a ring of often-underestimated cost centers rather than a model-license purchase. It covers data readiness, integration complexity, infrastructure and inference, LLMOps and monitoring, security and risk controls, governance and compliance, agent operations, human oversight, change management, and vendor or platform dependency.

## Key Points

- Data readiness costs include cleansing, enrichment, lineage, DSPM, and governance controls; poor data quality delays AI value.
- Integration complexity spans ERP/CRM connectivity, legacy systems, and API orchestration, and can exceed model costs.
- Infrastructure, inference, continuous evaluation, drift detection, observability, and high-availability deployments make compute and production oversight recurring costs.
- Security, governance, compliance, human oversight, and agent operations add controls for prompt injection, red teaming, HITL workflows, multi-agent orchestration, and regulatory readiness.
- Change management and vendor/platform dependency determine adoption and long-run switching costs.
