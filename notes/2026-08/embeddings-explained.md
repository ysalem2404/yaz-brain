# Embeddings Explained

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/embeddings-explained.png)

## Summary
An embeddings overview explains how text, images, or audio become high-dimensional vectors in which semantically similar items are placed near one another.

## Key points
- Embeddings convert content into numerical coordinates in a semantic vector space
- Similarity search, recommendations, clustering, and classification use these representations
- Common text-embedding families include OpenAI, BGE, E5, and Cohere models
- The same idea can represent text, image, audio, or other modalities

## Why I saved this
A bridge from model output to the vector-search systems used in retrieval and recommendation applications.
