# Five Layers of AI Guardrails

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/five-layers-of-ai-guardrails.png)

## Summary
A five-layer guardrails model organizes responsible AI across culture, governance, process, technical systems, and ethical principles.

## Key points
- Culture covers values and beliefs guiding behavior, while governance covers policies and ethical oversight
- Process addresses lifecycle-management procedures and technical systems cover controls for models and data pipelines
- The ethical layer emphasizes privacy, safety, accountability, explainability, human-centered design, and integrity
- The framework argues that all five layers matter for responsible deployment

A practical checklist for reviewing responsible-AI programs beyond model-level filters.
