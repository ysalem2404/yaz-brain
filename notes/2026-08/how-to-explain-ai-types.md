# How to Explain AI Types

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/how-to-explain-ai-types.png)

## Summary
A capability ladder explains AI types from predictive and classification systems through generative, workflow, knowledge, agentic, multi-agent, and product-integrated AI.

## Key points
- Traditional AI includes predictive analytics, classification, and anomaly detection for forecasting and spotting unusual patterns
- Generative AI creates content, while workflow automation plugs AI into routine tasks such as email sorting, meeting notes, and data cleaning
- Knowledge systems use retrieval-augmented generation to answer from business data; agents use APIs and tools to execute tasks
- Multi-agent orchestration coordinates specialized agents, and product integration embeds AI into products and services

## Why I saved this
A clear progression for explaining how AI capabilities build on one another without treating every problem as an autonomous-agent problem.
