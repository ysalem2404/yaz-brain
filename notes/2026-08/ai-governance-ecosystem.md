# AI Governance Ecosystem

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/ai-governance-ecosystem.png)

## Summary
An AI governance ecosystem map groups standards, regulations, risk frameworks, and industry or regional principles that organizations can use to govern AI responsibly.

## Key points
- Standards and frameworks shown include the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, GDPR, ISO 23894, ISO 27001, COSO, COBIT, and ISO 31000
- The map connects governance, risk, security, privacy, and accountability concerns rather than treating AI governance as a single policy
- Industry and regional principles are presented as a complementary layer to formal standards

## Why I saved this
A compact reference for orienting AI governance work across regulations, standards, and risk controls.
