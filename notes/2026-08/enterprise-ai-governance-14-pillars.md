# The Enterprise AI Governance Framework — 14 Pillars

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/enterprise-ai-governance-14-pillars.png)

## Summary
14-pillar enterprise AI governance framework with the tooling ecosystem mapped to each pillar: (1) AI Strategy & Vision, (2) Data Governance, (3) Privacy & Compliance, (4) IAM, (5) AI Security & Guardrails, (6) Model Governance, (7) Knowledge Governance (RAG), (8) Human-in-the-Loop, (9) Risk Management, (10) Monitoring & Observability, (11) Policy & Documentation, (12) Ethics & Responsible AI, (13) Incident Response, (14) Continuous Improvement. Each pillar names 3 concrete tools.

## Key points
- AI Security & Guardrails vendors: NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails, Guardrails AI, Azure AI Content Safety
- Knowledge Governance (RAG) explicitly separated as its own pillar — governance of what the AI can retrieve
- IAM stack: Okta / Auth0 / Microsoft Entra ID
- Incident Response ties AI failures to standard SRE tooling: PagerDuty, ServiceNow, Opsgenie
- MS Purview appears twice (Data Governance + Privacy) — reinforces its multi-role in the MS stack
