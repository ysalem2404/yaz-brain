# Governance at Scale Breaks in Three Places

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/governance-at-scale-breaks-in-three-places.png)

## Summary
A governance slide identifies three scaling failure points for access control: excessive privilege, inconsistent rules, and permission isolation across tasks.

## Key points
- Privilege for the task: broad privileges create unnecessary risk, so access should be scoped to the task.
- Consistency at scale: manual controls do not keep up as the organization grows; policies should apply automatically wherever conditions match.
- Isolation: permissions can accumulate across tasks, so only the right access should be active for the right context.
