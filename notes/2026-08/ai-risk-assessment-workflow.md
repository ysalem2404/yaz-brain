# AI Risk Assessment Workflow

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-risk-assessment-workflow.png)

## Summary
A structured AI risk assessment workflow guides responsible AI decisions from scope and context through risk identification, analysis, evaluation, treatment, approval, monitoring, and review.

## Key points
- Start by defining the AI system, intended use, stakeholders, context, and assessment scope.
- Identify and analyze risks across impact, likelihood, affected people, data, models, security, and operations.
- Evaluate risk against appetite and priorities, then select controls, owners, mitigations, escalation paths, and approvals.
- Monitor residual risk and changing conditions, document evidence, and revisit the assessment as the system or context changes.
