# 25 Pillars of a Modern Cybersecurity System

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/25-pillars-of-a-modern-cybersecurity-system.png)

## Summary
A systems view of modern cybersecurity organizes 25 capabilities across governance and risk, identity and access, threat and exposure management, security operations and resilience, and data, cloud, and application security.

## Key points
- The framework spans governance and risk, identity and access, threat and exposure management, security operations and resilience, and data, cloud, and application security.
- Specific controls include MFA, privileged access, phishing resistance, incident response, ransomware recovery, cloud posture, supply-chain security, API security, and continuous monitoring.
