# 8 Ways to Build AI Governance

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/8-ways-to-build-ai-governance.png)

## Summary
A wheel-diagram covering the 8 domains of a complete AI governance program: Audit & Accountability, Data Governance, Model Governance, Responsible AI, Risk Management, Security & Privacy, Compliance & Regulation, Monitoring & Observability. Each domain has 5 concrete practices. Grounded in six guiding principles: Trust & Transparency, Human-Centric design, Fairness & Inclusion, Privacy by Design, Continuous Improvement, Accountability.

## Key points
- Audit & Accountability = audit logs, decision traceability, immutable records, governance reporting, internal/external audits
- Data Governance = ownership, quality, metadata mgmt, catalog, lineage
- Model Governance = inventory/registry, versioning, approval/promotion, retraining/decommissioning, model cards
- Compliance ties to EU AI Act, DPDP, GDPR, HIPAA, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001
- Monitoring includes hallucination detection specific to GenAI + drift detection
