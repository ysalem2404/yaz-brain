# NVIDIA AI Factory — From Data to Intelligence at Enterprise Scale

## Summary

An NVIDIA AI Factory infographic maps a full-stack enterprise AI platform from governed enterprise data through model customization, inference at scale, accelerated infrastructure, and the enterprise AI software layer. Cross-cutting capabilities include security, governance, observability, automation, and FinOps, with outcomes framed as faster time to value, higher productivity, lower operating cost, compliance, enterprise scale, and new revenue.

## Key Points

- Five layers: enterprise data platform; models and customization; inference at scale; accelerated infrastructure; and enterprise AI software.
- NVIDIA NeMo is positioned for customization and fine-tuning, while NVIDIA AI Foundation Models provide leading models built on enterprise data.
- TensorRT and NVIDIA NIM support optimized, secure, scalable inference and microservices.
- CUDA and NVIDIA GPUs provide the accelerated-computing foundation; NVIDIA AI Enterprise, Omniverse, DGX Cloud, and BlueField form the software layer.
- Security, governance, observability, automation, and FinOps are shown as cross-cutting controls and operating capabilities.
