# 10 Phases of Building Production-Grade AI Systems

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/10-phases-of-building-production-grade-ai-systems.png)

## Summary
A production-AI lifecycle runs from problem definition and data collection through system architecture, evaluation, guardrails and safety, deployment and scaling, and continuous monitoring.

## Key points
- Begin with a clear use case, success metrics, business alignment, and relevant data collection.
- Define architecture, evaluate quality, establish guardrails, deploy and scale, then monitor performance and create feedback loops.

## Why I saved this
A lifecycle checklist that keeps an AI initiative grounded in reliability, safety, and operational feedback loops.
