# From Digital Twin to Agentic Twin Network

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/from-digital-twin-to-agentic-twin-network.png)

## Summary
A transformation map moves from digital twin networks that observe, simulate, and predict toward agentic twin networks that sense, reason, plan, coordinate, act, and learn across enterprise systems.

## Key points
- Digital twin networks represent physical or business environments and use observation plus simulation to support prediction
- Agentic twin networks add reasoning, planning, negotiation, coordination, execution, and learning
- The proposed architecture connects domain twins, agents, data fabric, collaboration, and executors across enterprise functions
- The shift is presented as moving from reactive to proactive operations and from human-driven decisions toward agent-assisted action

## Why I saved this
A forward-looking systems concept for connecting digital-twin telemetry with orchestration and agentic decision loops.
