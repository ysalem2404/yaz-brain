# Enterprise AI Transformation Framework

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/enterprise-ai-transformation-framework.png)

## Summary
A business-led, capability-driven framework moves AI from experimentation to enterprise impact through alignment, readiness assessment, opportunity portfolios, foundational capabilities, progressive adoption, and value measurement.

## Key points
- The sequence starts with vision, executive sponsorship, success metrics, readiness, maturity, and feasibility
- An opportunity portfolio balances value, strategic alignment, feasibility, risk, investment, and time to value
- Foundational capabilities include an AI platform, data and knowledge layers, enterprise RAG, integration, LLMOps, governance, security, and observability
- Adoption progresses from copilots and workflow intelligence to domain assistants, agentic AI, and autonomous AI

## Why I saved this
A practical operating model for turning scattered pilots into a governed, measurable AI capability.
