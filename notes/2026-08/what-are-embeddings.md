# What Are Embeddings

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/what-are-embeddings.png)

## Summary

A beginner-friendly guide defines embeddings as numerical vector representations that preserve semantic meaning and let machines compare concepts mathematically.

## Key points

- An embedding converts meaning into numbers rather than leaving it as raw text
- Each number is a learned abstract feature, not a human-readable label
- Words such as king and queen can have nearby vectors while an unrelated context such as apple is farther away
- The same idea can be illustrated with tokenized sentences and their vector representations

## Why I saved this

A simple entry point for explaining embeddings before moving to vector databases or retrieval pipelines.
