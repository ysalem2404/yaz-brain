# Building AI Agents Taught Me

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/building-ai-agents-taught-me.png)

## Summary
A lessons-learned graphic argues that reliable AI agents require system engineering around the model, including retrieval, tools, evaluation, observability, and human approval.

## Key points
- Failure modes include wrong retrieval, tool failures, hallucinations, missing context, infinite loops, and unreliable outputs
- The system should connect user goal and intent to planning, retrieval, tools, evaluation, human approval when needed, and final action or response
- The key takeaway is that the model is only one part; system design makes it useful
- Observability and human-in-the-loop review are presented as critical reliability practices

## Why I saved this
A concise reminder to evaluate agents as end-to-end systems rather than as prompt wrappers.
