# BIM Workflow — From Concept to Operations

## Summary

An Excellog.biz infographic explains Building Information Modeling as an intelligent 3D-model workflow that connects concept, design, coordination, construction, handover, and operation or maintenance. Integration and collaboration sit across the lifecycle, with BIM authoring, cloud collaboration, clash detection, project management, mobile and AR/VR, laser scanning, and drones supporting better planning, fewer errors, lower cost, and improved asset management.

## Key Points

- The lifecycle runs from concept and feasibility through design, model coordination, construction, handover, and operation or maintenance.
- Integration and collaboration connect disciplines and help detect clashes, resolve conflicts, and improve coordination.
- At handover, as-built data becomes a digital record for the owner; operations can use BIM models as a digital twin for renewals and asset tracking.
- Tools include BIM authoring and coordination, cloud collaboration, clash detection, project management, mobile and AR/VR, laser scanning, and drones.
- Benefits include improved visualization and collaboration, reduced rework, cost and time savings, efficiency, and better facility management.
