# Legacy MAS Platform — The Challenges

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/legacy-mas-platform-the-challenges.png)

## Summary
A pain-point inventory for a legacy MAS platform, cataloguing ten failure modes across architecture, operations, governance, and people — from daisy-chained pipelines and hard-coded configs to roughly half of team capacity consumed by business-as-usual support.

## Key points
- Architecture: disjointed daisy-chain design with multiple points of failure, hard-coded configs, and scaling limitations.
- Operational load: BAU overhead consumes roughly 50% of the team's capacity, plus server maintenance, backups, and patching.
- Governance: little governance or data quality applied, and some tooling was running outside of vendor support.
- People risk: difficult to onboard and train new engineers, very little documentation, and a critical dependency on the original contractors.
- Sprawl: code and ML models living on individual people's laptops rather than in a managed platform.

## Why I saved this
A ready-made checklist for framing the cost of technical debt — the 50% BAU capacity figure and the contractor-dependency risk are the two most persuasive lines for a modernization business case.
