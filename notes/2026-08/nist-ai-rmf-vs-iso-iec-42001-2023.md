# NIST AI RMF 1.0 vs ISO/IEC 42001:2023

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/nist-ai-rmf-vs-iso-iec-42001-2023.png)

## Summary
A comparison contrasts NIST AI RMF 1.0, a voluntary and flexible framework for managing AI risks across systems, use cases, and lifecycle activities, with ISO/IEC 42001:2023, an auditable organization-wide AI management system standard.

## Key points
- NIST AI RMF is structured around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage and is intended to operationalize AI risk practices.
- ISO/IEC 42001 defines a formal AI Management System with leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement.
- NIST emphasizes practical guidance and flexibility; ISO/IEC 42001 emphasizes repeatability, auditable requirements, and possible certification.
- The two can work together: use NIST practices for risk work and ISO/IEC 42001 for a management-system backbone.

A useful standards map for choosing between risk-management guidance and a certifiable AI governance system.
