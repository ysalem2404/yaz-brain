# Govern the Attributes Behind Policies

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/govern-attributes-behind-policies.png)

## Summary
An access-governance slide argues that tags and identity attributes only become reliable policy inputs when their taxonomy, assignment, ownership, and review are deliberately governed.

## Key points
- A controlled taxonomy defines what tags and attributes mean and how they should be used.
- Trusted assignment connects governance administration, user administration, data producers, and policy implementation.
- Governed tags and identity attributes become inputs to policies over securables with permission models.
- Regular review keeps classifications, user attributes, and policy conventions current.

## Why I saved this
A practical reminder that policy quality depends on the governance of the metadata behind the policy.
