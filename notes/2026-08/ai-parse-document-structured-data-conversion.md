# AI Parse Document — Structured Data Conversion

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-parse-document-structured-data-conversion.png)

## Summary
A Databricks conference slide presents ai_parse_document as a way to turn documents into structured data, exposing layout structure, text content, figure understanding, and confidence scores.

## Key points
- Supported inputs include PDF documents, image files such as PNG and TIFF, and Office documents including DOCX, PPT, and PPTX.
- The output schema identifies element-level bounding boxes and layout structures such as headings, footers, and other document elements.
- Text content returns a full text representation, while figure understanding summarizes figures and extracts their text.
- Per-element confidence scores support more precise review and auditability.
