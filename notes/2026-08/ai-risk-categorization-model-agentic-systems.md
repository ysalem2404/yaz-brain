# AI Risk Categorization Model — Agentic Systems

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-risk-categorization-model-agentic-systems.png)

## Summary
An AI risk categorization model groups systems into low, medium, high, and agentic risk tiers based on autonomy, access, impact, and the consequences of failure.

## Key points
- Agentic systems can combine tool misuse, identity abuse, prompt injection, memory poisoning, and cascading failures
- High-risk systems include decisions or actions with financial, legal, medical, employment, or fundamental-rights consequences
- Risk assessment should consider authority, sensitive data, automation level, and whether humans review actions

## Why I saved this
A useful starting taxonomy for triaging AI systems before choosing controls and review depth.
