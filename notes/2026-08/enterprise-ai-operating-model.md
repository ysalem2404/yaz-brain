# Enterprise AI Operating Model

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/enterprise-ai-operating-model.png)

## Summary
A football-pitch metaphor maps the roles that turn AI talent into enterprise capability: strategy and decision rights, workflow adoption and value, reliable production operations, defensible governance, reusable architecture, and trusted data.

## Key points
- The Coach/CAIO owns AI values, strategy, portfolio, and decision rights.
- The Playmaker/AI Product Owner connects evidence for opportunities to workflow adoption and realized value.
- The Builder/ML Engineer and Anchor/MLOps Lead make products dependable through reliable operations, drift monitoring, and rollback.
- The Defence/AI Governance role supports defensible decisions and compliance, while the Libero/AI Architect builds reusable patterns and guardrails.
- The Goalkeeper/Data Engineer protects trusted, reusable inputs for the rest of the operating model.

## Why I saved this
A memorable role map for discussing the cross-functional capabilities required to move AI from experimentation into enterprise operations.
