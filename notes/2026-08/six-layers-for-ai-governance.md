# Six Layers for AI Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/six-layers-for-ai-governance.png)

## Summary
A six-layer governance model covers AI inventory, data governance, model governance, security and privacy, execution governance, and incident handling.

## Key points
- Data governance includes source management, lineage, quality validation, and monitoring
- Model governance adds bias screening, fairness, red-teaming, benchmarks, testing, and model cost monitoring
- Execution governance includes approvals, least privilege, role management, escalation paths, incident alignment, enforcement, and reporting

## Why I saved this
A practical checklist for ensuring governance continues through deployment and execution rather than stopping at model review.
