# Why Embeddings Became Important

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/why-embeddings-became-important.png)

## Summary

An explanation of embeddings starts from the gap between exact keyword matching and human understanding of meaning, showing why semantic vector representations matter.

## Key points

- Traditional programs are effective with numbers, exact matches, and structured data but do not inherently understand meaning
- Keyword matching can miss that different words or phrases refer to the same concept
- Embeddings represent language as vectors so systems can compare semantic relationships mathematically
- The motivating use case is retrieving a relevant document even when it does not repeat the exact query words

## Why I saved this

A concise rationale for the move from lexical search toward embedding-based retrieval and RAG.
