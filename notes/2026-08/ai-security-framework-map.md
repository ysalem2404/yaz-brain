# AI Security Framework Map

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-security-framework-map.png)

## Summary
An AI security map organizes governance and compliance, threat modeling and adversary mapping, risk scoring and incident learning, and application and supply-chain security around an AI system security core.

## Key points
- Governance references NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OWASP governance guidance, the EU AI Act, and IEEE EAD 2.0.
- Threat modeling and adversary mapping connect STRIDE, OWASP LLM Top 10, MITRE ATT&CK, MITRE ATLAS, CAPEC, and CSA MAESTRO.
- Risk scoring and incident learning draw on the MIT AI Risk Repository, AI Incident Database, OWASP risk rating, DREAD, and related practices.
- AppSec and supply-chain controls include OWASP ASVS and Top 10, PCI DSS Requirement 6, SLSA, and CycloneDX/SPDX.

## Why I saved this
A crosswalk for choosing complementary frameworks instead of treating AI security as a single checklist.
