# Microsoft Copilot Across the Enterprise

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/microsoft-copilot-across-the-enterprise.png)

## Summary
A Copilot overview describes Microsoft’s family of AI assistants as a layer across Microsoft products, enterprise context, business workflows, security, and development.

## Key points
- A user prompt is combined with permitted files, emails, chats, meetings, and web information as context
- Copilot can create or summarize content, analyze data, search company knowledge, automate repetitive tasks, and help build agents
- Microsoft 365 Copilot brings assistance into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams
- The broader Copilot family extends across developer workflows, security, business applications, and Microsoft Fabric

## Why I saved this
A concise architecture view of how an enterprise assistant turns product context and permissions into work-oriented actions.
