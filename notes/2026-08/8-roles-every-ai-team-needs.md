# 8 Roles Every AI Team Needs

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/8-roles-every-ai-team-needs.png)

## Summary
A role map describes eight AI-team functions and the core skills that connect business use cases, data, models, security, governance, operations, and applications.

## Key points
- Roles include AI Product Manager, Data Engineer, AI Security Engineer, AI Solution Architect, Responsible AI and Governance Lead, Machine Learning Engineer, AI Operations and Adoption Lead, and AI Application Engineer
- Skills span use-case prioritization, data lakes and vector databases, prompt-injection defense, cloud architecture, policy and audit, MLOps, change adoption, and tool-calling or MCP development
- The roles are presented as complementary pieces of an enterprise AI operating model

## Why I saved this
A useful staffing and capability checklist for planning an enterprise AI function.
