# AI Governance Roles and Responsibilities

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/ai-governance-roles-and-responsibilities.png)

## Summary
An accountability map assigns AI governance responsibilities across boards, business owners, risk, data, development, operations, security, legal, privacy, audit, and change management.

## Key points
- Boards and governance committees set direction, risk appetite, policy oversight, and strategic priorities for AI
- Business owners define use cases, value, and outcome accountability while AI risk owners identify and treat risks
- Developers and data scientists build responsibly; data owners protect quality, security, lineage, and lawful use
- MLOps deploys and monitors reliability, performance, availability, changes, and incidents
- Security, legal, privacy, audit, HR, and change-management functions provide controls, assurance, compliance, privacy-by-design, and responsible-use culture

## Why I saved this
A clear reminder that AI governance is an operating model with named owners, not just a policy document.
