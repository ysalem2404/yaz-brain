# Modern Governance Risk and Compliance

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/modern-governance-risk-and-compliance.png)

## Summary
A GRC overview frames modern governance, risk, and compliance as a coordinated operating model linking oversight, risk appetite, obligations, controls, assurance, evidence, ethics, and continuous monitoring.

## Key points
- Regulatory divergence, AI accountability gaps, third-party exposure, weak control evidence, privacy risk, and board-reporting blind spots are major pressures
- The operating flow runs from setting direction and risk appetite through obligation mapping, control design, continuous monitoring, testing, assurance, reporting, and improvement
- Technology layers include AI governance tools, RegTech, control-evidence automation, integrated GRC analytics, third-party risk systems, and workflow orchestration
- Effective GRC produces clearer accountability, stronger compliance confidence, faster risk response, better control evidence, and more reliable board decisions
- The value formula combines risk intelligence, compliance clarity, control discipline, assurance evidence, and trusted governance decisions
