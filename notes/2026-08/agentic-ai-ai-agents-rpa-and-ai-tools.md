# Agentic AI, AI Agents, RPA, and AI Tools

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/agentic-ai-ai-agents-rpa-and-ai-tools.png)

## Summary
A comparison chart distinguishes agentic AI, AI agents, robotic process automation, and AI tools by their input, model or rule logic, memory, decision-making, and output loops.

## Key points
- AI tools generally provide a bounded capability in response to user input
- RPA follows predefined rules and workflows, while agents add model-driven decisions and tool use
- Agentic AI emphasizes broader autonomy, state, planning, and iterative action across a task
