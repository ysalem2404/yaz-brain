# Choosing the Right AI for the Problem

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/choosing-the-right-ai-for-the-problem.png)

## Summary
A decision guide places rules and logic, classical machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, and agentic AI on a capability ladder so teams can choose the simplest approach that solves the business problem.

## Key points
- Rules and logic fit deterministic approvals, calculations, policy checks, and threshold-based workflows
- Classical ML and predictive analytics forecast outcomes such as demand, churn, risk, maintenance, and pricing
- Generative AI creates content, drafts, summaries, code, and images, while deep learning supports perception tasks such as OCR and defect detection
- Agentic AI plans, decides, and takes actions for multi-step workflows, service resolution, research, scheduling, and cross-system triage

## Why I saved this
A practical anti-hype heuristic: identify the task first, then choose the least complex AI capability that meets it.
