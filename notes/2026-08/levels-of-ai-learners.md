# Levels of AI Learners

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/levels-of-ai-learners.png)

## Summary
A progression map moves from AI awareness and everyday productivity through power-user, creator, automation builder, agent builder, AI engineer, architect, and research roles.

## Key points
- Each level pairs a learning focus with tools and an intended outcome
- The progression runs from prompts and file-based research to workflows, tool-using agents, production applications, enterprise architecture, and advanced model research
- Tool examples span ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Zapier, LangChain, LangGraph, Python, Docker, cloud platforms, and ML systems
