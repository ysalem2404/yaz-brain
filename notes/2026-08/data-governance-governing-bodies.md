# Data Governance Governing Bodies

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/data-governance-governing-bodies.png)

## Summary
A governance operating model organizes enterprise data accountability through an Enterprise Data Strategy Board, Data Governance Office, business data steering committees, a stewardship council, and a community of practice.

## Key points
- The Enterprise Data Strategy Board provides enterprise strategy and oversight.
- The Data Governance Office translates governance into enterprise-wide implementation.
- Business Data Steering Committees handle business-level strategy and implementation, while the Data Stewardship Council establishes stewardship best practices.
- A Community of Practice supports training, data steward working groups, and data literacy.
