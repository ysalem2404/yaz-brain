# AI in Construction: Force Multiplier, Not Substitute

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/ai-in-construction-force-multiplier-not-substitute.png)

## Summary
A construction-focused infographic argues that AI is already entering field workflows as a force multiplier for technical teams, while experience and accountability remain essential for interpreting results.

## Key points
- Potential applications include design reviews, document control, submittal and RFI management, BIM coordination, clash detection, safety observations, quantity takeoffs, progress tracking, inspections, estimating, scheduling, and commissioning documentation
- AI may flag mismatches or patterns, but people still need to determine why a drawing, field condition, or installation is wrong and what the operational consequences are
- Construction knowledge often lives with superintendents, trades, QA/QC, and commissioning teams rather than in neat databases
- The adoption question is how fast the industry can understand and operationalize AI, not whether AI will appear on construction sites

## Why I saved this
A grounded reminder that domain expertise, accountability, and contextual judgment are part of the AI system in field-heavy industries.
