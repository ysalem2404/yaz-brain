# AI × BIM: The Next 10 Years

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-bim-the-next-10-years.png)

## Summary
An AI and BIM perspective distinguishes modelling from engineering: AI can generate geometry and automate model updates, while engineers must decide whether a system is correct, compliant, constructible, maintainable, and fit for purpose.

## Key points
- AI can route a pipe, avoid clashes, connect equipment, and update a model, but a pipe is part of a system that must perform, comply, and last.
- Engineering judgment covers flow requirements, pipe sizing, pressure loss, velocity, slope, equipment connections, access, maintenance, standards, and constructability.
- The future BIM professional combines modelling and engineering: AI generates, automates, analyses, and modifies while the engineer defines, decides, questions, and validates.
- The durable skill is understanding why a system exists, not merely how to draw its geometry.
