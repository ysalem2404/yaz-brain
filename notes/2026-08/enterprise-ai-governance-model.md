# Enterprise AI Governance Model

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/enterprise-ai-governance-model.png)

## Summary
A nine-layer enterprise AI governance model connects data foundations, AI inventory, automation eligibility, model assurance, decision authority, guardrails, audit trails, incident response, and board assurance.

## Key points
- Data foundations require traceable lineage to the source; an AI inventory should list decisions and systems, not only models
- Automation eligibility needs legal and reversibility checks, while model assurance requires independent challenge
- Decision authority names an approver; operating guardrails define stop conditions when AI drifts
- Audit trails make decisions reconstructable and incident controls provide tested rollback and escalation paths
- Board assurance verifies governance independently, while culture and AI literacy sustain the system

## Why I saved this
A strong control-oriented companion to role-based governance: it turns responsible AI into evidence, decision rights, and tested operating controls.
