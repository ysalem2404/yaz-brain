# Frontier vs Open-Source: The Real AI Decision

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/frontier-vs-open-source-the-real-ai-decision.png)

## Summary
A strategy card reframes the frontier-versus-open-source debate as a decision about which intelligence to rent, which to own, and where each model belongs.

## Key points
- Frontier models fit genuinely difficult reasoning, multimodal, coding, and ambiguous work when speed to market matters
- External models can serve important but non-differentiating tasks when security, privacy, contracts, and human review are controlled
- Open-weight or custom models become more attractive when proprietary data creates a moat or when the model is part of the product
- The decision should consider capability, ownership, workload importance, differentiation, and operating control

## Why I saved this
A concise decision lens for choosing model deployment strategy without reducing the question to raw benchmark power or price.
