# The 4 Layers of AI Governance

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/the-4-layers-of-ai-governance.png)

## Summary
A governance model organizes trusted AI around four layers: governance, data strategy, retrieval and context, and model and output controls, followed by continuous monitoring, learning, and improvement.

## Key points
- Governance sets policy, roles, accountability, risk appetite, ethics, compliance, fairness, and audit responsibilities.
- Data strategy emphasizes quality, security, privacy, provenance, lineage, access controls, ownership, and metadata.
- Retrieval and context grounds outputs in trusted internal facts through semantic search, RAG, permission-aware retrieval, and knowledge graphs.
- Model and output controls add fine-tuning, guardrails, output validation, PII detection, content filters, and monitoring.
- Continuous auditing, performance tracking, feedback, retraining, updates, and adaptive governance keep controls current.
