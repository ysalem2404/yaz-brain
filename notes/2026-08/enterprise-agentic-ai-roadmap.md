# The Enterprise Agentic AI Roadmap

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/enterprise-agentic-ai-roadmap.png)

## Summary
A roadmap moves from model access to reliable business execution through foundation, workflow portfolio, controlled execution, production operations, and coordinated business-system stages.

## Key points
- Establish the foundation with reasoning and memory, planning and tools, orchestration and environments, permissions and guardrails, and context.
- Prioritize a workflow portfolio, then engineer controlled execution with multi-agent coordination, human approvals, and business-system integration.
- Operate in production with observability and tracing, access control and auditability, budget controls, failure handling, and evaluation.
- The destination is coordinated operations where agents reliably execute business work rather than only answering questions.

## Why I saved this
A staged maturity model for sequencing agentic AI investments around control and business execution.
