# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AIMS — Study Mind Map

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/iso-iec-42001-2023-aims-study-mind-map.png)

## Summary
A study mind map summarizes ISO/IEC 42001 AI Management System requirements alongside AI concepts, related standards, leadership, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement.

## Key points
- ISO/IEC 42001 provides an AI management-system structure for responsible and trustworthy AI
- Related standards cover concepts, risk management, ML frameworks, AI governance, trustworthiness, impact assessment, and certification
- The operating model spans leadership, policy, resources, competence, documented information, evaluation, and continual improvement
