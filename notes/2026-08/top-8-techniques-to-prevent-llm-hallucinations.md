# Top 8 Techniques to Prevent LLM Hallucinations

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/top-8-techniques-to-prevent-llm-hallucinations.png)

## Summary
A reliability checklist recommends retrieval, grounded prompts, structured output, tool calling, advanced RAG, trusted-data fine-tuning, and validation or human review to reduce hallucinations.

## Key points
- Retrieval and reranking provide relevant evidence for grounded answers
- Clear instructions, few-shot examples, constrained answers, and structured schemas reduce ambiguity
- Tool calling and advanced RAG connect generation to search, databases, calculators, and corrective loops
- Trusted domain data, confidence checks, validators, and human approval add final safeguards
