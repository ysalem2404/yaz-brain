# Microsoft Copilot Ecosystem — Product Wheel

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/microsoft-copilot-ecosystem-product-wheel.png)

## Summary
A Microsoft Copilot ecosystem map organizes Copilot capabilities across Power Platform, security, business applications, modern work, developer tools, business intelligence, browser and search, operating systems, Azure, and AI agents.

## Key points
- The ecosystem spans apps, developer experiences, data and analytics, cloud infrastructure, and agent tooling
- Azure AI Foundry, model catalog, fine-tuning, model distillation, and content safety sit beneath the product surface
- Entra ID, responsible AI dashboards, and governance capabilities connect identity and oversight to the ecosystem

## Why I saved this
A high-level map for understanding how Microsoft positions Copilot as a connected platform rather than a single assistant.
