# Geospatial AI: The Next Intelligence Layer

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/geospatial-ai-next-intelligence-layer.png)

## Summary
A visual post frames geospatial AI as an intelligence layer that helps organizations understand location, physical relationships, and real-world events, extending enterprise AI beyond text and documents.

## Key points
- Geospatial AI combines AI with location and spatial relationships to reason about the physical world.
- Potential applications span infrastructure, climate and environment, logistics, urban planning, asset management, and risk intelligence.
- The core opportunity is to connect enterprise decisions to maps, imagery, sensors, and changing real-world conditions.
