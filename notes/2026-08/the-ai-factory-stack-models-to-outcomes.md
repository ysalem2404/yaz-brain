# The AI Factory Stack — Models to Outcomes

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/the-ai-factory-stack-models-to-outcomes.png)

## Summary
The AI Factory Stack uses a factory metaphor: the LLM is the machine, RAG supplies raw material, vector databases store knowledge, agents manage work, MCP connects tools, guardrails define safe action, and evals check quality.

## Key points
- Models generate and reason, but retrieval supplies relevant information before generation
- Agents decide what to do while MCP connects them to tools, applications, files, and databases
- Guardrails and evals protect users and measure correctness, safety, cost, latency, and success

A memorable one-page explanation that production AI is a system of connected components, not just a powerful model.
