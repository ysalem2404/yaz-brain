# AI in Oil and Gas Operating Model

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/ai-in-oil-and-gas-operating-model.png)

## Summary
An operating-model infographic argues that AI creates value in oil and gas when integrated with trusted data, core systems, workflows, decisions, and measurable business outcomes.

## Key points
- AI implementation changes technology, but transformation changes how the enterprise operates
- Value flows from high-value use cases through a trusted, unified, accessible technology ecosystem into embedded workflows and decisions
- The target outcomes are better decisions, better performance, and measurable value
- Common blockers are fragmented data, disconnected systems, unclear process ownership, weak adoption and change, and missing outcome measurement
- The central message is that AI amplifies existing systems when it is fully integrated rather than added as a disconnected tool

## Why I saved this
A domain-specific operating-model lens that makes the gap between an AI pilot and enterprise value concrete.
