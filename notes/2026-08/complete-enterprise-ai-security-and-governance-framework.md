# Complete Enterprise AI Security and Governance Framework

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/complete-enterprise-ai-security-and-governance-framework.png)

## Summary
A layered enterprise framework places strategy, architecture, data and knowledge governance, AI security, model risk, resilience, compliance, operations, and performance measurement around the safe delivery of AI systems.

## Key points
- Security is presented as a control layer rather than the foundation by itself
- The framework includes identity and access, prompt and model security, data protection, agent security, and integrations
- Governance covers model risk, human oversight, decision accountability, privacy, audit, and regulatory compliance
- Resilience and continuous measurement connect business continuity, vendor risk, observability, cost, and ROI

## Why I saved this
A strong checklist for discussing enterprise AI risk beyond the model alone, including execution and operations.
