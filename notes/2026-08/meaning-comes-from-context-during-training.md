# Meaning Comes from Context During Training

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/meaning-comes-from-context-during-training.png)

## Summary

A contextual-learning infographic explains that embedding models learn semantic relationships from repeated patterns of words appearing in similar contexts during training.

## Key points

- Sentences about a king and a queen ruling a kingdom provide overlapping context
- Repeated contextual patterns make king and queen vectors close in the learned space
- Apple appears in a different fruit-related context, so its vector is separated from the royalty examples
- Spatial closeness in the embedding space represents semantic similarity

## Why I saved this

A memorable explanation of the distributional idea that words gain useful representations from the contexts in which they appear.
