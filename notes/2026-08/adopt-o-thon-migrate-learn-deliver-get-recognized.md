# Adopt-o-thon — Migrate, Learn, Deliver, Get Recognized

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/adopt-o-thon-migrate-learn-deliver-get-recognized.png)

## Summary
An Adopt-o-thon slide turns platform adoption into a gamified journey: learn and certify, migrate and deliver, climb the leaderboard, and get recognized.

## Key points
- Learn and certify by completing onboarding, training, and Databricks certification to earn individual points.
- Migrate and deliver by moving reports, building pipelines, and delivering solutions to earn team points.
- Climb the leaderboard as teams are ranked each cycle.
- Recognize sprint champions, migration bonuses, and exceptional contributions.
