# Agentic Data Platform — Genie, Agent Bricks, and Lakehouse

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/agentic-data-platform-genie-agent-bricks-lakehouse.png)

## Summary
A photographed platform-stack slide layers agentic development on Genie, Agent Bricks, and Genie Ontology; unified governance on Unity Catalog and Unity AI Gateway; and agentic data on Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Lakebase over open-format infrastructure.

## Key points
- The top layer highlights automated building of data and ML pipelines through Genie and shows Genie, app builder, agents, code, zero-ops, and Agent Bricks.
- Genie Ontology is positioned between agentic development and the governed platform layer as enterprise context.
- Unity Catalog and Unity AI Gateway form the unified-governance layer.
- Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Lakebase provide the agentic-data layer above open-format data-lake infrastructure.
