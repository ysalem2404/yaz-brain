# AI and Revit MCP

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/ai-and-revit-mcp.png)

## Summary
An infographic maps how AI and Model Context Protocol (MCP) can automate repetitive BIM and Revit work while leaving complex engineering judgment to people.

## Key points
- Potential automation targets include model reading, element search, bulk parameter editing, quantity takeoffs, QA/QC, tagging, reporting, and Revit API work
- Listed time savings range from roughly 50% to 98% depending on task
- Junior BIM modelers, technicians, and CAD/Revit draftspeople are shown as the roles most affected
- Complex family creation, advanced MEP routing, structural optimization, and fully autonomous delivery remain human-led or difficult

## Why I saved this
A practical view of MCP-enabled automation in architecture, engineering, and construction workflows.
