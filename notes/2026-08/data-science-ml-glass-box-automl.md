# Data Science and ML — Glass Box AutoML

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/data-science-ml-glass-box-automl.png)

## Summary
A data-science-and-ML maturity slide presents a “glass box” approach to AutoML: use generated baselines and notebooks, iterate with MLflow, and expose results through dashboards or Genie.

## Key points
- The workflow starts with generated baseline notebooks and models, then iterates through experiment tracking and MLflow-style tooling.
- The glass-box framing keeps the model-development path inspectable rather than hiding every choice behind automation.
- AI/BI dashboards and Genie spaces are shown as ways to expose model results and keep pace with demand.
- The pattern balances accelerated starting points with human review and production visibility.

## Why I saved this
A practical counterpoint to black-box automation: AutoML is most useful when it accelerates repeatable work without removing inspection.
