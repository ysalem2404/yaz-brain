## Summary
A Field slide sketches an agentic security pipeline that brings together many threat-data sources, then applies ingestion, normalization, health monitoring, and error correction.

## Key points
- Sources include email, network and firewall, identity and access, SaaS apps, cloud and IaaS, DNS, EDR/XDR, and unstructured data such as LLM logs, Jira PDFs, Slack, and transcripts.
- The ingest layer highlights agentic data ingestion, data normalization, health monitoring, and error correction.
- The architecture treats data quality and operational health as first-class parts of agentic threat response.
