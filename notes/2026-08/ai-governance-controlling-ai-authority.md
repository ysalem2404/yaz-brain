# AI Governance: Controlling AI Authority

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-governance-controlling-ai-authority.png)

## Summary
A governance framework reframes the objective from merely controlling AI systems to controlling AI authority: who an agent is, what data and tools it can access, what it can recommend or execute, and which actions require human approval. It organizes the control layer around trust and accountability, compliance and risk, operational control, data and decision lineage, and AI authority and identity.

## Key points
- Replace document-only compliance with enforceable authorization boundaries and continuous control.
- Track data, model, prompt, tool, decision, and action lineage so an AI decision can be audited end to end.
- Use operational safeguards such as escalation paths and kill switches for agentic systems.
- Make identity and access explicit: who the agent is, what it can access, what it can recommend, what it can execute, and what requires human approval.
