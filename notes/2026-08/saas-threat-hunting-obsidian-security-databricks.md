# SaaS Threat Hunting with Obsidian Security and Databricks

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/saas-threat-hunting-obsidian-security-databricks.png)

## Summary
A summary slide for SaaS threat hunting, making the case that supply chain attacks are invisible from inside a single tenant and that cross-tenant intelligence plus normalized SaaS telemetry queried in Databricks enables consistent detection across applications.

## Key points
- Supply chain attacks are often invisible from within a single organization's environment — single-tenant visibility is structurally insufficient.
- Cross-tenant intelligence enables detection of attack patterns that span multiple organizations.
- Normalized SaaS telemetry queried through Databricks enables consistent detection logic across many different applications.
- The pattern pairs a specialist SaaS security vendor for collection and normalization with a lakehouse for query and detection.

## Why I saved this
The cross-tenant visibility argument is the part worth keeping: it explains why some classes of SaaS compromise cannot be found with internal logs alone, regardless of tooling quality.
