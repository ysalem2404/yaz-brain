# A Guide to Word Embeddings

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/a-guide-to-word-embeddings.png)

## Summary

A guide illustrates how words are represented as numeric vectors so models can compare semantic meaning rather than only exact tokens.

## Key points

- Words are converted into vectors with multiple numeric dimensions
- Words that appear in similar contexts tend to occupy nearby positions in vector space
- Cosine similarity measures the angle between vectors and provides a semantic-relatedness signal
- The dimensions are learned abstract features rather than human-readable labels

## Why I saved this

A visual foundation for understanding semantic search, retrieval, and the embedding layer behind many AI systems.
