# AI Reshaping the AEC Industry

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/ai-reshaping-the-aec-industry.png)

## Summary
A commentary on McKinsey research says AI may reshape whole AEC workflows, with connected project data enabling automation across estimating, scheduling, RFIs, financials, and project operations.

## Key points
- The referenced report examined 150 workflows across 25 AEC domains
- The graphic estimates AI could automate up to 39% of nonphysical work in construction
- The claim is about removing repetitive work from project teams, not eliminating 39% of jobs
- Connected, current project data is presented as the prerequisite for useful AI context

## Why I saved this
A relevant bridge between AI adoption and the data-foundation work required in architecture, engineering, and construction.
