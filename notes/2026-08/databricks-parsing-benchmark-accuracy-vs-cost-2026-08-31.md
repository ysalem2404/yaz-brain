# Databricks Parsing Benchmark — Accuracy vs Cost

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/databricks-parsing-benchmark-accuracy-vs-cost-2026-08-31.png)

## Summary
A Databricks parsing benchmark plots document-parsing systems on accuracy and per-thousand-page pricing, making the quality-cost trade-off visible for agent workflows.

## Key points
- The chart compares AI Parse Document with frontier and specialized parsing options such as Gemini, Mistral OCR, Claude, GPT, and other services.
- The benchmark emphasizes that parsing quality cannot be evaluated independently of price when the workload processes many pages.
- The highlighted trade-off supports choosing a fit-for-purpose parser before routing every document through a general-purpose frontier model.

## Why I saved this
A visual decision aid for evaluating document parsers on both fidelity and operating economics.
