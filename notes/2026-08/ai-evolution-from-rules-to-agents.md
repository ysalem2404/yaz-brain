# AI Evolution — From Rules to Agents

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-evolution-from-rules-to-agents.png)

## Summary
An executive-oriented timeline moves from rules-based AI and machine learning through deep learning, computer vision, generative AI, and action-oriented agents.

## Key points
- Rules-based systems are framed around explicit IF/THEN logic, while ML learns patterns from data
- Generative AI creates content and agents act across workflows rather than only answering
- World models are positioned as a research-stage frontier for robotics and physical simulation

## Why I saved this
A simple vocabulary ladder for explaining the progression of AI capabilities to non-specialist leaders.
