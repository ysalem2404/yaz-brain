# AI Governance Operating Flow

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/ai-governance-operating-flow.png)

## Summary
A compact AI governance flow starts with business strategy and use-case intake, classifies risk, and moves through data, model, and security governance into development, evaluation, human approval, production, monitoring, compliance, auditability, and continuous review.

## Key points
- Use cases are first tied to business strategy and intake before risk classification.
- Data governance covers privacy/PII, data quality, data lineage, and access control; model governance covers model risk, bias, accuracy, and explainability; security governance covers IAM/zero trust, encryption, threat protection, and secrets.
- AI development, evaluation and validation, and human oversight or approval precede production.
- Monitoring, compliance, auditability, and continuous review form the feedback loop for improvement and retirement.
