# The Enterprise AI Architecture — Capture

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/enterprise-ai-architecture-capture.png)

## Summary
An enterprise AI architecture capture shows users and triggers entering through identity, session, gateway, and prompt/context management before reaching orchestration, agents, retrieval, models, tools, and enterprise systems, with governance and observability spanning the stack.

## Key points
- Business users, customers, employees, web apps, chat or copilots, API events, and workflow triggers feed the experience and access layer.
- Identity and access, API gateway, session context, and prompt/context management sit before agent orchestration, routing, planning, supervisors, and execution.
- Knowledge and retrieval connect enterprise search, RAG, vector databases, knowledge graphs, document stores, SQL, and warehouses to model routing and tools.
- Security, governance, observability, evaluation, compliance, feedback, human approval, tracing, logging, cost, and quality are cross-cutting controls.

## Why I saved this
A visual reference for explaining enterprise AI as a layered, governed system rather than a single model endpoint.
