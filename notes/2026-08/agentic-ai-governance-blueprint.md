# Agentic AI Governance Blueprint

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/agentic-ai-governance-blueprint.png)

## Summary
A governance blueprint lays out eight control layers for agents: identity and permissions, action boundaries, human oversight, observability, multi-agent governance, evaluation and drift, compliance, and runtime controls.

## Key points
- Identity and permissions define the agent, delegated authority, scoped credentials, and tool allowlists.
- Action boundaries constrain read/write behavior with spend limits, irreversibility gates, sandboxing, and separation of duties.
- Human oversight adds approval tiers and escalation triggers; observability adds decision logging, reasoning audit, replay, and traceability.
- Multi-agent governance addresses agent trust and orchestration, while evaluation and drift measure capability and changing behavior.
- Compliance and runtime controls close the loop with standards, policy enforcement, kill switches, scoped credentials, and tool allowlists.

A concise control-plane reference for designing agents that can act without becoming unbounded or unauditable.
