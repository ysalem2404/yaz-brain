# Cosine Similarity Measures Meaning

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/cosine-similarity-measures-meaning.png)

## Summary

A worked example introduces cosine similarity as the angle between two vectors and uses king, queen, and apple to show how semantic relatedness can be quantified.

## Key points

- The similarity compares the direction of vector A with vector B rather than only their raw magnitudes
- Values near 1 indicate similar direction and meaning, 0 indicates unrelated vectors, and -1 indicates opposite directions
- King and queen are represented with close vectors while apple is intentionally far away
- The calculation begins with a dot product and normalizes by the vector lengths
