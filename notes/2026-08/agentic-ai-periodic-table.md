# Agentic AI Periodic Table

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/agentic-ai-periodic-table.png)

## Summary
A periodic-table-style reference organizes agentic AI terminology across capabilities, patterns, tools, evaluation, memory, collaboration, deployment, and business use cases.

## Key points
- Core concepts include LLMs, planning, reinforcement learning, RAG, context, short- and long-term memory, and human oversight
- Agent patterns include hierarchical supervisors, tool use, local and cloud agents, multi-agent systems, and feedback loops
- Protocols and infrastructure concepts include MCP, A2A, APIs, memory stores, history logs, and tool catalogs
- Business examples span education, HR, data analysis, marketing, shopping, customer support, and legal assistance
- The graphic mixes established and tailored abbreviations, so it is best used as an orientation map rather than a formal standard

## Why I saved this
A handy vocabulary index for navigating the rapidly expanding agentic-AI design space without treating every acronym as a separate architecture.
