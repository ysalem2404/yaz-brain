# The Hottest AI Roles in 2026

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/the-hottest-ai-roles-in-2026.png)

## Summary
A role map highlights a widening AI job family spanning architecture, data, model development, prompting, operations, knowledge, ethics, product, integration, agents, and cost optimization.

## Key points
- Roles include AI Solutions Architect, AI Trainer or Annotator, AI/ML Engineer, Prompt Engineer, MLOps Engineer, AI Knowledge Engineer, AI Ethics Specialist, AI Research Scientist, AI Product Manager, and AI Data Engineer
- Additional specialties cover LLM engineering, AI integration, computer vision, agent engineering, security, NLP, robotics, reinforcement learning, speech, and inference-cost optimization
- The map frames an AI capability integrator as someone who connects disciplines to business outcomes
- The roles collectively show that AI delivery needs product, data, systems, operations, security, governance, and domain translation

## Why I saved this
A useful capability inventory for thinking beyond the narrow title of machine-learning engineer when staffing AI programs.
