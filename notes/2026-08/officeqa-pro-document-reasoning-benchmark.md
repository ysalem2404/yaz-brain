# OfficeQA Pro — Document Reasoning Benchmark

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/officeqa-pro-document-reasoning-benchmark.png)

## Summary
OfficeQA Pro is presented as a benchmark for document-grounded reasoning: roughly 90,000 pages from nearly a century of U.S. Treasury material and 133 questions with exact, verifiable answers.

## Key points
- The corpus is made of old scanned PDFs, dense tables, changing units, and visual figures.
- The benchmark is designed to test parsing, retrieval, table understanding, and analytical reasoning in a realistic document-heavy workflow.
- The enterprise workflow proxy frames document understanding as a prerequisite for agents that must answer from long, heterogeneous records.
