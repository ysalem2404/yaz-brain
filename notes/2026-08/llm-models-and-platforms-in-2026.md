# LLM Models and Platforms in 2026

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/llm-models-and-platforms-in-2026.png)

## Summary
A landscape map groups frontier, enterprise, multimodal, open and open-weight, model-hosting, and open-model serving platforms shaping the AI ecosystem in 2026.

## Key points
- Frontier platforms include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, and Microsoft AI offerings
- Enterprise platforms include Azure AI Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, IBM watsonx.ai, and NVIDIA NIM
- Open and open-weight ecosystems include Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, OLMo, Hugging Face, Fireworks, Together AI, Replicate, and OpenRouter
- Local and open-model serving options include Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, LocalAI, and Hugging Face TGI
