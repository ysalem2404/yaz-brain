# GTM Companion — Synthesis for the User

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/gtm-companion-synthesis-for-the-user.png)

## Summary
A GTM Companion concept replaces the human-in-the-middle work of gathering signals with one interface that synthesizes account, customer, product, and activity data for the user.

## Key points
- The interface is positioned as a single place where synthesis happens for the user rather than manually across separate sources.
- Signals include account plans, customer-success notes, go-to-market activity, emails, events, activities, firmographics, technographics, entitlements, whitespace, and product usage.
- The slide cites a 1.5B+ data-point foundation and frames the companion as an intelligence layer for revenue workflows.

## Why I saved this
A useful reference for understanding AI copilots as signal-synthesis products, not just chat interfaces.
