# What Makes OfficeQA Difficult?

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-08/what-makes-officeqa-difficult.png)

## Summary
An OfficeQA slide frames document-grounded question answering as a compound challenge spanning faithful parsing, retrieval, and analysis.

## Key points
- Parsing must faithfully represent tables, text, figures, and other information from a document.
- Retrieval must locate the correct evidence from a corpus where pages can look almost identical but differ by year.
- Analysis still requires reasoning over the retrieved information to identify the correct answers.

## Why I saved this
A concise decomposition of why document QA is more than text extraction.

What Makes OfficeQA Difficult? Strong execution required in multiple areas. Parsing: Faithfully representing the information (tables, text, figures, etc.) from a given document to the agent. Retrieval: Retrieving the correct information for the agent from the corpus. Pages from documents can look almost identical but differ by year. Analysis: Given this information, the agent must reason over it to identify the correct answers.
