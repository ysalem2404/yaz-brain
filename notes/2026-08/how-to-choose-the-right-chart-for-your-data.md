# How to Choose the Right Chart for Your Data

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-08/how-to-choose-the-right-chart-for-your-data.png)

## Summary
A chart-selection guide maps analytical questions to visual forms, from bar charts for category comparisons to scatter plots for relationships, maps for geography, and Sankey diagrams for flows.

## Key points
- Start with the question: compare categories, show change over time, explain part-to-whole, reveal distribution, or trace flow.
- Use scatter or bubble plots for relationships, maps for geography, waterfall charts for contributions to a total, and Sankey diagrams for movement between stages.

## Why I saved this
A practical reference for choosing a visual that answers the question instead of decorating the data.
