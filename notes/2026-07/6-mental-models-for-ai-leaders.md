# 6 Mental Models for AI Leaders from BCG, Gartner, Bain, McKinsey and NIST

## Summary

A comparative infographic summarizing six strategic frameworks used by top consultancies and NIST to reason about enterprise AI: BCG's Deploy-Reshape-Invent, Gartner's AI Opportunity Radar, Bain's AI-era Operating Model, BCG's 10-20-70 rule (10% algorithms, 20% tech/data, 70% people/process), McKinsey's Six Dimensions of AI Value Capture, and NIST's Govern-Map-Measure-Manage risk lifecycle. Together they cover ambition-setting, operating model redesign, and systematic risk management.

## Key Points

- BCG Deploy-Reshape-Invent: automate tasks, redesign workflows, then create new business models
- Gartner AI Opportunity Radar maps use cases by customer-facing vs. internal and everyday vs. game-changing AI
- Bain's AI-era Operating Model redesigns structure, talent, leadership, and processes around 'right work'
- BCG's 10-20-70 rule: AI value is 70% people/process, only 10% algorithms
- McKinsey's Six Dimensions: strategy, talent, operating model, technology, data, adoption/scaling
- NIST's Govern-Map-Measure-Manage is a cyclical AI risk management framework
