# Your AI Strategy Doesn't Need a Better Roadmap — It Needs Better Questions First

## Summary

A Carolyn Healey infographic proposing seven questions AI leaders should ask leadership teams before any vendor call or roadmap discussion: which P&L line the initiative moves and who owns that number; what decisions the organization will let AI make without a human; which end-to-end workflow would be redesigned if AI didn't exist as an excuse to avoid it; who has personally spent 8 hours with the tools; what happens to hours saved; which current pilots would be killed today; and whose job changes first. It cites BCG, McKinsey, and Gartner statistics throughout, including that only ~6% of organizations attribute real EBIT impact to AI and that 40%+ of agentic AI projects are projected to be canceled by 2027.

## Key Points

- Seven leadership questions framed as prerequisites to any AI roadmap or vendor engagement
- Cites BCG 2026 data that AI spending is set to double from 0.8% to 1.7% of revenue
- Cites McKinsey 2025 finding that only ~6% of organizations attribute real EBIT impact to AI
- References Gartner's projection that 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027
- Argues that naming an executive P&L owner is the single most correlated factor with AI initiatives avoiding becoming 'zombie pilots'
- Frames model selection and vendor comparison as explicitly NOT the first-hour priority

## Why Yaz Saved This

A sharp leadership-alignment checklist Yaz can use before enterprise AI vendor engagements or pilot scoping — the P&L ownership and 'workflow redesign vs bolt-on AI' framing is directly applicable to his enterprise architecture advisory conversations.
