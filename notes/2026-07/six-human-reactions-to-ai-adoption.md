# SIX Human Reactions to AI Adoption

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-07/six-human-reactions-to-ai-adoption.png)

## Summary
Six behavioral archetypes leaders see when rolling out AI, each with 'what it looks like' + 'what to do': (1) The Over-Offloader — accepts outputs without forming a view; add cognitive friction, ask for hypotheses before prompts; (2) The Boxed-In Operator — quiet disengagement, feels AI done TO them; give teams agency, preserve human judgement; (3) The De-Skilled Expert — wonders if their capability matters; position AI as support, not competence test; (4) The Isolated Collaborator — more AI interaction, less peer debate; build human review/discussion forums; (5) The Shadow User — uses AI privately, hides it; make AI use visible, normal, leader-led; (6) The Identity Defender — sees AI as threat to craft/status; show how AI strengthens identity, not replaces it.

## Key points
- Shadow User archetype directly maps to 'shadow AI' governance risk
- Design solutions are behavioral (add friction, build forums, model use) not technical
- 'Boxed-In Operator' names the disengagement pattern most orgs miss
- Leaders should model AI use openly (fixes Shadow User + Identity Defender simultaneously)
