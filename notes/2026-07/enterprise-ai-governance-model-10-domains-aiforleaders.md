# Enterprise AI Governance Model — 10 Domains (AIForLeaders.com)

## Summary

A second Enterprise AI Governance Model infographic, this one from AIForLeaders.com, organizing governance into ten domains: data governance, model validation, bias and fairness, human oversight, regulatory compliance, security controls, vendor risk, audit logging, model monitoring, and incident response. Each domain is paired with a named risk area, business impact, and governance control, making it more granular than the 8-domain Dr. Vishal Pawar model captured elsewhere in this batch.

## Key Points

- Ten governance domains, each mapped to a specific risk area, business impact, and governance control
- More granular structure than the related 8-domain Pawar model captured in the same batch
- Data governance and model monitoring both included as first-class domains alongside compliance and security
- Vendor risk given its own row with a distinct governance control
- Useful as a checklist format rather than a purely conceptual diagram

## Why Yaz Saved This

A second, more granular governance framework to compare against the Pawar 8-domain model — useful for Yaz to synthesize a composite governance checklist for enterprise AI programs, tying into his ISO/IEC 42001 governance notes.
