# The AI Strategy Wheel — Seven Domains of Enterprise AI Strategy

## Summary

A Clare Kitching wheel diagram dividing enterprise AI strategy into seven domains: Ambition & Vision (own it at CEO level), Value & Use Cases (link initiatives to named owners and outcomes), Data (ready data for priority use cases, not everything), Technology (start with the current stack before exploring new investment), Operating Model (set clear approval and ownership for live initiatives), People, Skills & Culture (leaders role-model use to normalize it), and Risk & Governance (match controls to actual risk, not fear). Each domain pairs a short definition with a concrete action recommendation.

## Key Points

- Seven strategy domains: Ambition & Vision, Value & Use Cases, Data, Technology, Operating Model, People/Skills/Culture, Risk & Governance
- Ambition & Vision domain recommends CEO-level ownership tied to business goals
- Data domain explicitly recommends readying only the data needed for priority use cases, not full data completeness
- Risk & Governance domain recommends matching controls to actual risk level rather than blanket caution
- Operating Model domain focuses on approval and ongoing-ownership processes for live AI initiatives
