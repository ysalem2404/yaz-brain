# Building AI Fluency — 16 Ways to Move from AI Access to AI Capability

## Summary

A Clare Kitching infographic citing Microsoft research that 67% of the value people get from AI comes down to managers and culture, not tools. It organizes 16 fluency-building tactics into a 2x2 matrix of adoption vs capability and lower vs higher effort: Start Here (low effort, adoption-focused: access for all, acceptable-use policy, tips newsletter, hands-on training, prompt library), Add Next (low effort, capability-focused: leaders modeling AI use, champions, communities of practice, micro-practice), Invest as You Grow (higher effort, capability-focused: coaching, AI in workflows, role-based programs, skill sprints, hackathons), and Use Selectively (higher effort, adoption-focused: exec immersion, mandatory certification).

## Key Points

- Cites Microsoft research: 67% of AI value depends on managers and culture, not the tools themselves
- 16 tactics arranged in a 2x2 matrix: adoption vs capability, lower vs higher effort
- 'Start Here' quadrant (low effort/adoption) includes access for all, acceptable-use policy, and prompt library
- 'Invest as You Grow' quadrant (higher effort/capability) includes role-based programs, skill sprints, and hackathons
- 'Use Selectively' quadrant flags mandatory certification and exec immersion as higher-effort, use-with-care tactics
- Positions leaders modeling AI use as a low-effort but high-capability-impact lever

A practical, effort-sequenced AI upskilling roadmap for Yaz to reference when advising on enterprise AI training programs — the 67% managers-and-culture statistic is a strong anchor for change-management conversations.
