# AI Policy vs AI Governance in Enterprises — Comparison Table

## Summary

A 10-row comparison table distinguishing AI Policy from AI Governance across definition, purpose, scope, primary focus, ownership, key components, enforcement, lifecycle role, measurement, and outcome. Policy is framed as the documented rules an organization sets for AI use, while governance is the operational system that enforces, monitors, and adapts those rules across the AI lifecycle. The table is intended to clarify a frequently conflated distinction for enterprise leaders.

## Key Points

- Ten comparison dimensions: definition, purpose, scope, focus, ownership, components, enforcement, lifecycle role, measurement, outcome
- Policy = documented rules; Governance = the operational enforcement and monitoring system
- Ownership differs: policy often sits with legal/compliance, governance spans technical and operational teams
- Frames governance as covering the full AI lifecycle, while policy is more static
- Explicitly designed to resolve a common terminology conflation in enterprise AI discussions
