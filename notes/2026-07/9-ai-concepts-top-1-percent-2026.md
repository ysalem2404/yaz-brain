# The 9 AI Concepts Setting the Top 1% Apart in 2026

## Summary

An advanced practitioner-level infographic covering 9 AI concepts: Agentic Loops (think-execute-monitor-improve), MCP as a standard tool integration layer, Subagents/Multi-Agent Systems, AI Gateway (single access layer across model providers), Inference Economics (token cost and caching), Evals ('if you don't evaluate it, you can't deploy it'), Guardrails, Observability (traces/logs/metrics/dashboards), and 'The Bitter Lesson' (scale outperforms handcrafted systems).

## Key Points

- Agentic Loops: think, execute, monitor, improve, iterate until done
- MCP is framed as 'one standard layer for every tool' connecting agents to Gmail, GitHub, DBs, browsers
- AI Gateway provides a single access layer with auth, logging, and rate limiting across OpenAI/Anthropic/open source
- Inference Economics: cache hits reduce cost, cache misses increase cost, tracked against total bill
- Evals gate deployment: test inputs through model responses and an evaluation layer for pass/fail
- The Bitter Lesson: general models plus compute outperform handwritten rules long-term
