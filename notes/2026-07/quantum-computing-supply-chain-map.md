# Quantum Computing Supply Chain Map

## Summary

A supply-chain diagram from letstalkstocks.ca mapping the quantum computing industry across seven layers: materials, cryogenics, qubit hardware, chip fabrication, control electronics, software stack, and cloud access. Named companies span materials (DuPont), cryogenic cooling (Bluefors), qubit hardware and full-stack players (IBM, Google, IonQ, PsiQuantum, Rigetti), fabrication (Intel, TSMC), control electronics (Keysight), and cloud access (AWS, Azure). It also lists relevant thematic ETFs including QTUM, AIQ, BOTZ, SMH, and CIBR for tracking the sector financially.

## Key Points

- Seven-layer quantum supply chain: materials, cryogenics, qubit hardware, chip fab, control electronics, software stack, cloud access
- IBM, Google, IonQ, PsiQuantum, and Rigetti positioned as full-stack or hardware-focused quantum players
- Intel and TSMC included as fabrication partners bridging classical chipmaking into quantum
- AWS and Azure named as cloud access points for quantum-as-a-service
- Lists five thematic ETFs (QTUM, AIQ, BOTZ, SMH, CIBR) for financial tracking of the sector
