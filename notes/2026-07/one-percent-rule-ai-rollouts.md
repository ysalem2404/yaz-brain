# The 1% Rule for AI Rollouts — Concentrate Value, Don't Spread It

## Summary

A Carolyn Healey infographic arguing that successful AI rollouts concentrate investment in the narrow slice of work where AI compounds value, rather than spreading budget across many scattered pilots. It cites Gartner's prediction that 30%+ of GenAI projects were abandoned after POC by end of 2025 and 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027, BCG's finding that only 5% of companies are 'future-built' and generating 5x revenue/3x cost-reduction value from AI at scale, and McKinsey's finding that workflow redesign (not bolt-on AI) is one of the strongest contributors to AI business impact. Eight numbered principles guide where to concentrate effort, including 'spend 70% of effort on people and process' (per BCG's 10-20-70 rule) and looking at employees' existing shadow-AI workflows as a map to where value already lives.

## Key Points

- Central thesis: concentrate AI investment in the 1% of work where it compounds, stop funding the rest
- Gartner: 30%+ of GenAI POCs abandoned by end 2025; 40%+ of agentic AI projects to be canceled by end 2027
- BCG: only 5% of companies are 'future-built,' achieving 5x revenue increase and 3x cost reduction versus peers
- BCG's 10-20-70 rule: 10% algorithms, 20% tech/data, 70% people/process/change
- Recommends studying employees' existing 'shadow AI' power-user workflows as a map to real value
- Closing question for leaders: 'What do we stop funding?' rather than 'what do we fund next?'
