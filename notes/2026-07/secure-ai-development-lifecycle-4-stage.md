# Secure AI Development Lifecycle — 4-Stage Governance Pipeline

## Summary

An Aakash Yadav infographic detailing a four-stage secure AI development lifecycle: Step 1 Define Inputs and Governance (training datasets, adversarial examples, ethical AI guidelines, threat intelligence, compliance regulations); Step 2 Develop, Test, and Secure the Model (data validation, model version control, automated and adversarial/red-team testing, bias/fairness auditing, secure container registry management); Step 3 Approve and Deploy the Model (approval gates, CI/CD automation, policy enforcement, digital sign-off, continuous monitoring, automated alerts); and Step 4 Govern and Maintain the Output (production-ready artifacts, secure deployment configuration, comprehensive audit trails, compliance certification, security patches, documentation).

## Key Points

- Four-stage lifecycle: Define Inputs/Governance, Develop/Test/Secure, Approve/Deploy, Govern/Maintain
- Adversarial and red-team testing explicitly included in the development stage, not bolted on later
- Digital sign-off and policy enforcement engine are named as deployment control gates
- Automated alerts and incident notifications are part of the deployment stage, not just post-deployment monitoring
- Final stage requires compliance certification and end-user/technical documentation as deliverables, not just working code
- Emphasizes traceability — comprehensive audit trail is a named deliverable of stage 4

## Why Yaz Saved This

A detailed, stage-gated MLOps/AI governance pipeline Yaz can reference when designing secure AI development processes for FedRAMP-compliant enterprise deployments — the explicit approval-gate and audit-trail structure maps well to government contracting requirements.
