# Enterprise AI Governance Model — 8 Domains (Dr. Vishal Pawar)

## Summary

An infographic by Dr. Vishal Pawar (CAIO/CDO/CAIA) organizing enterprise AI governance into eight domains arranged around a central robot icon: incident response and escalation, model performance monitoring, audit logging and accountability, vendor and third-party risk, security and access controls, human oversight and approval gates, regulatory and compliance alignment, data foundations, model validation and testing, and bias detection and fairness controls. The domains collectively span technical, operational, and compliance dimensions of governance.

## Key Points

- Eight governance domains spanning technical, operational, and compliance dimensions
- Vendor and third-party risk is explicitly called out as its own domain
- Human oversight and approval gates are distinguished from incident response/escalation
- Bias detection and fairness controls are treated as a distinct governance domain, not folded into model validation
- Audit logging and accountability paired together as one domain
- Author credentials (CAIO/CDO/CAIA) signal a practitioner-level governance framework

## Why Yaz Saved This

Another practitioner AI governance framework for Yaz's ISO 42001 and enterprise governance research — useful to compare against the AIForLeaders 10-domain model captured in the same batch for building a composite governance checklist.
