# Palantir and Nvidia Expand Partnership to Power Secure Government AI

## Summary

A TradingNews social post reports Palantir and Nvidia have expanded their partnership to bring Nvidia AI Enterprise software and the open Nemotron AI models into sovereign environments built for U.S. government agencies and critical infrastructure. The platform is designed to run in highly secure settings including classified, air-gapped, and on-premise systems, letting organizations train and deploy AI while keeping sensitive data under their own control. The move strengthens Palantir's position in government AI and gives Nvidia a growth avenue beyond chips, with the piece framing secure sovereign AI deployments as one of the industry's fastest-growing markets as governments increase investment.

## Key Points

- Palantir and Nvidia expanded partnership targets sovereign AI for U.S. government agencies and critical infrastructure
- Platform bundles Nvidia AI Enterprise software with the open Nemotron model family
- Explicitly supports classified, air-gapped, and on-premise deployment — data stays under the organization's own control
- Strengthens Palantir's government-AI market position; extends Nvidia beyond chip sales into enterprise software/services
- Framed as part of a fast-growing sovereign/secure government AI deployment market
