# ServiceNow and NVIDIA — Autonomous Enterprise AI Agents

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-07/servicenow-and-nvidia-autonomous-enterprise-ai-agents.png)

## Summary
An architecture overview of the ServiceNow–NVIDIA partnership for governed digital labor. ServiceNow contributes workflow orchestration, system actions, permissions, and an AI Control Tower; NVIDIA contributes GPU infrastructure, NIM microservices, open-weight Nemotron models, and the OpenShell sandboxed runtime for policy-enforced execution.

## Key points
- ServiceNow's AI Control Tower centralizes policy authoring, governance, audit logs, and workflow actions
- NVIDIA OpenShell provides sandboxed, zero-trust execution with observability and auditability
- The partnership links enterprise workflows, model infrastructure, industrial AI, robotics, and autonomous desktop tasks

## Why I saved this
A concrete vendor architecture reference for comparing AI control-plane, workflow, and secure execution patterns in enterprise and industrial environments.
