# 10 AI Concepts Everyone Should Understand in 2026

## Summary

An AIForLeaders.com infographic defining 10 foundational AI concepts for 2026: Agentic AI, AI Copilots, Multi-Agent Systems, RAG, Model Context Protocol (MCP), Human-in-the-Loop AI, AI Orchestration, AI Governance, PromptOps, and AI ROI Mapping. Each entry lists its role, what it does, where it adds value, and its key risk. The piece is designed as an executive-level glossary distinguishing related-but-distinct AI concepts that get conflated in leadership conversations.

## Key Points

- Agentic AI: autonomous action across tools; key risk is unintended actions from weak guardrails
- AI Copilots: embedded assistive AI for productivity; risk of over-reliance
- Multi-Agent Systems: specialized collaborating agents; risk of coordination failures
- RAG grounds AI in company data; key risk is outdated/incorrect retrieval
- MCP is described as the integration standard connecting AI tools to apps/data/systems
- AI Governance covers policy, permissions, monitoring, and accountability for safe AI use
- PromptOps and AI ROI Mapping round out the operational and value-measurement layers
