# How to Go From Data Governance to AI Governance — 5 Maturity Levels

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-07/data-to-ai-governance-5-levels.png)

## Summary
5-level maturity model most organizations never fully progress through, mapped across 5 dimensions (Policy, Risk, Roles, Compliance, Data): Level 1 Data Only — basic data handling, no AI visibility, IT owns storage, no AI-specific obligations, employees paste sensitive data into public AI; Level 2 Data Governed — formal data policy, data stewards, no AI oversight, GDPR/privacy only, clean data catalog no AI tracking; Level 3a AI Extended — data policy covers AI I/O, shadow AI inventory done, AI coordinator bridges data/leadership, GDPR↔EU AI Act gap analysis, data classified by AI sensitivity; Level 3b (Level 4) AI Controlled — approved vendor list with usage boundaries, output validation before business use, embedded AI risk owners, mapped to EU AI Act risk tiers, training vs production data separation; Level 5 AI Governance — AI decisions auditable, automated drift detection, AI ethics board reviews high-impact use cases, regulatory filings include AI disclosures, real-time data lineage from source to AI output.

## Key points
- Level 1 → Level 5 progression: policy, risk, roles, compliance, data mature together
- Shadow AI inventory is a Level 3 requirement — most orgs are Level 1 or 2 pretending to be higher
- AI ethics board only appears at Level 5
- EU AI Act tier mapping = Level 4 milestone
- Real-time data lineage source→AI output = Level 5
