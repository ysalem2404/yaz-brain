# AI in Cyber — The Autonomous Cyber Defense Trinity (GRC, Pentest, SOC)

## Summary

An infographic depicting how AI transforms three cybersecurity functions into an interconnected 'autonomous cyber defense trinity': AI GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance) providing automated policy mapping and predictive risk scoring; AI Pentest running automated exploit simulation, vulnerability prioritization, and red-teaming at scale; and AI SOC (Security Operations Center) delivering noise reduction, autonomous response, and contextual investigation. A feedback loop connects all three: AI Pentest finds a hole, AI GRC logs and prioritizes the risk, and AI SOC deploys a guardrail or patch. A comparison table contrasts legacy manual security (reactive, days, static, annual, human triage) with AI-integrated security (proactive, milliseconds, live, 24/7, AI orchestration).

## Key Points

- Three-function trinity: AI GRC, AI Pentest, AI SOC, connected by a synergy/feedback loop
- AI SOC's noise reduction claims filtering 95% of false-positive alerts to surface real signal
- AI Pentest enables 'red teaming at scale' — thousands of simulations without large human teams
- Legacy vs AI-integrated comparison table: reactive/days vs proactive/milliseconds response times
- Feedback loop explicitly closes the gap between finding vulnerabilities (pentest), logging/prioritizing risk (GRC), and deploying fixes (SOC)
- Positions 24/7 AI orchestration as replacing calendar-based annual pentest cycles
