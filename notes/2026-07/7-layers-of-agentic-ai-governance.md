# 7 Layers of Agentic AI Governance

## Summary

A seven-layer framework for governing autonomous AI agents responsibly: Governance Strategy & Principles, System Inventory & Transparency, Risk Assessment & Classification, Controls & Guardrails, Monitoring & Observability, Audit/Testing & Assurance, and Response & Continuous Improvement. Each layer lists three concrete actions, moving from top-level policy definition down to red-teaming, incident response, and continuous learning.

## Key Points

- Layer 1 sets mission, ethical principles, and governance ownership roles
- Layer 2 maintains a registry of all AI agents, models, tools, and third-party integrations
- Layer 3 classifies agents by risk and documents mitigation plans
- Layer 4 implements access controls, behavioral constraints, and human override validation
- Layer 5 monitors performance/behavior in real time and detects anomalies
- Layer 6 conducts audits, red-teaming, and maintains compliance evidence
- Layer 7 closes the loop with incident response, rollback plans, and policy updates

A layered governance blueprint directly applicable to structuring an agentic AI governance program at Bechtel, from initial policy through continuous audit — pairs well with FedRAMP continuous monitoring requirements.
