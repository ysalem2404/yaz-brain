# Difference Between AI, ML, DL, GenAI, LLMs, RAG and Agentic AI

## Summary

A hierarchical explainer positioning AI as the umbrella term, with ML as a subset of AI, DL as a subset of ML, GenAI as a subset of DL, LLMs as a type of GenAI, RAG as LLMs combined with external knowledge, and Agentic AI as autonomous systems that reason, plan, use tools, and act. Includes examples for each layer (e.g., expert systems for AI, spam detection for ML, image recognition for DL, ChatGPT/Midjourney for GenAI, GPT-4/Claude for LLMs, enterprise chatbots for RAG, and AutoGPT/LangGraph agents for Agentic AI) plus an evolution flow and real-world example table.

## Key Points

- Hierarchy: AI > ML > DL > GenAI > LLMs > RAG > Agentic AI, each a more specialized subset
- AI examples: expert systems, rule-based systems, robotics, game AI, autonomous agents
- ML examples: spam detection, recommendation systems, fraud detection, predictive analytics
- GenAI examples: ChatGPT, Midjourney, DALL-E, Claude, Stable Diffusion, Suno, Runway
- RAG combines GenAI with external knowledge for more accurate, up-to-date answers
- Agentic AI examples: OpenAI Agents, Claude Computer Use, AutoGPT, LangGraph Agents, Manus AI
