# From Models to Autonomy: The Agentic AI Stack and Why Most Companies Are Stuck at Ring Three

## Summary

This layered-ring infographic maps the full progression from foundational AI/ML through Deep Learning, GenAI, AI Agents, to fully Agentic AI, citing Gartner's prediction that 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027. It calls out that most companies are 'stuck at ring three' — they generate content but few execute with governed autonomy. The outer orange ring lists the governance-heavy capabilities needed to progress: feedback loops and evaluators, cost/resource management, long-term autonomy and goal chaining, governance/safety/guardrails, memory governance and retention policies, observability and tracing, delegation and handoff protocols, risk management, agent marketplaces, and failure recovery/replanning.

## Key Points

- Progression: AI & ML → Deep Learning → GenAI → AI Agents → Agentic AI, each ring adding new capabilities on top of the last
- Gartner prediction cited: over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027
- Most companies are stuck at 'ring three' (GenAI) — generating content but not executing with governed autonomy
- Outer ring frames the governance requirements for real autonomy: guardrails, observability/tracing, risk management, memory retention policies, delegation protocols, failure recovery/replanning
- AI Agents ring includes context management, human-in-the-loop oversight, memory systems, and self-reflection/error recovery as prerequisites before reaching full agentic autonomy
