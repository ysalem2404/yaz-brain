# Enterprise AI on One Page — 9 Reference Frameworks

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-07/enterprise-ai-on-one-page.png)

## Summary
9-panel mega-poster: (1) AI Strategy Wheel — 7 spokes (Ambition/Vision, Value & Use Cases, Data, Technology, Operating Model, People/Skills/Culture, Risk & Governance) each with a leadership imperative; (2) AI Value Funnel — Opportunity → Readiness → Experimentation → Adoption → Scale, with 'where value gets lost' + 'what leaders can do' per stage; (3) Total Cost of AI Ownership — iceberg diagram: approved costs (build, licenses, integrations, pilot compute) vs hidden actual costs (data prep, production API costs, retraining/evals/drift, HITL oversight, compliance/audit, change mgmt/training, vendor lock-in exit, shadow AI cleanup); (4) 4 Foundations for Successful AI Adoption — help people learn, build AI into how work gets done, make the tech easy to use, give AI the right data; (5) Types of AI — AI Agents & Agentic AI, Generative AI, Deep Learning, Classical ML, Rules & Logic (with example use cases per type); (6) Building AI Fluency — 16 tactics on effort/capability grid (Prompt library → mandatory certification, tips newsletter → hackathons, coaching, communities of practice, micro-practice); (7) 20 Ways to Measure AI Impact — financial (revenue from AI, cost savings, ROI, benefit-cost), operational (post-AI throughput, cycle time, straight-through, labor capacity), customer (conversion, retention, satisfaction, first-contact resolution), scaling (efficiency improvement, time to value, pilot-to-production, AI use case value coverage), workforce (employee engagement, adoption rate, business use employment rate), adoption (AI literacy score); (8) AI Governance for Every Leader — CEO/CFO/COO/CIO/CDO responsibilities in one row each; (9) 4 Layers of AI Guardrails — Governance (risk appetite, accountability, oversight), Operating Model (translate strategy to behavior), Process (embed safeguards in delivery), System (technical safeguards in AI systems), with maturity stats (75% CEOs say it's their call by 2026, 32% of employees use AI at work but keep it secret, 15% AI-mature orgs have AI board vs 14% low-maturity, 57% business units trust new AI vs 14% at low-maturity orgs).

## Key points
- TCO iceberg is the strongest single visual — 'what costs get approved' vs 'what AI ownership actually costs'
- 20 KPIs across 6 categories — most orgs measure 3-4, this is the full menu
- AI Value Funnel maps each stage-loss with a specific leader action — good rescue playbook
- 'AI Governance for Every Leader' row-per-C-suite: CEO 'move fast, safely'; CFO 'control is cheaper than cleanup'; COO 'agents act, they don't just answer'; CIO 'shadow AI is already here'; CDO 'data foundation, no governance'
- 16-tactic AI Fluency grid gives leaders a menu ordered by effort vs capability delta
