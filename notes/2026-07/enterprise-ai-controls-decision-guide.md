# Enterprise AI Controls: A Guide to Choosing the Right Control

## Summary

A decision-tree infographic mapping five enterprise AI problem categories — Discover, Govern, Secure, Operate, Measure — to concrete controls. Discover AI covers asset inventory and CMDB mapping; Govern AI covers approval workflows and lifecycle review; Secure AI covers least-privilege access and data protection; Operate AI covers testing, monitoring, and fallback; Measure Value covers adoption, cost, and ROI metrics. Ends with a 'where to focus first' summary per pillar.

## Key Points

- Five pillars: Discover, Govern, Secure, Operate, Measure
- Discover AI: AI asset inventory and CMDB mapping to link agents/models to services
- Govern AI: approval workflows, policy assignment, and lifecycle review for new vs existing AI
- Secure AI: least-privilege access, data protection/masking, human approval, prompt guardrails
- Operate AI: testing/evaluation, runtime monitoring, retry/fallback, escalation paths
- Measure Value: adoption metrics, cost metrics (token cost per task), business value and user satisfaction
- Explicit guidance to always define ownership, risk level, and business outcome before scaling

## Why Yaz Saved This

A ready-made decision framework for structuring Bechtel's AI governance program pillar-by-pillar — maps cleanly onto how a FedRAMP/GCCH AI Center of Excellence would need to organize discovery, governance, security, operations, and value tracking.
