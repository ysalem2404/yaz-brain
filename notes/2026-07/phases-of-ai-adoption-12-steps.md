# Phases of AI Adoption: 12 Steps from Foundation to Scale

## Summary

A 12-phase infographic tracing enterprise AI adoption across four stages — Foundation, Enablement, Execution, and Scale. Phases include executive sponsorship, business-aligned strategy, readiness assessment, building a data foundation, establishing governance before scaling, running high-impact pilots, redesigning workflows, managing organizational change, training and upskilling staff, developing AI champions, tracking KPIs and ROI, and finally scaling what works. The sequencing argues that governance must precede scale, not follow it.

## Key Points

- Four macro-stages: Foundation, Enablement, Execution, Scale
- Governance is explicitly sequenced before scaling, not as a late-stage addition
- Readiness assessment and data foundation come before any pilots are run
- AI champions and upskilling are treated as adoption infrastructure, not soft extras
- KPI/ROI tracking is a distinct phase preceding the decision to scale
- Ends with 'scale what works' rather than scaling everything simultaneously
