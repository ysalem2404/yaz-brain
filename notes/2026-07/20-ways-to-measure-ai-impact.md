# 20 Ways to Measure AI Impact

## Summary

A Clare Kitching infographic cataloging twenty concrete KPIs for measuring AI impact across five categories: financial (revenue uplift, cost savings, payback period, benefit-cost ratio, cost per transaction), operational (cycle time reduction, straight-through-processing value), customer (sales/retention/CSAT/first-contact-resolution uplift, collection efficiency), scaling (time to value, pilot-to-production rate, AI use case coverage), and workforce/adoption (employee experience uplift, adoption rate, business unit engagement, AI literacy score, labour capacity released). The list is designed to move AI evaluation beyond vague productivity claims into named, trackable metrics.

## Key Points

- Twenty KPIs grouped into financial, operational, customer, scaling, and workforce/adoption categories
- Financial metrics include payback period and benefit-cost ratio, not just cost savings
- Pilot-to-production rate is called out as a scaling-specific metric distinct from adoption rate
- AI literacy score included as a workforce readiness metric
- Labour capacity released is distinguished from raw cost savings
- Designed to counter vague 'productivity' claims with named, trackable numbers
