# AI in Construction: Solving the Industry's Top Six Pain Points

## Summary

A LinkedIn post by Fatima Cheema (building AI for real estate and construction) argues AI will transform construction by removing friction rather than replacing people. The infographic lists six top pain points — document overload, manual/time-consuming work, poor communication, risk and cost uncertainty, lack of real-time insights, and disconnected tools/data — paired with concrete AI interventions like automating takeoffs, extracting data from plans, predicting risks/delays, and summarizing RFIs and meeting notes. Key insights close the piece: AI won't replace construction professionals, it will empower them, and impact will come from solving real problems rather than chasing hype.

## Key Points

- Six named pain points: document overload, manual/time-consuming work, poor communication, risk & cost uncertainty, lack of real-time insights, disconnected tools & data
- AI interventions mapped directly to pain points: automate takeoffs/estimations, extract/organize plan data, predict risks and cost overruns, summarize RFIs and reports
- Core thesis: AI transforms construction 'by removing friction,' not by replacing people
- Recommends starting small — solve one problem well, then scale with impact rather than broad hype-driven rollouts
- Positions AI as empowerment for construction professionals rather than a headcount-reduction tool

## Why Yaz Saved This

Directly relevant to Bechtel's engineering-procurement-construction business — a framework for pitching AI use cases (RFI summarization, document extraction, risk prediction) that map cleanly onto EPC project pain points Yaz sees firsthand.
