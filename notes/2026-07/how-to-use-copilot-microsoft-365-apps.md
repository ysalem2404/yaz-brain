# How to Use Copilot Across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, and OneNote

## Summary

A per-app breakdown of Copilot capabilities across the core Microsoft 365 suite: Word (structured content generation, tone/clarity improvement), Excel (data analysis, formulas, charts/dashboards), PowerPoint (idea-to-slide conversion, layout suggestions), Outlook (drafting replies, tone adjustment for busy inboxes), Teams (meeting summarization, action item tracking), and OneNote (note summarization and organizing brainstorms into action plans).

## Key Points

- Word: generate structured content from prompts, improve clarity/tone for professional documents
- Excel: analyze data, suggest formulas/trends, build dashboards and reports
- PowerPoint: turn documents into slides, suggest layout/content/visuals for pitch decks
- Outlook: draft and adjust tone of replies for busy inboxes
- Teams: summarize meetings, track action items and decisions for alignment
- OneNote: summarize notes and organize brainstorms into actionable plans
