# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1 — GPT-5.6 Luna cost/intelligence chart

## Summary
Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1 scatter plot: Intelligence Score (y-axis, 25-55) vs Cost per Task (x-axis, log-scale $0.01–$1). GPT-5.6 Luna leads the frontier at ~51 intelligence for ~$0.06/task, beating Claude Opus 5 Low, GLM-5.2 Max, Gemini 3.6 Flash on cost-efficiency. DeepSeek V4 Pro sits at ~44 for ~$0.05. Claude Haiku Reasoning is a striking outlier — high cost (~$0.20) for low score (~29).

## Key points
- GPT-5.6 Luna = new price/perf frontier — same intelligence as Claude Opus 5 Low at ~1/6 the cost
- Gemini 3.6 Flash competitive on intelligence but ~10x GPT-5.6 Luna cost
- DeepSeek V4 Pro remains a strong open-weight-ish challenger — best sub-$0.05 non-OpenAI option
- Claude Haiku Reasoning underperforms its price band — worth verifying benchmark methodology
- GLM-5.2 Max highest raw score (~51) but at ~5x GPT-5.6 Luna cost
