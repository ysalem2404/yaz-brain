# 3 Roles of AI Every Leader Should Understand: Analyst, Creator, Worker

## Summary

A hand-drawn style infographic distinguishing Traditional AI (the Analyst — studies data, finds patterns, high maturity), Generative AI (the Creator — produces new content/ideas/code, medium maturity), and Agentic AI (the Worker — takes multi-step action across systems, early maturity). Each role lists where it shines, what it needs, maturity level, risks, and how to onboard it, e.g., starting with one narrow high-value workflow for agentic AI.

## Key Points

- Traditional AI: high maturity, predictable patterns, needs clean labelled data, best for clear predictions
- Generative AI: medium maturity but fast-improving, needs a strong base model and grounding data
- Agentic AI: early maturity, powerful but needs strong controls/guardrails, risk of cascading errors
- Onboarding guidance: start with one prediction problem, a simple chatbot, or one narrow high-value workflow respectively
- Frames the CEO/data-team tension of 'why aren't we using AI yet' when traditional AI/ML has existed for years

## Why Yaz Saved This

A simple three-tier mental model useful for explaining to non-technical Bechtel stakeholders why agentic AI requires materially more governance maturity than the analytics AI they're already comfortable with.
