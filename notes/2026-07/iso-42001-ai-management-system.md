# ISO 42001: AI Management System Standard for Responsible and Trustworthy AI

## Summary

An overview of ISO/IEC 42001:2023, described as the world's first global standard for AI Management Systems. The wheel diagram covers seven components: AI Governance, Risk Management, Data Governance, AI System Lifecycle, Transparency & Explainability, Human Oversight, Performance Evaluation, and Continual Improvement. It lists key benefits (reduced AI risk, improved transparency/fairness/accountability, regulatory compliance) and target adopters (enterprises, public sector, AI solution providers).

## Key Points

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the first global standard specifically for AI Management Systems
- Seven-part cycle: AI Governance, Risk Management, Data Governance, AI System Lifecycle, Transparency & Explainability, Human Oversight, Performance Evaluation, Continual Improvement
- Key benefits include reduced AI risk, improved transparency/fairness/accountability, and regulatory compliance
- Applicable to organizations developing or using AI, AI solution providers, enterprises, and public sector/government organizations
- Positions certification as both a risk-management tool and a competitive differentiator
