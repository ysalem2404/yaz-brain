# AIQ Agentic AI Cuts Geoscience Work from Months to 45 Minutes

## Summary

A CNBC Managing Middle East interview transcript featuring Dennis Jol, CEO of AIQ, describing ENERGY.ai, an agentic AI platform built on G42 infrastructure and ADNOC operational data. The system processed 1.1 million documents to compress subsurface geoscience workflows from weeks/months down to 45 minutes. Jol frames the differentiator as access to six-plus years of real large-scale operational data versus competitors' theoretical physics-only models. The transcript includes visual context of ADNOC control rooms and digital twin displays.

## Key Points

- ENERGY.ai is AIQ's large-scale agentic AI platform for the energy sector
- Processed 1.1 million documents plus ADNOC operational data
- Reduced geoscience workflows from weeks/months to 45 minutes
- Built on G42 infrastructure combined with ADNOC's proprietary data
- Models have been in production for over six years, a maturity differentiator
- Competitors are seen as relying on theoretical physics models without comparable data scale
