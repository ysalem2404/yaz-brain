# What People Think Enterprise AI Implementation Is vs. What It Really Is (15-Layer Reality)

## Summary

This side-by-side infographic from aidigital.co.uk contrasts the simplistic three-step mental model most people hold of enterprise AI implementation (AI Tool → Prompt → Deploy) against the real 15-layer process: business problem definition, executive sponsorship, AI readiness assessment, data quality foundation, use-case prioritization, architecture selection, security & privacy, governance framework, model evaluation, system integration, human-in-the-loop design, change management, user training, feedback & iteration, and enterprise-wide scaling.

## Key Points

- Popular misconception: enterprise AI is just 'AI Tool → Prompt → Deploy'
- Reality is a 15-stage sequence starting with business problem definition and executive sponsorship, not tool selection
- Security & privacy and governance framework appear as distinct, dedicated stages before model evaluation even begins
- Human-in-the-loop, change management, and user training are treated as core implementation stages, not afterthoughts
- Enterprise-wide scaling is explicitly the final stage, after feedback and iteration cycles — not the starting assumption
