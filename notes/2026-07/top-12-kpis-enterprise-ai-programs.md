# Top 12 KPIs for Enterprise AI Programs

## Summary

A 12-KPI framework for measuring enterprise AI program health, spanning adoption (AI Adoption Rate, Active Usage Rate), performance (Task Completion Rate, AI Accuracy Rate), value (Productivity Improvement, Business Value Realized, ROI, Cost per AI Task), and risk/trust (Human Escalation Rate, AI Incident Rate, Compliance Pass Rate, User Satisfaction Score). Each KPI includes what it measures and why it matters.

## Key Points

- AI Adoption Rate and Active Usage Rate distinguish reach from sustained engagement
- Task Completion Rate and AI Accuracy Rate measure operational reliability
- Productivity Improvement and Business Value Realized connect AI to measurable outcomes
- ROI and Cost per AI Task quantify financial return and efficiency
- Human Escalation Rate reveals autonomy limitations and confidence gaps
- AI Incident Rate and Compliance Pass Rate track risk and regulatory alignment
- User Satisfaction Score captures trust and usability
