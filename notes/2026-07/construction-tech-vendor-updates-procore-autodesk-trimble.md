# Construction Tech Vendor Updates — Procore, Autodesk, Trimble M&A and Interoperability

## Summary

A construction technology vendor update covering four recent moves: Procore's acquisition of AI startup Datagrid to automate document-heavy workflows and integrate data across systems; Autodesk's acquisition of Rhumbix to strengthen Autodesk Construction Cloud with real-time field data capture and labor productivity insights; Trimble's acquisition of Document Crunch, bringing AI-powered contract and risk analysis to Trimble's platform; and an Autodesk-Nemetschek interoperability announcement aimed at breaking down data-exchange silos across leading construction platforms. The piece frames the overall trend as platform consolidation, AI-powered workflows, and more connected ecosystems.

## Key Points

- Procore acquires Datagrid: AI agents to automate document-heavy workflows and cross-system data integration
- Autodesk acquires Rhumbix: strengthens Construction Cloud with real-time field data capture and labor productivity insights
- Trimble acquires Document Crunch: AI-powered contract and risk analysis for margin protection
- Autodesk and Nemetschek announce interoperability — a notable step toward breaking down platform silos in construction software
- Overall trend framed as platform consolidation + AI-powered workflows + connected ecosystems
- Directly relevant to construction/EPC technology stack decisions
