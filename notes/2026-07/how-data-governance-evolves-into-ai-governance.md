# How Data Governance Evolves into AI Governance — 10-Row Mapping

## Summary

A 10-row infographic mapping each data governance discipline to its AI governance evolution: data quality management extends to model reliability prediction; data lineage tracking evolves into bias detection and audit trails; access controls become ethical use boundaries; data cataloging becomes model registry and discovery; compliance frameworks extend to regulatory readiness for laws like the EU AI Act; data stewardship roles become model ownership and accountability; schema standards become training data specifications; versioning and change control become model drift monitoring; security and encryption extend to adversarial defense; and data quality metrics evolve into explainability requirements.

## Key Points

- Ten direct one-to-one mappings from data governance disciplines to their AI governance equivalents
- Frames AI governance as a natural extension of existing data governance muscle memory, not a separate discipline
- Compliance frameworks (GDPR/CCPA/HIPAA) extend to regulatory readiness for the EU AI Act and algorithmic accountability laws
- Security and encryption extend to adversarial defense against prompt injection, data poisoning, and model extraction
- Data quality metrics evolve into explainability requirements (fairness, transparency, prediction confidence)
- Positions data lineage tracking as the direct ancestor of bias detection and audit trail capability
