# The 4-Layer AI Governance Framework — Policy, Process, People, Proof

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-07/4-layer-ai-governance-policy-process-people-proof.png)

## Summary
4-layer pyramid: (1) Policy — Documents you need: AI Use Case Policy, Data Handling Policy, Vendor Policy, Incident Response Policy. 'Org-wide guardrails, not model-specific rules.' (2) Process — Workflows you need: Intake + risk classification, Vendor approval workflow, Eval design + quality gates, Pre-prod review, Monitoring cadence, Version change approval, Incident response playbook. 'Each workflow must be documented and tested.' (3) People — RACI you need: Executive sponsor (Accountable), AI PM (Coordination lead), Legal counsel (Consulted), ML engineering lead (Consulted), CISO (Consulted), Business owner (Informed). 'The AI PM enforces the RACI in practice.' (4) Proof — Artifacts you need: Use case registry, Eval results archive, Vendor memos, Incident post-mortems, Model version changelog, Compliance mapping. 'Auditors ask for evidence, not intent.' Grounded on EU AI Act (risk-based obligations), NIST AI RMF (trustworthy AI framework), ISO 42001 (AI Management System Standard). What the AI PM really does: translate legal ↔ engineering, turn policy into workflow, assign clear ownership, keep proof audit-ready.

## Key points
- Pyramid ordering matters: Policy → Process → People → Proof. Skip a layer and the framework 'collapses under audit'.
- AI PM emerges as the pivot role — sits between legal, engineering, and executive
- Proof = artifacts, not intent. This is the audit-readiness lens.
- Ties directly to EU AI Act + NIST AI RMF + ISO 42001 trilogy
