# The Governance Stack Behind Reliable AI Agents

## Summary

An infographic by @rakeshgohel01 contrasting Data Governance with AI Governance side by side, then laying out a five-layer governance stack architecture that sits beneath reliable production AI agents. It identifies five concrete governance controls: trusted data pipelines, access controls, model guardrails, human escalation paths, and continuous monitoring. The piece frames AI governance as an evolution of, rather than a replacement for, existing data governance discipline.

## Key Points

- Side-by-side comparison of Data Governance vs AI Governance scope and focus
- Five-layer governance stack architecture underlying reliable agents
- Five governance controls: trusted data pipelines, access controls, model guardrails, human escalation, continuous monitoring
- Frames AI governance as built on top of mature data governance practice
- Emphasizes human escalation paths as a required control, not optional
