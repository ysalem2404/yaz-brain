# 4 Foundations for Successful AI Adoption

## Summary

Clare Kitching's four-circle Venn diagram names four foundations for successful AI adoption: Help People Learn How to Use AI (celebrate small wins, let teams experiment safely, show practical recognizable examples), Build AI Into How Work Gets Done (identify tasks where AI saves time, redesign workflows with AI included, remove unnecessary manual steps), Make the Technology Easy to Use (provide the right tools, integrate AI with existing systems, remove unnecessary friction), and Give AI the Right Data (connect AI to trusted data sources, organize knowledge so AI can access it, improve data quality and visibility). Overlapping regions describe compounding outcomes: people know when/how to use AI, people trust AI insights, AI tools understand organizational information, people can use AI easily — converging on the center: 'We See AI Making a Difference.'

## Key Points

- Four foundations arranged as overlapping circles rather than a linear list, emphasizing that adoption success requires all four simultaneously, not sequentially
- Overlap regions name compounding effects: people know when/how to use AI (learning × workflow), people trust AI insights (data × technology), AI tools understand organizational information (workflow × data), people can use AI easily (technology × learning)
- Center convergence point explicitly labeled 'We See AI Making a Difference' — the outcome only achieved when all four foundations overlap
- 'Build AI Into How Work Gets Done' foundation explicitly calls for redesigning workflows with AI included, not bolting AI onto unchanged processes — consistent with the workflow-first theme across Yaz's saved content
- Companion piece to Clare Kitching's other adoption/governance infographics in this collection, sharing the same visual branding
