# 6 Mental Models for Enterprise AI: A Cheat Sheet From BCG, Gartner, Bain, McKinsey, and NIST

## Summary

Clare Kitching's cheat sheet compiles six named mental models from major consulting and standards bodies for enterprise AI strategy, investment, operating model, value capture, and risk: BCG's 'Deploy, Reshape, Invent' progression; Gartner's 'AI Opportunity Radar' (mapping use cases across everyday-vs-game-changing and internal-vs-external-facing quadrants); Bain's 'AI-era Operating Model' (a six-part wheel covering structure, management systems, leadership/culture, talent, business processes, technology/data); McKinsey's 'Six Dimensions of AI Value Capture' (strategy, talent, operating model, technology, data, adoption/scaling); BCG's '10-20-70' rule; and NIST's 'Govern, Map, Measure, Manage' AI risk lifecycle.

## Key Points

- Six frameworks from six different authorities compiled onto a single reference sheet: BCG (Deploy/Reshape/Invent and 10-20-70), Gartner (AI Opportunity Radar), Bain (AI-era Operating Model), McKinsey (Six Dimensions of AI Value Capture), NIST (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage)
- Gartner's AI Opportunity Radar plots use cases on two axes: everyday AI vs game-changing AI, and internal operations vs external customer-facing — a prioritization tool for use-case selection
- NIST's 'Govern, Map, Measure, Manage' is explicitly described as managing AI risk systematically across the full lifecycle, with Govern at the center connecting to the other three
- Bain's AI-era Operating Model wheel covers six dimensions: technology/data, structure/teams/accountabilities, management systems, leadership/culture, talent engine/roles, business processes
- BCG's Deploy/Reshape/Invent progression explicitly balances quick productivity wins against workflow redesign and new business model creation
