# 7 Reasons Agentic AI Projects Fail

## Summary

Carolyn Healey's data-backed breakdown of why agentic AI projects fail cites Gartner's prediction that 33% of enterprise software will include agentic capabilities by 2028, set against a warning that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027 due to organizational unreadiness. Seven root causes are detailed with supporting statistics: unready data (63% lack proper data management practices per Gartner 2024; 80% cite data limitations per McKinsey 2025), underestimated integration complexity (42% need 8+ data sources; 86% require tech stack upgrades per Tray.ai 2025), security/governance as an afterthought (only 1 in 5 companies has mature agent governance per Deloitte 2026), "agent washing" (only ~130 of thousands of vendors are genuinely agentic per Gartner 2025), a silent skills gap (only 28% of employees know how to use their company's AI tools per WalkMe 2025), missing business cases (57% of failed AI projects expected too much too fast per Gartner 2026), and infrastructure that can't keep pace (IT costs projected to rise 2-3x as agentic workloads expand per McKinsey 2026).

## Key Points

- Gartner: 33% of enterprise software will include agentic capabilities by 2028, but 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end of 2027
- Reason 1 - Data isn't ready: 63% lack proper data management practices (Gartner 2024); 80% cite data limitations as primary roadblock (McKinsey 2025)
- Reason 2 - Integration complexity underestimated: 42% need 8+ data sources; 86% require tech stack upgrades (Tray.ai 2025)
- Reason 3 - Security & governance as afterthought: only 1 in 5 companies has a mature governance model for autonomous agents (Deloitte 2026)
- Reason 4 - Agent washing: only ~130 of thousands of agentic AI vendors are genuine (Gartner 2025)
- Reason 5 - Silent skills gap: only 28% of employees know how to use company AI tools (WalkMe 2025); 38% of IT leaders cite skills gaps as failure cause (Gartner 2026)
- Reason 6 - No business case: 57% of failed AI projects expected too much too fast (Gartner 2026)
- Reason 7 - Infrastructure can't keep pace: IT infrastructure costs projected to rise 2-3x for agentic workloads (McKinsey 2026)
