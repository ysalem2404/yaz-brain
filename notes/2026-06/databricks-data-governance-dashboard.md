# Databricks Data Governance Dashboard

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-06/databricks-data-governance-dashboard.png)

## Summary
A Databricks governance dashboard shows catalog and asset counts, asset usage, governed tags, data classification, data quality, and recommendations for missing tags, comments, and unused assets.

## Key points
- The dashboard brings catalog, asset, usage, classification, and quality signals into one view
- Recommendations highlight missing tags, missing comments, and unused assets for curation
- Governance metrics can make data stewardship visible and operational
