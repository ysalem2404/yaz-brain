# Databricks Center of Excellence — Adoption Playbook

![Infographic](/yaz-brain/public/img/notes/2026-06/databricks-center-of-excellence-adoption-playbook.png)

## Summary
A Databricks Center of Excellence playbook covers onboarding and training, office hours and technical support, adoption advocacy, collaboration, performance monitoring, cost management, and knowledge sharing.

## Key points
- Enablement includes onboarding users, study groups, certifications, and custom training
- Adoption work combines office hours, technical troubleshooting, proofs of concept, best-practice sharing, and collaboration
- The operating model also includes performance monitoring, cost management, a knowledge base, and a community of practice

## Why I saved this
A concrete operating model for turning a platform rollout into sustained adoption, support, and shared practice.
