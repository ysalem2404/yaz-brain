# GRC Intelligence Orchestrator: A Tiered Workflow for AI Governance, Cyber, Risk, and Compliance

## Summary

Excellog.Biz's flowchart depicts a tiered GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance) process triggered by a regulatory, cyber, vendor, audit, or board signal. After GRC ownership is assigned, a 'GRC Intelligence Orchestrator' maps obligations, controls, and assurance methods, then runs four parallel workstreams: AI Governance (models, agents, human oversight), Cyber Resilience (NIST, NIS2, DORA alignment), Risk Intelligence (KRIs, scenarios, impact exposure), and Compliance Evidence (controls, records, audit trails). These feed 'Integrated GRC Findings,' which route to eight Tier 2 specialist domains for detail analysis (agentic AI risk, third-party risk, data governance, control monitoring, policy attestation, regulatory change, operational resilience, board reporting) before a gap analysis, senior analyst validation, and board approval loop produces a 'Validated GRC Action Plan.'

## Key Points

- Process starts with a regulatory/risk signal (AI, cyber, vendor, audit, or board trigger) and ends with a board-approved, validated GRC action plan
- Four parallel Tier 1 workstreams: AI Governance, Cyber Resilience (explicitly citing NIST, NIS2, DORA alignment), Risk Intelligence, Compliance Evidence
- Eight Tier 2 specialist domains include 'Agentic AI Risk' (tool use, drift, escalation) as its own dedicated domain, distinct from general AI governance
- Gap Analysis compares 'Current vs Required' state and feeds back into the orchestrator for continuous refinement
- Approval loop includes explicit rework/modification paths — board review can send findings back to the senior GRC analyst before final approval

A sophisticated end-to-end GRC workflow that treats agentic AI risk as a distinct specialist domain within a broader cyber/compliance program — useful architecture reference for Yaz when proposing how Bechtel's AI governance should integrate with existing cyber/DORA/NIST-aligned GRC processes.
