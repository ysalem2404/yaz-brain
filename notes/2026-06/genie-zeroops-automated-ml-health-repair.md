# Genie ZeroOps — Automated ML Health Repair

![Infographic](/yaz-brain/public/img/notes/2026-06/genie-zeroops-automated-ml-health-repair.png)

## Summary
Genie ZeroOps is presented as a health loop for ML systems: detect data drift, serving errors, and broken pipelines; diagnose causes; repair or roll back; and request approval before action.

## Key points
- Data drift can trigger retraining and revalidation
- Serving errors are isolated, traced upstream, and followed by rollback or restoration of serving
- Broken pipelines are repaired and their flow restored, with a human approval step shown at the end

## Why I saved this
A compact MLOps reference for separating detection, diagnosis, and repair while retaining an approval gate for production changes.
