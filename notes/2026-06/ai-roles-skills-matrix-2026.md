# AI Roles Skills Matrix 2026: Skills Priority by Role Across Modeling, Data, Deployment, and Governance

## Summary

A comprehensive skills matrix maps thirteen AI-related job roles — from Model Manager and ML Engineer to AI Risk & Governance Specialist, Head of AI, Agent Engineer, and AI Ethicist — against a detailed grid of technical and business skill areas. Skills are grouped into four categories: modeling & algorithms (Python, DL frameworks, GenAI/RAG/agent systems, AI evaluation), data & pipelines (SQL, vector databases, data wrangling), deployment & infrastructure (APIs, cloud platforms, CI/CD, observability), and business/security/governance (communication, AI security, AI ethics). Each cell is scored 0-3 for priority level per role, with the 2026 update noting increased emphasis on agents, evaluation, governance, and workflow integration.

## Key Points

- 13 roles mapped: Model Manager, ML Engineer, Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, AI Architect, AI Risk/Governance Specialist, Head of AI, AI Product Manager, UX Designer, D&A/AI Translator, Data Scientist, Agent Engineer, plus emerging roles
- Skills grouped into 4 areas: modeling & algorithms, data & pipelines, deployment & infrastructure, business/security/governance
- GenAI, RAG & Agent Systems is a critical skill (score 3) for AI Architects and Agent Engineers specifically
- AI Security, Permissions & Risk Controls scored as critical across AI Architect, Risk Specialist, Agent Engineer, and AI Ethicist roles
- 2026 update emphasizes agents, evaluation, governance, and workflow integration over prior years' focus areas
- Emerging roles highlighted separately: Model Validator, Prompt Engineer, Knowledge Engineer, AI Ethicist, Decision Engineer
