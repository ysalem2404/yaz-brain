# Major ISO/IEC Standards Every AI Leader Should Know

## Summary

This reference infographic summarizes six major ISO/IEC AI standards every AI leader should know: ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System — the world's first AIMS standard, covering AI-specific requirements, policies, lifecycle, and independent certification), ISO/IEC 24028+24368 (Trustworthy & Ethical AI — fairness, human rights, AI maturity model), ISO/IEC 23894 (AI Risk Management — identification, treatment, monitoring of AI risks), ISO/IEC 38507 (Corporate AI Governance — board/C-suite oversight, decision rights), ISO/IEC 5259 series (Data Quality for AI — quality dimensions, lineage, bias detection), and ISO/IEC 23053+22989 (ML System Framework — common language, lifecycle, traceability). It opens with the line: 'If your AI roadmap cannot be mapped to ISO/IEC, you do not have governance; you have experiments.'

## Key Points

- ISO/IEC 42001 named explicitly as 'the world's first standard for AI Management Systems (AIMS)' with independent assessment and certification available
- ISO/IEC 23894 (AI Risk Management) covers full lifecycle monitoring and residual risk management, anchoring model risk, safety, security, and documentation
- ISO/IEC 38507 (Corporate AI Governance) is specifically aimed at board and C-suite oversight — bridging technical AI teams and business leadership
- ISO/IEC 5259 series focuses on data quality dimensions, dataset documentation/lineage, and bias detection/mitigation as the data foundation for AI
- Six-step 'how to get started' sequence: map AI initiatives to standards, define governance roles, assess risks/data quality, implement controls, measure performance, continuously improve
- Closing framing: 'Good AI isn't just built — it's governed' with governance standards providing a global, vendor-neutral framework
