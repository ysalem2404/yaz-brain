# What Leadership Thinks AI Readiness Is vs. the 13-Layer Reality

## Summary

John Wernfeldt's two-column comparison contrasts leadership's simplistic mental model of AI readiness (a stack of just 'AI Model' on top of 'Data') against the real 13-layer stack required: AI Model, Clean Training Data, Agreed Definitions, Named Data Owners, Documented Lineage, Quality Rules Enforced, Validated Pipelines, Consistent Schemas, No Conflicting Sources, Regulatory Compliance, Access Controls, Audit Trails, and Governance Framework — with the caption 'The model is the easy part.'

## Key Points

- Leadership's mental model: two blocks, 'AI Model' stacked on 'Data' — deceptively simple
- Reality requires 13 distinct layers beneath the model, most of them data-governance and compliance work rather than technical model work
- Named Data Owners and Agreed Definitions are called out as distinct layers — governance clarity must precede pipeline validation
- Regulatory Compliance, Access Controls, Audit Trails, and Governance Framework occupy the bottom four layers as the true foundation
- Punchline: 'The model is the easy part' — reframes AI readiness as primarily a data and governance maturity problem
