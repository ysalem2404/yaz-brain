# 20 AI Use Cases for Finance: Prompts, Inputs, Formats, and Outcomes

## Summary

A 20-row reference table lists AI use cases for finance teams with a ready-to-use prompt, required inputs, expected output format, and outcome for each: financial statement analysis, forecasting/scenario planning, budget variance analysis, KPI dashboard creation, 13-week cash flow forecasting, working capital optimization, cost structure/breakeven analysis, DCF company valuation, board presentation drafting, investor reporting, PE due diligence checklists, covenant monitoring, operational efficiency benchmarking, sensitivity analysis, private equity investment memo drafting, expense trend detection, headcount/payroll forecasting, risk assessment matrices, and automated commentary generation.

## Key Points

- 20 finance use cases each paired with a copy-ready prompt template, required inputs, output format, and expected outcome — a practical prompt-engineering reference
- Covers the full finance function: FP&A (forecasting, variance analysis), valuation (DCF), investor relations (board decks, investor updates), and risk (covenant monitoring, risk matrices)
- DCF Company Valuation use case specifies exact prompt parameters: 5 years of projections, 10% WACC, 3% terminal growth
- 13-week cash flow forecasting use case is a specific, commonly-used treasury management cadence, distinct from generic 'forecasting'
- Due diligence checklist use case is scoped specifically to PE acquisitions of B2B SaaS firms as an example, illustrating how to adapt the general template

## Why Yaz Saved This

A ready-to-use prompt library for finance-adjacent AI tasks — useful for Yaz's own financial planning and quarterly tax/investment analysis work, and as an example of well-structured prompt engineering (explicit inputs/format/outcome) he can model for enterprise prompt libraries.
