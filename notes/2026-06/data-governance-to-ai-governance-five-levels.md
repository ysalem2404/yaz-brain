# How to Go From Data Governance to AI Governance: The 5 Levels Most Companies Never Progress Through

## Summary

A five-level maturity model tracks the progression from 'Data Only' governance to full 'AI Governance,' with each level scored across Policy, Risk, Roles, Compliance, and Data columns. Level 1 (Data Only) has basic data handling rules, no visibility into AI tool usage, and employees pasting sensitive data into public AI tools. Level 3 splits into 'Data Governed' (data stewards defined but no one owns AI oversight, GDPR-only compliance) and 'AI Extended' (AI coordinator bridges data team and leadership, gap analysis between GDPR and EU AI Act). Level 4 (AI Controlled) has embedded AI risk owners in business units and AI systems mapped to EU AI Act risk tiers. Level 5 (AI Governance) has an AI ethics board reviewing high-impact use cases and real-time data lineage from source to AI output.

## Key Points

- Five-level maturity progression: Data Only → Data Governed → AI Extended → AI Controlled → AI Governance
- Level 1 risk column explicitly states 'employees paste sensitive data into public AI tools' — a concrete, common real-world failure mode of ungoverned organizations
- Level 3 'AI Extended' introduces the AI Coordinator role bridging data team and leadership, plus formal gap analysis between GDPR and EU AI Act requirements
- Level 4 'AI Controlled' explicitly maps AI systems to EU AI Act risk tiers and embeds AI risk owners directly within business units, not just centrally
- Level 5 'AI Governance' requires an AI ethics board reviewing high-impact use cases and real-time (not periodic) data lineage from source to AI output
- Subtitle explicitly warns 'the 5 levels most companies never progress through' — framing this as an aspirational, rarely-achieved maturity ceiling
