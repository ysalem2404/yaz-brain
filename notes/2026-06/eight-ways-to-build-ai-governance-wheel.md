# 8 Ways to Build AI Governance: A Wheel of Data, Model, Risk, and Compliance Controls

## Summary

A wheel-diagram from Setu School organizes AI governance into eight spokes around a central robot icon: Data Governance, Model Governance, Responsible AI, Risk Management, Security & Privacy, Compliance & Regulation, Monitoring & Observability, and Audit & Accountability — each with five concrete sub-capabilities. It explicitly names NIST AI RMF alignment, ISO/IEC 42001 compliance, and EU AI Act/DPDP Act/GDPR/HIPAA regulatory tracking under Compliance & Regulation, and closes with six guiding principles: trust & transparency, human-centric design, fairness & inclusion, privacy by design, continuous improvement, and accountability.

## Key Points

- Eight governance spokes: Data Governance, Model Governance, Responsible AI, Risk Management, Security & Privacy, Compliance & Regulation, Monitoring & Observability, Audit & Accountability
- Compliance & Regulation spoke explicitly names EU AI Act, DPDP Act, GDPR, HIPAA, NIST AI RMF alignment, and ISO/IEC 42001 compliance as concrete standards to track
- Model Governance spoke covers model inventory/registry, version control, approval/promotion workflow, and model cards/datasheets — a full model lifecycle view
- Monitoring & Observability spoke includes hallucination detection for GenAI specifically, alongside drift detection and performance monitoring
- Six guiding principles closing panel: trust & transparency, human-centric, fairness & inclusion, privacy by design, continuous improvement, accountability

## Why Yaz Saved This

Directly names ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF — Yaz's core governance framework references — making this a useful cross-check against his existing ISO 42001 governance notes and a template for structuring Bechtel's AI governance wheel.
