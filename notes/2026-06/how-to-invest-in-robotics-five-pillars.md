# How to Invest in Robotics: The 5 Pillars of the Robotics Industry

## Summary

A companion investor infographic to the "What Is AI" pillar map breaks the robotics industry into five investable segments: robotics companies that build robots (Intuitive Surgical, ABB, Fanuc, Kuka, Teradyne), AI and robotics software that provides the "brains" (Nvidia, Palantir, UiPath, Autodesk, C3.ai), robotics hardware and components (Keyence, Nidec, SMC, Omron, Cognex), automation and integration leaders (Rockwell Automation, Siemens, Honeywell, Schneider Electric, Emerson), and robotics ETFs/funds for diversified exposure (BOTZ, ROBO, IRBO, ARKQ, KOIN).

## Key Points

- Robotics companies: Intuitive Surgical, ABB, Fanuc, Kuka, Teradyne build the physical robots
- AI & robotics software: Nvidia, Palantir, UiPath, Autodesk, C3.ai provide the intelligence layer
- Hardware & components: Keyence, Nidec, SMC, Omron, Cognex supply essential parts
- Automation & integration: Rockwell Automation, Siemens, Honeywell, Schneider Electric, Emerson deploy at industrial scale
- Robotics ETFs for diversified exposure: BOTZ, ROBO, IRBO, ARKQ, KOIN
- Framed as five distinct investable layers rather than a single robotics theme
