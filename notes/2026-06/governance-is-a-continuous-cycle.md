# Governance Is a Continuous Cycle

![Infographic](/img/notes/2026-06/governance-is-a-continuous-cycle.png)

## Summary
A governance cycle connects defining policies and standards, maintaining controls, monitoring change, detecting anomalies and risks, and responding with action and adoption.

## Key points
- Define sets policies and standards while Maintain keeps controls current
- Monitor tracks change over time and Detect spots anomalies and risks
- Respond turns findings into action and adoption, feeding the cycle continuously

## Why I saved this
A memorable operating model for governance as an active control loop rather than a static policy artifact.
