# 93% of Your AI Budget Goes to Technology, Only 7% Goes to People and Workflow

## Summary

Carolyn Healey's compilation synthesizes findings from Deloitte, McKinsey, Bain, BCG, Accenture, IBM, PwC, and Microsoft into eight reality-check points about enterprise AI spending. The core argument is that the real value gap is a people-and-workflow gap, not a technology gap, since technology accounts for only about 20% of value while workflow redesign drives the other 80%. It stresses that governance must be designed in from the start rather than bolted on later, and that platform alignment determines whether pilots scale, citing 2.2x revenue growth and 37% EBITDA lift when aligned. It also reframes capacity as more than headcount, noting 55% of workers in AI-forward organizations can absorb more work versus 25% globally, and flags that only 5% of companies are structurally "future-built" for AI.

## Key Points

- 93% of AI budget dollars go to technology; only 7% goes to people and how work flows
- Real value gap is a people-workflow gap, not a technology gap
- Technology is only ~20% of value; workflow redesign is the other 80%
- Governance must be designed in from the start, not bolted on after deployment
- Platform alignment determines whether pilots scale — 2.2x revenue growth, 37% EBITDA lift when aligned
- Capacity is no longer just headcount: 55% of workers in AI-forward orgs can take on more work vs 25% globally
- Only 5% of companies are structurally "future-built" for AI at scale
