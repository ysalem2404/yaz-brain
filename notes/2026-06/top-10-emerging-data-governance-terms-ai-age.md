# Top 10 Emerging Data Governance Terms in the AI Age

## Summary

A reference glossary of 10 emerging data governance terms specific to the AI era: Context Governance (managing business context fed to AI — definitions, rules, approved sources, prompts, usage boundaries), Semantic Metadata Management (enriching data with business meaning AI can understand), AI-Ready Data (trusted, governed, high-quality data safe for AI use), Retrieval Governance (controlling which sources AI can retrieve from and how results are ranked), Data Provenance (tracking where data came from and how AI used it), AI Data Lineage (showing how data flows through AI pipelines/models/prompts/outputs), Policy-as-Code (turning governance rules into automated enforceable controls), Machine-Readable Policies (structured policy formats AI systems can interpret), Memory Governance (managing what AI can remember/retain/forget to protect sensitive data), and Knowledge Graph Governance (managing quality/ownership/access of knowledge graphs AI uses).

## Key Points

- 10 terms specifically coined for the 'AI age' rather than traditional data governance vocabulary
- Context Governance explicitly covers prompts and usage boundaries as governance objects — a newer concept than traditional data governance scope
- Memory Governance is a distinctly AI-era concept: managing what AI systems can remember, retain, reuse, forget, or share to protect sensitive data and ensure compliance
- Retrieval Governance directly addresses RAG-specific concerns: which sources are trusted, how results are ranked, what content should be restricted
- Policy-as-Code and Machine-Readable Policies are paired terms describing the shift toward automated, system-enforceable governance rather than manual policy documents
