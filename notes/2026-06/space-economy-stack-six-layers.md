# The Space Economy Stack: Launch, Satellites, Connectivity, Data, and Defense

## Summary

The first of a six-part series mapping the space economy stack across six layers: launch and access (SpaceX, Rocket Lab, Blue Origin, Arianespace), spacecraft and satellite manufacturing (MDA Space, Airbus, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Redwire), connectivity constellations (Starlink, Iridium, Globalstar, AST SpaceMobile, SES, Eutelsat OneWeb), Earth observation and geospatial data (Planet, BlackSky, Spire, Maxar, ICEYE), ground infrastructure and networks (Viasat, Gilat, Kratos, Comtech, KSAT), and cloud/defense/applications (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Palantir, RTX, L3Harris). The key idea is that space is not one company but a stack of launch, hardware, bandwidth, data, and mission-critical services.

## Key Points

- Layer 1 - Launch & Access: SpaceX, Rocket Lab, Blue Origin, Arianespace get payloads to orbit
- Layer 2 - Spacecraft & Satellite Manufacturing: MDA Space, Airbus, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Redwire
- Layer 3 - Connectivity Constellations: Starlink, Iridium, Globalstar, AST SpaceMobile, SES, Eutelsat OneWeb
- Layer 4 - Earth Observation & Geospatial Data: Planet, BlackSky, Spire, Maxar, ICEYE
- Layer 5 - Ground Infrastructure & Networks: Viasat, Gilat, Kratos, Comtech, KSAT
- Layer 6 - Cloud, Defense & Applications: AWS, Microsoft Azure, Palantir, RTX, L3Harris turn space assets into mission value
- Key idea: space economy is a full stack, not a single company or theme
