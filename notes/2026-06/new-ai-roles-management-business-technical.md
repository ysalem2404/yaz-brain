# New AI Roles: A Map of Emerging AI Job Titles Across Management, Business, and Technical Tracks

## Summary

AIForLeaders.com's role map catalogs emerging AI job titles across three tracks. Management: Chief AI Officer, Head of AI, Head of Applied AI, AI Ethicist, AI Delivery Manager, AI Strategy Manager, Director of AI, Chief Data and AI Officer, AI Product Manager, AI Risk & Governance Specialist, Responsible AI Lead, AI Operations Lead, Director of AI Transformation and Strategy, AI Policy & Advocacy Lead. Business: AI Accounting Analyst, AI-Powered Auditor, AI Tax Consultant, AI Financial Reporting Specialist, AI Compliance Analyst, AI Payroll Specialist, AI Accounts Payable/Receivable Specialist, AI Talent Acquisition Specialist, AI HR Data Analyst, AI HR Business Partner, AI People Analytics Manager, AI Customer Success Manager, AI Business Intelligence Analyst. Technical: AI Architect, Model Manager, Model Validator, Model/ML Engineer, AI Application Developer, AI App Dev Platform Architect, Data Scientist, Analytics Engineer, AI Cybersecurity Analyst/Researcher, AI Redteam Engineer, Prompt Engineer, AI Process Automation Engineer, Data Engineer, D&A and AI UX Designer.

## Key Points

- Three-track taxonomy of 40+ named AI job titles: Management (14 roles), Business (13 roles), Technical (14 roles)
- AI Risk & Governance Specialist and Responsible AI Lead appear as distinct named management roles, reflecting AI governance maturing into a dedicated career track
- Business track heavily represents finance/accounting-adjacent roles: AI Accounting Analyst, AI-Powered Auditor, AI Tax Consultant, AI Financial Reporting Specialist, AI Compliance Analyst, AI Payroll Specialist
- Technical track includes AI Redteam Engineer and Prompt Engineer as distinct, established roles alongside more traditional titles like Data Engineer and ML Engineer
- Positioned as a reference for org design and job description writing as companies formalize AI-related roles
