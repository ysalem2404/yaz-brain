# How to Explain AI Types: Predictive, Generative, Agentic, and Physical AI

## Summary

AI For Leaders' framework categorizes AI systems into four types to help leaders pick the right tool for the job: Predictive AI (predicts/classifies outcomes for smarter decisions and personalization), Generative AI (produces new content — text, code, images — from prompts, including RAG-based knowledge systems), Agentic AI (acts autonomously toward goals via planning, execution, and adaptation), and Physical AI (operates in the real world through robotics and embodied systems). The central message is that most teams don't need more AI — they need the right type of AI for the job, closing with a decision flow: need to decide → predictive; need to create → generative; need to execute → agentic or physical.

## Key Points

- Predictive AI: predicts/classifies outcomes using rules or trained models — for smarter decisions and personalization
- Generative AI: produces new content from prompts — content generation, workflow automation, RAG knowledge systems
- Agentic AI: acts autonomously toward goals via plan-execute-adapt loops — for multi-step tasks and orchestration
- Physical AI: operates in the real world via sensors/robotics — autonomous systems, spatial intelligence
- Central thesis: most teams need the right type of AI, not simply "more AI"
- Decision framework: decide→Predictive, create→Generative, execute→Agentic/Physical
