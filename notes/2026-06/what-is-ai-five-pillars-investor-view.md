# What Is AI? The 5 Pillars of Artificial Intelligence (Data, Chips, Data Centers, Models, Applications)

## Summary

An investor-oriented infographic from the mikebuildsmoney account breaks artificial intelligence down into five commercial pillars: data (the fuel, exemplified by YouTube, Google, TikTok, Netflix, Spotify), chips (the compute brains, led by Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, TSMC, Micron), data centers (where AI runs, hosted by AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, Oracle, Dell), AI models (the software "thinking," including ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Llama), and applications (how people use AI daily, via Adobe, Salesforce, Palantir, UiPath, Duolingo). Each pillar is mapped to public-market tickers, framing AI as a full investable ecosystem rather than a single technology.

## Key Points

- Data pillar: YouTube/Google, TikTok/Meta, Netflix, Spotify as AI training fuel sources
- Chips pillar: Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, TSMC, Micron power AI compute
- Data centers pillar: AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, Dell run AI at scale
- AI models pillar: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Llama are the "thinking" software layer
- Applications pillar: Adobe, Salesforce, Palantir, UiPath, Duolingo bring AI to daily use
- Each company mapped to its public ticker for investment context
