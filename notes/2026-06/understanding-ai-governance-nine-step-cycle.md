# Understanding AI Governance: The 9-Step Cycle From Guidelines to Reliable Systems

## Summary

Rushikesh Meharwade's circular nine-step diagram sequences AI governance from usage guidelines through threat/impact analysis, data management systems, regulatory alignment, system observability, model record keeping, responsibility frameworks, failure management, to the outcome of reliable AI systems. Each step lists three concrete sub-actions, e.g., threat & impact analysis includes identifying business-critical risks and measuring potential user impact; failure management includes detecting/responding to issues quickly and implementing rollback strategies.

## Key Points

- Nine-step cycle: usage guidelines → threat/impact analysis → data management → regulatory alignment → system observability → model record keeping → responsibility framework → failure management → reliable AI systems outcome
- Step 1 (usage guidelines) explicitly restricts high-risk automation areas and standardizes AI adoption practices before any deployment
- System observability step covers real-time health monitoring, anomaly tracking, and usage/performance trend analysis
- Responsibility framework step requires clear ownership roles, approval workflows, and accountability in decisions — not just documentation
- Failure management closes the loop with rollback strategies and continuous improvement of recovery plans
- Final outcome framed as consistent, accurate, scalable performance that builds long-term user trust
