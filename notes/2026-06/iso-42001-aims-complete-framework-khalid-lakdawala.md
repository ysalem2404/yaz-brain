# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System (AIMS): Complete Framework for Responsible AI Governance

## Summary

Khalid Lakdawala's detailed reference explains ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the world's first international standard for AI Management Systems, covering the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) framework, nine core components (context of the organization, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement, AI system lifecycle management, AI risk management), the seven-stage AI system lifecycle in scope, a six-step risk management process, typical use cases (chatbots, AI-enabled decision support, HR/finance/operations), certification benefits, and a seven-step certification journey from gap analysis to surveillance audits. It also lists eight AIMS principles: fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy/data protection, safety/security, human oversight, robustness/reliability, and societal/environmental well-being.

## Key Points

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the world's first certifiable international standard for AI Management Systems
- PDCA framework: Plan (establish AIMS), Do (implement/operate), Check (monitor/review), Act (improve)
- 9 core components including AI system lifecycle management and AI risk management
- 7-stage AI system lifecycle: plan/design, develop/build, test/validate, deploy, operate/monitor, maintain/improve, retire/decommission
- 6-step risk management process: establish context, identify, analyze, evaluate, treat, monitor & review risks
- 8 principles addressed: fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy/data protection, safety/security, human oversight, robustness/reliability, societal well-being
- 7-step certification journey: initiate, assess, plan, implement, evaluate, improve, certify
