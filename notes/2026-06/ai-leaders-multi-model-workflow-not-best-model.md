# AI Leaders Don't Pick the 'Best' Model — They Build the Right Model Into the Right Workflow

## Summary

Carolyn Healey's comparison argues ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot are not interchangeable, each having different strengths, failure modes, and organizational roles: ChatGPT for breadth/adaptability/innovation velocity (with governance/consistency struggles at scale); Claude for precision, reasoning depth, and regulated environments (with less breadth across general workflows); Gemini for multimodal workflows inside Google ecosystems (value drops outside that ecosystem); Microsoft Copilot for embedded productivity at scale inside Word/Excel/Outlook/Teams grounded in Microsoft Graph data (struggles with user preference vs. standalone tools). Gartner (2026) and IDC (2026) are cited confirming multi-model enterprise environments are becoming the norm, with most enterprises already running AI in production in some form.

## Key Points

- ChatGPT: best for breadth, adaptability, innovation velocity; struggles with governance/consistency in large orgs
- Claude: best for precision, reasoning depth, regulated environments (legal, compliance, financial workflows); less breadth across general use
- Gemini: best for multimodal workflows inside Google ecosystems (Docs, Sheets, Gmail, Drive); value drops sharply outside that ecosystem
- Microsoft Copilot: best for embedded productivity at scale, grounded via Microsoft Graph, built for enterprise compliance; struggle is standalone-tool preference among users
- Gartner (2026) and IDC (2026) cited: multi-model enterprise environments are becoming the norm; majority of enterprises already have AI in production
- Leadership recommendation: assign models to specific workflows, build governance around usage (not just access), and treat AI as a portfolio, not a single platform
