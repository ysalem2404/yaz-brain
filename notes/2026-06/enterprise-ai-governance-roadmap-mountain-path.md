# Enterprise AI Governance Roadmap: The Mountain Path to Trusted, Scalable AI

## Summary

aidigital.co.uk's mountain-climb metaphor lays out an enterprise AI governance roadmap across four layers climbing toward a 'Peak State' of trusted enterprise AI: the Foundation Layer (identity & access control, human oversight, data protection, usage monitoring, policy foundations, audit logs), the Operational Governance Layer (real-time monitoring, governance workflows, auditability, change controls, reporting/accountability, cross-functional ownership), the Risk Management Layer (risk classification, compliance mapping, model risk management, bias/fairness checks, data usage controls, incident readiness), the Control Layer (prompt/input security, output validation, model access boundaries, workflow permission, escalation paths, approval rules), and the Advanced Assurance Layer (governance automation, continuous assurance, adaptive guardrails, AI incident response, enterprise trust layer, scalable control model), culminating in board-level confidence and governed AI growth.

## Key Points

- Five-layer climb: Foundation → Operational Governance → Risk Management → Control → Advanced Assurance → Peak State (trusted, scalable enterprise AI)
- Foundation layer requires identity & access control, human oversight, data protection, and audit logs before any operational governance is possible
- Control layer specifically addresses prompt/input security and output validation — direct defenses against prompt injection and unsafe outputs
- Advanced Assurance layer introduces 'adaptive guardrails' that update restrictions based on new threats or failures, plus governance automation for policy checks
- Peak state explicitly defined as AI being 'governed, monitored, explainable, and aligned with business trust' with board-level visibility into risk and control
