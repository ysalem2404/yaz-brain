# The AI Transformation 7P Model: Seven Pillars of Successful Enterprise AI Transformation

## Summary

Prem Natarajan's 7P Model names the seven pillars of successful enterprise AI transformation arranged around a central hub: Purpose (why AI is needed and the business value it creates), People (leaders, employees, specialists responsible for adoption), Processes (workflows AI will improve, automate, or redesign), Platforms (models, data, infrastructure, APIs, tools needed to deploy at scale), Policies (rules for AI security, compliance, data access, risk, human oversight), Portfolio (AI initiatives prioritized by value, feasibility, risk, strategic alignment), and Performance (metrics tracking adoption, reliability, cost, and business impact). Six actionable implementation steps follow: start with business purpose, assess people and skills, redesign processes first, build a scalable platform, govern the AI portfolio, and review performance regularly.

## Key Points

- Seven pillars: Purpose, People, Processes, Platforms, Policies, Portfolio, Performance — each with a one-line definition
- Six actionable steps map 1:1 to six of the seven pillars, starting explicitly with business purpose before any technical work
- Step 3 ('Redesign Processes First: improve the workflow before adding AI or automation') echoes the workflow-before-tooling theme seen across multiple other governance infographics
- Portfolio pillar explicitly calls for governing the AI portfolio by prioritizing valuable use cases and stopping weak initiatives early
- Performance pillar ties metrics to adoption, reliability, cost, risk, and business results — not adoption alone
