# Jensen Huang's Five-Layer AI Cake: Energy, Chips, Infrastructure, Models, Applications

## Summary

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's five-layer stack maps the AI industry from first principles as a cake: Energy → Chips → Infrastructure → Models → Applications. The Applications layer includes chatbots, digital biology, robotaxis, enterprise AI agents, science, robotics, manufacturing, and AI coders. The Models layer spans LLM, VLM, VLA, MMLLM, GPT, DM, GNN, MOE, SSM, LBM architectures. Infrastructure, Chips, and Energy form the foundational base, collectively producing what Huang calls 'AI Factories' — large-scale server infrastructure shown as racks of compute hardware.

## Key Points

- Five-layer stack framed as a literal cake metaphor: Applications (top, thinnest layer) down to Energy (bottom, foundational layer)
- Applications layer names eight concrete use case categories: chatbots, digital biology, robotaxis, enterprise AI agents, science, robotics, manufacturing, AI coder
- Models layer lists ten named model architecture types: LLM, VLM, VLA, MMLLM, GPT, DM, GNN, MOE, SSM, LBM — a broader model taxonomy than most infographics in this collection
- 'AI Factories' is the term Huang uses for the physical infrastructure that instantiates the bottom three layers (Energy, Chips, Infrastructure) at scale
- Reinforces the idea that AI capability is fundamentally bottlenecked by physical infrastructure (energy and chips) before software/model considerations
