## Summary
A Databricks data-platform diagram links 100+ connectors and enterprise sources to ingestion, data preparation, real-time operations, agents, dashboards, SQL, Python, and analytics consumers.

## Key points
- The source side spans enterprise apps, databases, unstructured data, cloud storage, SaaS, cloud APIs, and message queues
- The platform layer combines no-code preparation, ZeroBus Ingest, Spark Declarative Pipelines, batch and streaming, and observability
- Consumers include agents, dashboards, SQL, Python, real-time applications, and analytics
