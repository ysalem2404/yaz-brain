# How Nvidia's AI Supply Chain Fits Together: 8-Layer Ecosystem Map

## Summary

A companion piece to the geographic supply chain map, this infographic sequences Nvidia's AI ecosystem as eight vertically stacked functional layers: chip design and EDA tools (ARM, Synopsys, Cadence, Ansys), manufacturing and equipment (TSMC, Samsung, Intel, ASML, Applied Materials), HBM memory (SK hynix, Samsung, Micron), advanced packaging (TSMC CoWoS, Amkor, ASE, SPIL), systems and server partners (Foxconn, Wistron, Inventec, Quanta), high-speed connectivity and optics (Broadcom, Marvell, Nvidia networking/Mellanox, Arista, Corning), power/cooling/electrical (Vertiv, Delta Electronics, Emerson, nVent, CoolIT Systems), and cloud/demand pull (Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud, Meta, Cloudflare) — summarized as Nvidia orchestrating this ecosystem end-to-end from silicon to systems to scale.

## Key Points

- Layer 1 - Chip Design & EDA: ARM, Synopsys, Cadence, Ansys design and verify GPU IP
- Layer 2 - Manufacturing & Equipment: TSMC, Samsung, Intel, ASML, Applied Materials build the chips
- Layer 3 - HBM Memory: SK hynix, Samsung, Micron supply high-bandwidth memory for AI performance
- Layer 4 - Advanced Packaging: TSMC CoWoS, Amkor, ASE, SPIL connect chips and memory at speed
- Layer 5 - Systems & Server Partners: Foxconn, Wistron, Inventec, Quanta build servers at scale
- Layer 6 - High-Speed Connectivity & Optics: Broadcom, Marvell, Nvidia networking, Arista, Corning move data at speed
- Layer 7 - Power, Cooling & Electrical: Vertiv, Delta, Emerson, nVent, CoolIT keep infrastructure reliable
- Layer 8 - Cloud & Demand Pull: Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Oracle, Meta, Cloudflare drive AI compute demand
