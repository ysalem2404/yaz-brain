# Nvidia's Global Supply Chain: Design, Manufacturing, Memory, and Infrastructure

## Summary

Part 2 of the stockswithpawan series maps Nvidia's global AI supply chain across five countries and four functional layers. The United States hosts design and software (Nvidia, Applied Materials, Broadcom, Arista, Vertiv, Corning) alongside major infrastructure demand from Microsoft, Meta, Oracle, and Cloudflare; the Netherlands (ASML) and Japan (SUMCO) supply manufacturing equipment and materials; South Korea (SK hynix, Samsung) supplies memory and packaging; Germany (Linde) supplies industrial gases; and Taiwan (TSMC, ASE, SPIL, Quanta, Wistron, Foxconn, Inventec) handles fabrication, packaging, and final assembly at scale — illustrating how the AI ecosystem spans multiple countries and geopolitical chokepoints.

## Key Points

- US layer: Nvidia (design), Applied Materials, Broadcom, Arista, Vertiv, Corning plus Microsoft/Meta/Oracle/Cloudflare as demand drivers
- Netherlands: ASML supplies critical lithography equipment
- Japan: SUMCO supplies silicon wafer materials
- South Korea: SK hynix and Samsung supply memory (HBM) and packaging
- Germany: Linde supplies industrial gases for fabrication
- Taiwan: TSMC, ASE, SPIL, Quanta, Wistron, Foxconn, Inventec handle fab, packaging, and assembly
- Four functional layers: design & software, manufacturing & equipment, memory & packaging, infrastructure & demand
