# How AI Consulting Agents Work: From Disconnected Reports to a Plug-In Chief AI Transformation Officer

## Summary

Doczen's illustrated case study frames the problem of disconnected reports, misaligned meeting rooms, siloed process knowledge, and consultants presenting massive invoices — then proposes Doczen as a plug-in 'Chief AI Transformation Officer' agent. The workflow has three stages: Discovery (AI listens to the entire organization through natural conversations and real-talk surveys so employees are finally heard), Planning (chaos becomes a prioritized AI roadmap as insights flow into a structured plan), and Resolution (roadmap becomes real execution — building, selecting vendors, shipping).

## Key Points

- Problem framing uses four illustrated vignettes: stressed executive with disconnected reports, misaligned meeting room, siloed employee knowledge, consultant with a massive invoice
- Doczen is positioned specifically as a 'plug-in Chief AI Transformation Officer' — an AI agent replacing/augmenting traditional management consulting for AI roadmap-building
- Three-stage workflow: Discovery (AI listens via conversations/surveys) → Planning (insights become prioritized roadmap) → Resolution (roadmap becomes execution with vendor selection and shipping)
- Directly targets a pain point around traditional consulting cost and slow discovery cycles by using an AI agent to accelerate organizational diagnosis
- No specific metrics or client case studies shown — this is a product/vendor pitch rather than a data-driven case study

An example of AI-agent-as-consultant positioning that Yaz can reference when evaluating vendor pitches or considering how Bechtel's own AI initiatives might use agents for organizational discovery rather than traditional consulting engagements.
