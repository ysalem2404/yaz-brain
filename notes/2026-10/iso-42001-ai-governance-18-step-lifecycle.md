# ISO 42001 AI Governance: 18-Step Lifecycle to Full Compliance

![ISO 42001 AI governance: 18-step lifecycle infographic](/img/notes/2026-10/iso-42001-ai-governance-18-step-lifecycle.png)

**TL;DR**
- The infographic organizes an AI management-system program into foundation and planning, risk and controls, and execution and audit.
- Its 18 items cover leadership, scope, policy, risk and impact assessments, control selection and implementation, workforce capability, monitoring, audit, review, and corrective action.
- This is a visual implementation sequence, not a certification guarantee; applicability and evidence need to be assessed against the standard and the organization’s context.
