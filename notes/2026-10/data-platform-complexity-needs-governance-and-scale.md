## Summary

A platform architecture slide maps sources and consumers through connectors and ETL, using CI/CD, governance, and scale as the capabilities needed to manage growing complexity.

## Key points

- Sources include databases, unstructured data, and cloud storage feeding a data lake.
- The pipeline layer includes connectors, CDC tools, homegrown ETL, and services such as Flink, Spark, dbt, Airflow, and AWS Glue.
- Data consumers include real-time operations, dashboards, and analytics.
- The slide visually calls out CI/CD, governance, and scale as the hard-to-solve needs in a complex platform.
