![Databricks Agent-Ready ML Lifecycle](/img/notes/2026-10/databricks-agent-ready-ml-lifecycle.png)

## Summary

A photographed platform slide describes a closed, governed ML lifecycle as the foundation for making the Databricks stack agent-ready.

## Key points

- The lifecycle is drawn as a loop: data, features, training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring.
- The platform map associates lifecycle layers with services including Lakehouse/Lakebase, Feature Store/Feature Serving, AI Runtime/ML Runtime, MLflow/Notebooks, Model Serving/Model Registry, and Inference Tables/Data Quality Monitoring.
- The slide's positioning is serverless, unified, closed-loop data operations with governance by default.
- These are the slide's product labels and claims; current service availability should be checked in live Databricks documentation.
