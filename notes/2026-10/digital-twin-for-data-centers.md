# Digital Twin for Data Centers

**TL;DR**
- Combines real-time infrastructure data, AI analytics, and a digital twin to inform facility decisions.
- Highlights energy and cooling optimization, predictive maintenance, risk reduction, capacity optimization, and more disciplined capital expenditure.
- Frames the outcome as delivering more usable IT capacity per dollar invested.

## Content
The graphic contrasts a real data center with a digital representation. Real-time data, AI analytics, and a digital twin connect the two.
The digital model highlights power, cooling, assets, operations, and capacity as monitored areas.
Presented applications are energy optimization (lower consumption and operating cost), cooling optimization (thermal performance and cooling cost), predictive maintenance (fewer failures and longer asset life), risk reduction (simulate failures and improve resilience), capacity optimization (more usable megawatts from existing infrastructure), and CAPEX optimization (build only what is required and defer investments).
