![Legacy SIEM: Fragmented Telemetry and Cost](/img/notes/2026-10/legacy-siem-fragmented-telemetry-cost-security.png)

## Summary

A photographed security slide argues that the scale and pricing of legacy solutions can weaken security by funneling many attack surfaces into an expensive SIEM.

## Key points

- The diagram lists email, network/firewall, identity/access, SaaS apps, cloud/IaaS, DNS, and EDR/XDR as attacker-facing surfaces.
- Those sources converge on a SIEM, while the slide says defenders can see only what they can afford to inspect.
- The central claim is that legacy solution scale and pricing can leave security coverage incomplete; the slide does not provide cost figures or comparative evidence.
