## Summary

A photographed companion diagram shows Genie Ontology and its permission-aware retrieval layer serving both Databricks surfaces and external agent/client applications.

## Key points

- The diagram connects Genie, Genie One, agents, and code on one side with an “Any Agent/Client” group on the other.
- Both paths lead through Genie Ontology, where OntoRank is described as a permissions-aware algorithm for inferring relevant knowledge snippets.
- The lower layer combines modeled semantics from Unity Catalog with learned knowledge sources.
- This is a different photographed view of the ontology architecture, emphasizing how clients connect rather than only the semantic inputs.
