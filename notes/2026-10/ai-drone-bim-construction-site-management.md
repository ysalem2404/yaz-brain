# AI, Drones, and BIM for Construction Site Management

**TL;DR**
- AI analyzes site imagery, drawings, reports, and schedules; drones capture imagery/video and map progress; BIM supplies the digital model and project information.
- Use cases include progress tracking, quantity takeoff/BOQ, site layout and surveying, quality inspection, safety management, and planning/cost support.
- AI outputs can be incomplete or incorrect; validate them against approved drawings, site conditions, and engineering experience.

## Content
The infographic combines AI, drone capture, and Building Information Modeling (BIM) as an integrated workflow for smarter planning, monitoring, and site management.
AI analyzes site data such as images, drawings, reports, and schedules, and identifies patterns or provides insights. Drones capture site imagery and video and map progress. BIM integrates design, quantity, and site data in a digital model for planning, analysis, and visualization.
Use cases shown: (1) site monitoring and progress tracking; (2) quantity takeoff and bill of quantities (BOQ); (3) site layout and survey; (4) quality inspection; (5) safety management; and (6) planning, cost, and decision support.
The image names ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, BIM software (including Revit and Navisworks), and drone/mapping software such as DJI, Pix4D, and DroneDeploy.
Limitations include inaccurate or incomplete AI information, dependence on input-data quality, limited access to project-specific data, and the need for human expertise. The graphic advises verification against approved drawings, specifications, and site conditions.
