## Summary

A photographed Databricks Governance Hub slide presents a central workflow for understanding access, finding governance gaps, and prioritizing cleanup and optimization.

## Key points

- The hub is positioned as one place to understand what each user can access, what they can do, and how access was granted.
- It surfaces missing metadata and classification, policy-coverage gaps, and risky or unused access.
- The proposed response is to prioritize cleanup, optimization, and other governance actions.
- The slide labels Governance Hub as “Beta coming soon.”
