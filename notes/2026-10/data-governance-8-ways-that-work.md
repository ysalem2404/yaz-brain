# 8 Ways to Build Data Governance That Actually Works

![8 Ways to Build Data Governance That Actually Works](/img/notes/2026-10/data-governance-8-ways-that-work.png)

## Summary

An eight-part data-governance map connects ownership and stewardship with data foundations, quality, master/reference data, security, standards, operations, and compliance evidence.

## Key points

- Governance and ownership: executive sponsorship, governance council, stewardship model, roles/RACI, and domain ownership.
- Data foundation and quality: glossary, metadata, catalog, lineage, classification, profiling, rules, issue management, freshness, and remediation.
- Master and reference data: matching/merging, golden records, and hierarchy management.
- Security and privacy: access control, PII protection, consent and retention, secure storage, and encryption.
- Policies and operations: definitions, naming, lifecycle rules, documentation, steward workflows, escalation, change management, training, and issue resolution.
- Compliance and audit: regulatory mapping, audit trails, control testing, exception tracking, and reporting.
