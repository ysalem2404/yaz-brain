# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Govern AI Responsibly, Build a Trusted Tomorrow

![ISO/IEC 42001:2023 overview](/img/notes/2026-10/iso-42001-govern-ai-responsibly-trusted-tomorrow.png)

## Summary

A visual overview of ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and the Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). It frames the standard as a risk-based, lifecycle-wide management approach, highlights the management-system cycle, and summarizes organizational benefits such as clearer accountability, risk management, regulatory readiness, and sustainable innovation.

## Key points

- The poster describes ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as an international standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AIMS.
- Its lifecycle wheel connects organizational context, leadership and commitment, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement.
- The visual presents the standard as applicable across organization sizes and industries, including public-sector and nonprofit settings.
- It links adoption to governance expertise, risk management, responsible AI, a broader view of people and process, and readiness for evolving expectations.
- The benefits panel emphasizes trust, management of risks and opportunities, efficiency, regulatory readiness, and sustainable innovation.

## OCR text

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Govern AI Responsibly. Build a Trusted Tomorrow. The world’s first international standard for AI Management Systems (AIMS). A structured approach to manage AI risks and opportunities, enabling innovation with trust, transparency, and responsibility. What does it cover? The entire AI lifecycle with a risk-based approach: context of the organization; leadership and commitment; planning; support; operation; performance evaluation; improvement. Why adopt it? Build expertise in AI governance; strengthen risk management; advance responsible AI; take a holistic view of people, process, risk, and impact; stay future-ready. Key benefits: build trust; manage risks and opportunities; drive efficiency; regulatory readiness; enable sustainable innovation.
