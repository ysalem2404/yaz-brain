## Summary

A platform-layer slide organizes Databricks capabilities around marketplace integrations, agentic development/work, unified governance, agentic data services, and open infrastructure.

## Key points

- Marketplace integrations are presented across sales, support, engineering, operations, IT, finance, security, and marketing.
- The agentic development/work layer includes Genie Ontology, Genie, and Agent Bricks.
- The unified-governance layer shows Unity Catalog and Unity AI Gateway.
- The agentic-data layer groups Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Lakebase; the open-infrastructure band names Delta Lake, Iceberg, and choice of cloud, model, and data.
