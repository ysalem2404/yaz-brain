# AI Impact Assessment: Five-Step Roadmap

**TL;DR**
- Define the AI system's purpose, intended audience, and data inputs before assessing impacts.
- Identify technical, ethical, and legal risks; score likelihood and severity; prioritize controls proportionately.
- Document oversight, data lineage, access controls, monitoring thresholds, decisions, and reassessments as systems change.

## Content
The graphic presents an AI Impact Assessment (AIIA) as a practical roadmap associated with ISO 42001 and EU AI Act compliance. It is organized into five steps:
1. Define scope and intentionality: document the system purpose and target audience, clarify the core business purpose, identify intended users, and list data inputs and types.
2. Identify risks and negative impacts: assess technical, ethical, and legal risks, including data drift, hallucinations, algorithmic bias, discrimination, privacy, and regulatory non-compliance.
3. Evaluate risk exposure: score likelihood and severity, assign quantitative or qualitative ratings, categorize exposure (low, medium, or critical), and prioritize resources.
4. Implement mitigation controls: establish guardrails and accountability, provide human-in-the-loop oversight, maintain data lineage and access controls, and define performance-monitoring thresholds.
5. Document and continuously review: maintain an audit-ready AIIA record, document decisions and mitigations, and revisit the assessment when the model or system changes or is retrained.
