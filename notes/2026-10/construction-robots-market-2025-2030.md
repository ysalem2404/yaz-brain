# Construction Robots Market: 2025–2030 Snapshot

![Construction Robots Market: 2025–2030 Snapshot](/img/notes/2026-10/construction-robots-market-2025-2030.png)

## Summary

A market infographic estimates construction robotics at USD 1.29B in 2025 and USD 3.66B by 2030, with a displayed 18% CAGR, and maps companies to construction tasks and enabling technologies.

## Key points

- The graphic displays a USD 1.29B market size for 2025 and USD 3.66B for 2030, with an 18.0% CAGR; these are the infographic's estimates, not independently verified figures.
- Use cases include demolition, bricklaying and masonry, 3D concrete printing, material handling, surveying/layout, drilling, assembly, and autonomous earthmoving.
- Named supplier examples include Built Robotics, ABB, Boston Dynamics, Caterpillar, Husqvarna, KUKA, Komatsu, Liebherr, FANUC, Terex, Hilti, JLG, Mitsubishi Electric, and Yaskawa.
- The technology strip names autonomous mobile robots, machine vision, AI/ML, exoskeletons, LiDAR, and digital platforms.
