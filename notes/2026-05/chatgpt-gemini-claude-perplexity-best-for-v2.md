# ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude vs Perplexity: Best-For Comparison (V2)

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/chatgpt-gemini-claude-perplexity-best-for-v2.png)

## Summary
A four-way positioning comparison of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity by 'best for' use case. ChatGPT: 'every day, creative thinking, strategic problem-solving, fast synthesis' with broad versatility and a large plugin ecosystem — tip: 'upload frameworks, notes, or brand docs to train it on your system.' Gemini: 'AI that integrates seamlessly into your daily Google-powered workflow' across Gmail/Docs/Sheets/Slides — tip: 'use Gemini for real-time analysis in Sheets.' Claude: 'deep reasoning, nuanced writing, and complex synthesis,' processing 100k+ tokens without losing accuracy, strong ethical/factual guardrails — tip: 'use Claude to simplify complexity, it's precise, not performative.' Perplexity: 'credible, fact-checked research and deep topic exploration' with real-time cited search — tip: 'use Perplexity to audit ChatGPT outputs, it's your accuracy layer.'

## Key points
- Frames Perplexity explicitly as an 'accuracy layer' to audit ChatGPT outputs rather than a standalone replacement
- Claude is positioned for defensibility — 'strong ethical and factual guardrails' for policy/legal/research work
- Gemini's edge is Workspace-native integration, not raw model capability
- ChatGPT's strength framed as versatility and plugin/ecosystem breadth, not any single specialization
- Each tool gets one practical 'tip' for daily workflow use, not just an abstract description
