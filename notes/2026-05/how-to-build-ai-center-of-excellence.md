# How to Build an AI Center of Excellence That Actually Works

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/how-to-build-ai-center-of-excellence.png)

## Summary
This infographic defines an AI Center of Excellence (CoE) as "the engine that drives enterprise-wide AI adoption and real business value," with the stated goal to "accelerate AI value creation through governance, expertise, tools, and adoption." It lays out 7 building blocks: define purpose and scope, secure executive sponsorship, build the right (cross-functional) team, build the foundation (strategy, roadmap, standards, governance), launch high-impact use cases, scale and industrialize (standardized deployment, reusable components, self-service), and drive adoption and culture. Supporting sections list key enablers (strong data foundation, robust governance, modern platform, partnerships, continuous learning), how to measure success (business impact, use cases in production, adoption rate, time to value, ROI), and tips for long-term success including staying vendor-agnostic and focusing on outcomes over models.

## Key points
- 7 building blocks: define purpose/scope → executive sponsorship → right team → build foundation → launch high-impact use cases → scale/industrialize → drive adoption/culture
- Key enablers: strong data foundation, robust governance & responsible AI, modern technology platform, partnerships/ecosystem, continuous learning
- Success metrics: business impact (revenue/cost/risk/CX), number of use cases in production, adoption rate & satisfaction, time to value, ROI/value realized
- Long-term tips include staying vendor-agnostic/solution-agnostic and evolving the CoE as needs mature
- Frames a CoE's differentiator as delivering transformation, not just models
- Closing mantra: 'Start small. Learn fast. Scale smart.'
