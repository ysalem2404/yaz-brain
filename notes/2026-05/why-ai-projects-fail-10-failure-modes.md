# Why AI Projects Fail: The 10 Failure Modes

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/why-ai-projects-fail-10-failure-modes.png)

## Summary
A brain-shaped map of 10 named AI project failure modes: (1) Hallucination — AI generates false information, causes confusion/mistrust, can spread misinformation; (2) Data Leakage — sensitive data exposed, legal/privacy risk; (3) Bias — unfair treatment from biased data, unfair decisions; (4) Model Drift — performance declines over time as data changes; (5) Adoption Failure — users don't trust the system, teams resist, lack of training; (6) Lack of Clear ROI — value hard to measure, misaligned with business goals; (7) Overfitting — too dependent on past data, struggles with new data; (8) Technical Debt — unmanageable complexity, hard to scale/maintain; (9) Integration Failures — doesn't fit existing systems, disrupts workflows; (10) Lack of Proper Testing — unverified results, bugs remain undiscovered, fails in real-world use.

## Key points
- 10 named, mutually distinct failure modes rather than a vague 'AI can fail' warning
- Adoption Failure and Lack of Clear ROI are treated as equally critical as technical failures like drift or overfitting
- Data Leakage is explicitly tied to legal/privacy consequences, not just a technical incident
- Technical Debt is framed as 'unmanageable complexity' that compounds over time if unaddressed
- Lack of Proper Testing is positioned as the final gate — failures here mean all upstream risks go undetected
