# How Top 15 Companies Adopted AI

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/how-top-15-companies-adopted-ai.png)

## Summary
A 15-company case-study grid of concrete AI adoption metrics. Microsoft: 15M paid Copilot seats (Q2 FY2026) across Word/Excel/Teams/Azure. Salesforce: Agentforce closed 18,500 deals, $500M ARR, saving $100M annually internally. Goldman Sachs: GS AI Assistant deployed to 46,000 employees, 150+ use cases. Google: Gemini Enterprise scaled to 8M seats, $750M fund. Meta: Llama 3 family reached 500M MAU, 350M+ downloads. Amazon: AWS Bedrock Agents GA, MCP-native. NVIDIA: Nemotron Agent Toolkit, $47.5B data center revenue, 4x throughput. JPMorgan Chase: internal LLM Suite across all workflows. Reddit: AI support automation, 46% case deflection, 84% faster resolution. Walmart: agentic commerce + OpenAI partnership. Databricks: Unity AI Governance/MCP GA. Visa: 6 AI partners for delegated agent payments. Abridge: clinical documentation AI across 250 health systems, 80M conversations. PepsiCo: one of first FMCG Agentforce deployments. Anthropic: Claude Code scaled to $1B+ revenue run-rate within 6 months.

## Key points
- Microsoft leads on scale with 15M paid Copilot seats as of Q2 FY2026
- Salesforce Agentforce and Reddit both report concrete efficiency metrics (deals closed, case deflection %) not just adoption counts
- Databricks and Amazon both emphasize MCP-native / governance-native agent infrastructure as differentiators
- Anthropic's Claude Code hit $1B+ run-rate within 6 months — fastest cited revenue ramp in the set
- Abridge's 80M healthcare conversations across 250 health systems is the largest named vertical-specific deployment
