# Claude's 10 Finance Agents (Anthropic, May 2026 Launch)

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/claude-10-finance-agents-anthropic.png)

## Summary
Anthropic's 5 May 2026 launch of 10 purpose-built Claude finance agents, available inside Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Outlook. Research & Client Coverage agents: Pitch builder (branded pitch decks from comps/precedents/LBO numbers), Meeting preparer (one-page briefs from notes/news), Earnings reviewer (flags surprises vs. forecast), Model builder (working financial models from a prompt), Market researcher (sector/competitor memos). Finance & Operations agents: Valuation reviewer (audits and challenges model assumptions), General ledger reconciler (matches books to bank statements), Month-end closer (runs the monthly close checklist end-to-end), Statement auditor (audits P&L/balance sheet for control gaps), KYC screener (vets clients against watchlists). Rollout: install via Cowork plugin settings, admin installs the Microsoft 365 add-ins, connect via 17 data partners at launch, then map one agent to a weekly task to start.

## Key points
- Ships natively inside Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Outlook via Microsoft 365 add-ins — not a standalone app
- Clear split: 5 client/research-facing agents vs. 5 back-office finance/operations agents
- Each agent card includes a literal 'try this prompt' example — designed for immediate non-technical use
- 17 data connector partners available at launch for plugging in bank/market data
- Rollout guidance is deliberately narrow: 'pick one agent, map to a job you do this week, run today'
