# PMI Guide to Leading and Managing AI Projects: PMI-CPMAI Framework, Seven Patterns of AI, and Six Project Phases

## Summary

A 90-page PMI digital guide personally licensed to Yasser Salem (PMI Member ID 2929391) supporting the PMI Certified Professional in Managing AI (PMI-CPMAI) certification — originally the CPMAI methodology cofounded by Kathleen Walch's Cognilytica in 2017, acquired by PMI in 2024. The guide covers why traditional project management frameworks fall short for AI projects, the 'Seven Patterns of AI' (conversational/human interaction, recognition, predictive analytics, goal-driven systems, hyper-personalization, autonomous systems, patterns/anomalies), AI-enabled automation vs. AI agents vs. agentic AI, a Trustworthy AI Framework with five layers, and the Six Phases of the PMI-CPMAI methodology starting with Phase I (Business Understanding) and Phase II (Data Understanding, including the 'Big Data Vs': Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity).

## Key Points

- Personally licensed to Yasser Salem, PMI Member ID 2929391 — this is Yaz's own PMI-CPMAI certification study material, not a generic download
- PMI-CPMAI is about embedding AI into organizational operations, driving adoption, establishing governance, mitigating risk, and transforming business processes — not just using AI to deliver projects faster
- The Seven Patterns of AI: conversational/human interaction, recognition, predictive analytics/decision support, goal-driven systems, hyper-personalization, autonomous systems, patterns and anomalies — most projects require multipattern integration
- Distinguishes automation vs. AI vs. AI agents vs. agentic AI (autonomous workflow intelligence), and covers project management challenges specific to the age of AI agents
- Trustworthy and Ethical AI Considerations built around a five-layer Trustworthy AI Framework, embedded throughout the CPMAI methodology as a living, continuously improving framework
- Six Phases of PMI-CPMAI: Phase I Business Understanding (determining business fit for AI, mapping needs to AI patterns, defining ROI/scope/MVPs) and Phase II Data Understanding (including the Big Data Vs: Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity)
