# Layers of AI: From Classical AI and Machine Learning Through Deep Learning to Agentic AI

## Summary

A six-tier cylindrical stack diagram showing the historical/technical progression of AI capability layers: Classical AI (symbolic AI, expert systems, knowledge representation, logic & reasoning) at the base, up through Machine Learning (supervised/unsupervised, classification, reinforcement learning, regression), Neural Networks (perceptrons, cost functions, backpropagation, activation functions), Deep Learning (transformers, LSTMs, RNNs, CNNs, autoencoders), Generative AI (LLMs, diffusion models, multimodal models, VAEs), and Agentic AI (memory, planning, tool use, autonomous execution) at the top.

## Key Points

- Six-layer stack bottom-to-top: Classical AI -> Machine Learning -> Neural Networks -> Deep Learning -> Generative AI -> Agentic AI
- Classical AI base layer: symbolic AI, expert systems, knowledge representation, logic and reasoning — the pre-ML era of AI
- Deep Learning layer names specific architectures: Transformers, LSTMs, RNNs, CNNs, Autoencoders
- Generative AI layer: LLMs, diffusion models, multimodal models, VAEs — sits just below Agentic AI
- Agentic AI top layer: memory, planning, tool use, autonomous execution — framed as the current frontier built atop all prior layers

## Why Yaz Saved This

A clean historical-progression framing of AI's technical layers from symbolic AI through today's agentic systems — useful as an explainer slide for stakeholders unfamiliar with how modern agentic AI builds on decades of prior ML/DL foundations, distinct from Yaz's other stack diagrams which focus on infrastructure/hardware rather than the ML lineage.
