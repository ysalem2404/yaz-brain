# The Strategy Pyramid: From Purpose to Execution

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/strategy-pyramid-purpose-to-execution.png)

## Summary
A 3-tier, 9-layer strategy pyramid (sourced from Wendy McGuiness's 'The Strategy Pyramid'). Purpose tier: Mission ('why do we exist?'), Values ('what is important to us?'), Vision ('what do we want to be?'). Strategy tier: Strategic Intent ('how will we get there?'), Drivers ('what will we focus on?'), Enablers ('what frameworks, resources, and skills we will use?'). Execution tier: Targets and Initiatives ('what will we need to do?'), Performance Indicators ('how will we know we are successful?'), Strategy Map ('how will we test and communicate the strategy?'). Anchored by Henry Mintzberg's quote: 'Strategy is not the consequence of planning, but the opposite: its starting point.'

## Key points
- Each of the 9 layers is phrased as a single diagnostic question, making it usable as a workshop facilitation tool
- Explicitly separates Purpose (why/values/vision) from Strategy (how) from Execution (what/measurement)
- Enablers layer names 'frameworks, resources, and skills' as a distinct planning input, not an afterthought
- Closes the loop with a literal Strategy Map for testing and communicating the plan, not just producing it
- Mintzberg quote reframes strategy as an origin point, not a downstream output of planning

## Entities & tools mentioned
Wendy McGuiness, Henry Mintzberg, Igor Buinevici

## Why I saved this
Useful structuring tool for framing Bechtel's AI strategy narrative to leadership — mission/vision/values through to measurable execution — beyond just technical architecture slides.
