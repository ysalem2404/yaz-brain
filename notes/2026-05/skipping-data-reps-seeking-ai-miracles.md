# Skipping the Data Reps, Seeking Miracles from AI: A Gym Analogy for AI Transformation Failure

## Summary

Clare Kitching's illustrated gym metaphor contrasts a lone person doing the unglamorous 'data reps' (collect, clean, model, validate, iterate) with a crowd flocking to an 'AI Transformation' supplement stand promising automation, optimization, and magic without doing the foundational work — visually arguing that organizations chasing AI shortcuts without solid data foundations will not get lasting results.

## Key Points

- 'Data Reps' basics: collect, clean, model, validate, iterate — framed as the unglamorous foundational work
- 'AI Transformation' stand promises automate, optimize, and magic — framed as the shortcut most people chase
- Caption: 'No shortcuts. Just strong foundations' displayed on the data-reps side
- Core message: skipping data fundamentals while expecting AI to deliver miracles is a common organizational failure pattern
