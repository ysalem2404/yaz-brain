# 20 Ways to Measure AI Impact: A Color-Coded KPI Framework

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/20-ways-to-measure-ai-impact.png)

## Summary
Clare Kitching's "20 Ways to Measure AI Impact" organizes 20 formula-backed KPIs into six color-coded categories: Financial, Operational, Customer, Scaling, Workforce, and Adoption. Financial metrics include Revenue Uplift from AI, Cost Savings Realised, AI Investment Payback Period, and AI Benefit-Cost Ratio. Operational metrics cover Cycle Time Reduction and Straight-Through Processing Value, while Customer metrics track Sales Conversion Uplift, Customer Retention Uplift, Satisfaction Uplift, and First Contact Resolution Uplift. Scaling metrics (Time to Value, Pilot-to-Production Value Rate, AI Use Case Value Coverage) and Workforce/Adoption metrics (Employee Experience Uplift, Adoption Rate, Business Unit Engagement Rate, AI Literacy Score) round out the framework, overlapping substantially with other enterprise AI KPI cheat sheets captured in this same batch.

## Key points
- Six categories: Financial, Operational, Customer, Scaling, Workforce, Adoption — each color-coded
- Financial metrics: Revenue Uplift from AI, Cost Savings Realised, AI Investment Payback Period, AI Benefit-Cost Ratio, Cost per AI-Assisted Transaction
- Customer metrics: Sales Conversion Uplift, Customer Retention Uplift, Satisfaction Uplift, First Contact Resolution Uplift, Collection Efficiency Improvement
- Scaling metrics: Time to Value, Pilot-to-Production Value Rate, AI Use Case Value Coverage
- Workforce/Adoption metrics: Employee Experience Uplift, Adoption Rate, Business Unit Engagement Rate, AI Literacy Score
- Framework closely parallels the separate 'Enterprise AI KPIs Cheat Sheet (2026)' captured in the same period, suggesting convergence around a standard KPI taxonomy across the industry
