# The Total Cost of AI Ownership: What Business Cases Miss Below the Waterline

## Summary

Clare Kitching's iceberg diagram contrasts the visible costs that get approved in AI business cases (build cost, software licenses, vendor fees, pilot compute) with the much larger hidden costs of actually owning AI in production: data prep and integration, production API costs, retraining/evals/drift monitoring, human-in-the-loop oversight, compliance and audit trail, change management and training, vendor lock-in and exit planning, and shadow AI cleanup.

## Key Points

- Visible/approved costs: build cost, software licenses, vendor fees & initial integrations, pilot compute
- Hidden costs below the waterline: data prep and integration, production API costs, retraining/evals/drift monitoring
- Also hidden: human-in-the-loop oversight, compliance and audit trail, change management and training
- Also hidden: vendor lock-in and exit planning, shadow AI cleanup
- Core message: business cases systematically underestimate the true, larger costs of AI ownership beyond initial build

## Why Yaz Saved This

Directly useful for Yaz's enterprise AI budgeting and business-case work at Bechtel — highlights hidden TCO categories (drift monitoring, shadow AI cleanup, vendor lock-in) often missing from initial AI proposals.
