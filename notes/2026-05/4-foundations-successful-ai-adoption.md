# 4 Foundations for Successful AI Adoption

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/4-foundations-successful-ai-adoption.png)

## Summary
A 4-circle Venn diagram framing successful AI adoption as the intersection of: (1) 'Help people learn' — celebrate wins, experiment safely, show use cases; (2) 'Build AI into work' — save time, redesign workflows, cut manual steps; (3) 'Make tech easy' — right tools, easy integration, reduce friction; (4) 'Give AI the right data' — trusted sources, organized knowledge, high quality. The overlaps name the real success conditions: 'workplace savvy' (people know when/how), 'trust insights,' 'AI used daily,' and 'organizational knowledge' (AI tools understand the org's info). Center convergence = 'We see AI making a difference.'

## Key points
- Adoption succeeds only at the intersection of people, workflow, tooling, and data — not any one alone
- 'Workplace savvy' (knowing when/how to use AI) sits between learning and daily use as a bridging skill
- Data quality foundation explicitly requires trusted sources + organized knowledge, not just volume
- Frictionless integration is treated as equally important as model capability
- Center outcome is behavioral ('AI used daily') not just deployed — adoption ≠ availability
