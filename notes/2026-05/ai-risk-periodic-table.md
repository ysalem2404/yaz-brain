# The AI Risk Periodic Table: 70+ Named Risks Across 7 Categories

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/ai-risk-periodic-table.png)

## Summary
Greg Coquillo's "AI Risk Periodic Table" maps more than 70 named, coded risks (each given a 3-letter element-style abbreviation) across 7 color-coded categories: Model Risks, Data Risks, Security Risks, Governance & Compliance, Operational Risks, Business & Reputation Risks, Human & Ethical Risks, Monitoring & Control, and Agentic AI Risks. Named entries range from familiar risks like HAL (Hallucination), BIA (Bias), and PJI (Prompt Injection) to agentic-specific risks unique to autonomous systems: KILL (Kill Switch Gap — "AI cannot be stopped quickly"), AGT (Agent Autonomy Risk), CHAIN (Multi-Agent Failure — "one agent's error affects others"), GOAL (Goal Misalignment), LOOP (Loop Failure — "agents repeat actions endlessly"), and DEL (Delegation Risk — "agents pass tasks without control"). The table is explicitly framed as a structured map for "every AI team, founder, and enterprise leader" to use as shared risk vocabulary.

## Key points
- 70+ risks coded with 3-4 letter abbreviations across 8 color-coded categories (Model, Data, Security, Governance & Compliance, Operational, Business & Reputation, Human & Ethical, Monitoring & Control, Agentic AI)
- Classic risks included: HAL (Hallucination), BIA (Bias), PJI (Prompt Injection), ADV (Adversarial Attack), DAT (Data Leakage)
- Agentic-specific risk cluster is the most novel: KILL (Kill Switch Gap), AGT (Agent Autonomy Risk), CHAIN (Multi-Agent Failure), GOAL (Goal Misalignment), LOOP (Loop Failure), DEL (Delegation Risk), MEM (Memory Risk)
- Governance & Compliance risks include GOV (Governance Failure), REG (Regulatory Risk), CMP (Compliance Risk), AUD (Audit Gap), OWN (Ownership Gap)
- Security risks include EXT (Data Exfiltration), API (API Abuse), TOK (Token Theft), SUP (Supply Chain Risk)
- Designed as a shared vocabulary/reference table rather than a process framework — intended for risk register and taxonomy-building use
