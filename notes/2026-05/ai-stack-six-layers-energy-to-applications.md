# The AI Stack: Six Essential Layers From Energy to Applications

## Summary

An 'AI Simplified Series' infographic depicting the AI technology stack as six essential layers, from bottom to top: Energy (power for AI computation), Chips (GPUs and specialized hardware), Infrastructure (cloud services and data centers/'AI factories'), Data (training data, curated datasets, pipelines), AI Models (GPT, Claude, DeepSeek as the 'brain' of AI systems), and Applications (business, retail, education, healthcare, public services, security use cases). Each layer depends on the one below it.

## Key Points

- Bottom-up stack: Energy -> Chips (GPUs) -> Infrastructure (cloud/data centers) -> Data (training pipelines) -> AI Models (GPT/Claude/DeepSeek) -> Applications
- Explicitly names GPT, Claude, and DeepSeek as example large models forming the 'brain' layer
- Applications layer spans business/operations, retail/e-commerce, education, health/wellness, public services, and security/safety
- Key message: each layer has a unique role and depends on the one below it; together they form the foundation for intelligent systems

## Why Yaz Saved This

A concise full-stack view of AI infrastructure from energy/hardware up through applications — useful framing for Yaz's enterprise architecture conversations about where cloud compute, chip choices, and model selection fit together, and distinct from his existing '2026 AI Stack: Silicon to Application' capture in its energy-first framing and named-model callouts.
