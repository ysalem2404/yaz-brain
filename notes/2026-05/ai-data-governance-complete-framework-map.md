# AI & Data Governance Complete Framework Map: Principles to Execution

## Summary

A comprehensive eight-section governance map traces the full path from ethics to execution: Ethics & Principles (OECD AI Principles, UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation), Regulation & Laws (EU AI Act risk tiers, EU GDPR's 7 principles and data subject rights), Management Systems (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF's 4 core functions of Govern/Map/Measure/Manage, COBIT 2019), Data Governance & Operations (DAMA-DMBOK's 11 knowledge areas, DCAM maturity model, ISO/IEC 27001), Core Governance Components (people, process, technology), Lifecycles (data lifecycle and AI/model lifecycle from design to retrain), Key Risks (bias, drift, privacy breach, poor data quality, regulatory non-compliance, over-reliance on AI), and Key Deliverables mapped to each governance area.

## Key Points

- 8-section framework: Ethics & Principles → Regulation & Laws → Management Systems → Data Governance & Operations → Core Components → Lifecycles → Key Risks → Key Deliverables
- EU AI Act risk-based tiers: unacceptable, high, limited, minimal risk with specific requirements per tier
- NIST AI RMF's 4 core functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage, outputting risk assessment/metrics/mitigation plan/monitoring dashboard
- DAMA-DMBOK's 11 data management knowledge areas including data architecture, quality, and governance
- Key risks tracked: bias/fairness, data privacy breach, security threats, lack of explainability, model drift, poor data quality, regulatory non-compliance, over-reliance on AI
- Key deliverables table maps each governance area (governance, risk & compliance, data, monitoring, security) to specific concrete outputs
