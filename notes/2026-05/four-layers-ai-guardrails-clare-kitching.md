# 4 Layers of AI Guardrails: Governance, Operating Model, Process, and System

## Summary

Clare Kitching's four-layer guardrail model nests governance (setting risk appetite, accountability, and oversight via risk frameworks and ethics committees) around an operating model (translating strategy into everyday behavior through usage policies and staff training), around process (embedding safeguards into delivery via human review and approval/escalation paths), around a technical system layer (output validation, content filters, prompt guardrails, PII detection). Supporting statistics show 55% of organizations have an AI board or oversight body (Gartner), 32% of employees using AI at work keep it secret from their employer (Security Magazine), and in high-maturity organizations 57% of business units trust new AI solutions versus 14% in low-maturity organizations (Gartner).

## Key Points

- 4 nested layers: Governance (outermost) → Operating Model → Process → System (innermost, most technical)
- Governance layer: risk framework, AI & ethics committees, accountability model
- Operating Model layer: usage policies, staff training, shadow AI management
- Process layer: human review, approval & escalation paths, monitoring
- System layer: output validation, content filters, prompt guardrails, personal information detection
- 55% of organizations have an AI board or oversight body (Gartner)
- 32% of employees using AI at work keep it secret from their employer (Security Magazine) — a shadow AI risk signal
- High-maturity orgs: 57% of business units trust new AI solutions vs 14% in low-maturity orgs (Gartner)
