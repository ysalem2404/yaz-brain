# AI Strategy KPI Periodic Table 2026: 36 KPIs Across 6 Categories

## Summary

A periodic-table-style reference organizes 36 KPIs for enterprise AI strategy into six color-coded categories: ROI & Financial Impact (cost savings realized, payback period, revenue impact, cost per AI task, AI program P&L, time to value), Operational Performance (throughput improvement, task completion rate, error reduction, straight-through processing rate, automation rate, cycle time reduction), Adoption & Change (AI adoption rate, active usage frequency, AI literacy score, automation rate, business unit engagement, change resistance index), Delivery & Scaling (use case coverage, deployment speed, reusability rate, pilot-to-production rate, stakeholder satisfaction score, integration readiness), Risk & Governance (human review rate, incident response time, scalability readiness, model drift rate, hallucination rate, compliance adherence, risk coverage), and Quality & Output Value (accuracy score, output quality score, consistency rate, relevance score, feedback improvement rate, trust score).

## Key Points

- 36 KPIs organized into 6 categories: ROI & Financial Impact, Operational Performance, Adoption & Change, Delivery & Scaling, Risk & Governance, Quality & Output Value
- ROI KPIs include Payback Period, Revenue Impact, AI Program P&L, and Time to Value
- Risk & Governance KPIs include Model Drift Rate, Hallucination Rate, Compliance Adherence, and Risk Coverage
- Adoption KPIs include AI Literacy Score, Change Resistance Index, and Business Unit Engagement
- Delivery & Scaling includes Pilot-to-Production Rate and Scalability Readiness as key scale-up gates
- Framed as core building blocks for measuring adoption, value, scale, and control in enterprise AI programs
