# How Leaders Create High-Performing Teams: 6 Frameworks (RAPID, Team Stages, Trust Triangle, Johari Window)

## Summary

A leadership reference compiles six management frameworks for building high-performing teams: the RAPID decision-making framework (Recommend, Agree, Perform, Input, Decide), the Five Stages of Team Development (Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning), the Energy vs. Impact Matrix (mapping people by engagement and contribution into promote/coach/reignite/reassess quadrants), the 70-20-10 Learning Principle (70% hands-on experience, 20% mentoring/coaching, 10% formal training), the Trust Triangle (authenticity, empathy, logic), and the Johari Window (open, blind, hidden, and unknown areas of self-awareness).

## Key Points

- RAPID framework: Recommend (propose plan), Agree (secure buy-in), Perform (execute), Input (provide expertise), Decide (make final call)
- Five Stages of Team Development: Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning — each with a description of team dynamics
- Energy vs. Impact Matrix: high-energy/high-impact = promote and empower; low-energy/low-impact = reassess role or exit
- 70-20-10 Learning Principle: 70% through hands-on experience, 20% through mentoring/coaching, 10% through formal training
- Trust Triangle: trust grows through authenticity (genuine/consistent), empathy (people feel understood), and logic (reasoning holds up)
- Johari Window: open area, blind spot, hidden area, unknown area — a model for feedback and self-awareness
