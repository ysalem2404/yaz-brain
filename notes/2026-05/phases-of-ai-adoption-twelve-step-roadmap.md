# Phases of AI Adoption: Foundation, Enablement, Execution, Scale (12-Step Roadmap)

## Summary

Jason Moccia's twelve-step AI adoption roadmap is organized into four phases: Foundation (executive sponsorship, business-aligned strategy, readiness assessment), Enablement (data foundation, governance before scaling, high-impact pilots), Execution (workflow redesign, change management/communication, training/upskilling at every level), and Scale (appoint AI champions, track KPIs/measure ROI, scale what works and build for continuous innovation). Each step includes a short rationale for why it matters at that stage.

## Key Points

- Foundation phase: executive sponsorship & leadership commitment, business-aligned strategy (not just tech trends), readiness assessment
- Enablement phase: clean/governed data foundation, governance established before scaling (not after an incident), 2-3 high-impact pilot workflows
- Execution phase: redesign workflows (not just add AI to existing ones), change management addressing job-displacement fears, role-tailored training
- Scale phase: appoint internal AI champions bridging IT and business, track KPIs beyond accuracy, systematically expand proven pilots
- Core principle: governance and data foundations must precede scaling, not follow an incident
