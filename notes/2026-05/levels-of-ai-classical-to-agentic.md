# Levels of AI: From Classical AI to Agentic AI

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/levels-of-ai-classical-to-agentic.png)

## Summary
A 6-tier nested hierarchy of AI concepts, broadest to narrowest/most advanced: Artificial Intelligence (intelligent robotics, reinforcement learning, speech recognition, expert systems, AI ethics, cognitive computing); Machine Learning (K-Means, PCA, decision trees, linear/logistic regression, KNN, SVM, Naive Bayes); Neural Networks (Boltzmann Machine, CNN, backpropagation, MLP, feed-forward, perceptron, Hopfield network, RNN); Deep Learning (CNN, RNN, LSTM, GAN, autoencoders, transformers, deep belief networks, self-organizing maps); Generative AI (RLHF, LLMs, few-shot learning, GANs, foundation models, diffusion models, multimodal AI, LangChain); Agentic AI (memory & context handling, multi-agent systems, feedback loops, workflow automation, AI agents, goal-oriented reasoning, tool use/integration, autonomous decision-making).

## Key points
- Presents AI/ML/DL/GenAI/Agentic AI as strictly nested subsets, each layer narrower and more specialized than the last
- Transformers appear under Deep Learning, while LLMs and LangChain appear under the separate Generative AI layer
- Neural Networks is treated as its own tier distinct from (and beneath) Deep Learning — an unusual but pedagogically clear split
- Agentic AI tier explicitly lists 'tool use & integration' and 'autonomous decision-making' as defining traits, not just automation
- Useful as a single-slide taxonomy for orienting audiences unfamiliar with where 'AI agents' sit relative to classical ML
