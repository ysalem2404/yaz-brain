# How to Explain AI Types: Predictive, Generative, Agentic, Physical (vertical layout variant)

## Summary

A vertical four-column variant of the four-AI-types framework (Predictive, Generative, Agentic, Physical AI) by Jason Moccia, covering the same conceptual ground as the AI For Leaders circular version captured earlier in this backfill (same batch 7) but with different visual design, author, and slightly expanded sub-bullets: predictive AI examples extend to anomaly detection for fraud/security; generative AI includes workflow automation and RAG-based knowledge systems; agentic AI covers multi-agent orchestration and AI product integration; physical AI covers autonomous vehicles/drones and spatial intelligence (3D computer vision, LiDAR, sensor fusion).

## Key Points

- Predictive AI: predictive analytics, classification systems, anomaly detection (fraud, system failures, security breaches)
- Generative AI: content generation, workflow automation, knowledge systems (RAG) fed with custom business data
- Agentic AI: AI agents & tool use, multi-agent orchestration, AI product integration into products/services
- Physical AI: robotics & automation, autonomous systems (vehicles/drones), spatial intelligence via 3D vision/LiDAR/sensor fusion
- Same four-category conceptual structure as the AI For Leaders version captured in batch 7, different author and visual style
- Positioned as founder/product-launch insight content rather than enterprise leadership framing
