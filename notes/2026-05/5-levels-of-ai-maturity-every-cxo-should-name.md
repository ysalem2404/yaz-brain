# The 5 Levels of AI Maturity Every CXO Should Be Able to Name

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/5-levels-of-ai-maturity-every-cxo-should-name.png)

## Summary
Carolyn Healey's maturity model opens with a pointed scenario — a board asking where the AI money is, and a leader showing "47 pilots, 12 dashboards, and a Copilot rollout" that "none of it answered the question" — before laying out 5 maturity levels: Curious (AI is mostly conversation, no funded strategy), Experimenting ("Pilot Theater" — visible activity but hard-to-prove value, shadow AI spreading), Producing ("At Least One Thing Actually Works" — named owners, budget, and executive sponsorship on live systems), Scaling (AI as Infrastructure — governance scales across functions, workflow redesign matters more than tool deployment), and Native (AI-Powered Operating Model — agentic systems operate inside audit-able, reversible boundaries and AI changes how the business competes). Each level pairs with a specific "board test" question a CXO should be able to answer, and the piece closes by asking what maturity level the org operates at and "the cost of staying there for another quarter."

## Key points
- Level 1 Curious: AI is mostly conversation, no funded strategy, no production use cases, discussion driven by competitive anxiety
- Level 2 Experimenting ('Pilot Theater'): visible PoCs, uneven Copilot/tool rollout, shadow AI spreading, measurement focused on adoption not impact
- Level 3 Producing: one or more systems in production with named owners/budget/sponsorship; governance emerging but not embedded
- Level 4 Scaling (AI as Infrastructure): AI factored into every major digital initiative; governance scales across functions; workflow redesign prioritized over tool deployment
- Level 5 Native (AI-Powered Operating Model): no more separate 'AI projects'; agentic systems operate inside auditable, reversible boundaries; AI changes how the org competes
- Each level paired with a specific board-test question, e.g. Level 5: 'Has AI changed how the business operates, learns, and competes?'
