# Must-Know AI Terms: 12 Core Concepts (LLM, Hallucination, Token, RAG, Fine-tuning, and More)

## Summary

AI For Leaders' glossary-style infographic defines twelve foundational AI terms in plain language: LLM (the AI brain behind ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Copilot), hallucination (confidently incorrect outputs, often from missing context), token (basic unit of AI communication, a word chunk), training vs inference, fine-tuning (specializing a general model), reinforcement learning (agent-environment-reward loop), distillation (teaching a smaller model to mimic a larger one), RAG (grounding answers in your own documents to reduce hallucination), chain of thought (breaking complex problems into steps), weights (learned parameters), validation loss (a training-quality score, lower is better), and coding agents (autonomous code writing/testing/debugging).

## Key Points

- LLM: the AI brain behind ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot — predicts next word at scale
- Hallucination: confidently incorrect output, often from missing data/context
- Token: basic unit of AI communication (a chunk of a word, not a whole word) — more tokens means more cost
- Fine-tuning: takes a general model and specializes it with targeted data for a specific job
- RAG: combines AI with your own documents/data to reduce hallucinations and keep answers current
- Chain of Thought: breaks a complex problem into sequential steps — slower but often more accurate
- Coding Agent: an AI that writes, tests, and debugs code more autonomously than a simple assistant
