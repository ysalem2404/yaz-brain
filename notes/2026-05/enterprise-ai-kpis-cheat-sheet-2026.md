# Enterprise AI KPIs Cheat Sheet (2026): 25 Metrics Across 5 Categories

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/enterprise-ai-kpis-cheat-sheet-2026.png)

## Summary
Vaibhav Aggarwal's "Enterprise AI KPIs Cheat Sheet (2026)" catalogs 25 formula-backed metrics organized into five categories: ROI & Financial, Operational, Scaling and Delivery, Governance & Risk, and Adoption & Change. Financial metrics include Cost Savings Realised, AI Investment Payback Period, Revenue Impact, Cost Per AI Transaction, and AI Program P&L. Operational metrics track Cycle Time Reduction, Process Automation Rate, Straight-Through Processing Rate, Error Rate Reduction, and Agent Task Completion Rate. Governance & Risk metrics — Model Drift Rate, Compliance Adherence Rate, Hallucination/Error Rate, Incident Response Time, and AI Risk Coverage — are the most relevant for regulated enterprise reporting. The sheet closes with Adoption & Change metrics like Employee AI Adoption Rate, Change Resistance Index, and AI Literacy Score.

## Key points
- ROI & Financial: Cost Savings Realised, AI Investment Payback Period, Revenue Impact, Cost Per AI Transaction, AI Program P&L
- Operational: Cycle Time Reduction, Process Automation Rate, Straight-Through Processing Rate, Error Rate Reduction, Agent Task Completion Rate
- Scaling and Delivery: Time to Deploy New AI Agent, Pilot to Production Rate, AI Use Case Coverage, Reusability Rate, Time to Value
- Governance & Risk: Model Drift Rate, Compliance Adherence Rate, Hallucination/Error Rate, Incident Response Time, AI Risk Coverage
- Adoption & Change: Employee AI Adoption Rate, Change Resistance Index, AI Literacy Score, Business Unit Engagement Rate
- Every KPI is paired with an explicit calculation formula, making the sheet usable as a direct reporting template
