# GRC Framework for the AI Era: Governance, Risk & Compliance Wheel with Agentic AI Oversight

## Summary

Excellog.Biz's GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance) wheel adapts classic GRC to the AI era across eight segments: Governance (board accountability, decision rights), Risk Intelligence (KRIs, scenario analysis), Compliance (regulatory obligations, control evidence), AI Governance (agentic AI oversight, model risk, responsible AI use), Cyber Resilience (identity, cloud, third-party exposure), Operational Resilience (continuity, incident readiness), Data Integrity (trusted data, audit trails, privacy), and Integrated Assurance (control testing, executive reporting). It also includes a six-part governance framework (strategy, policies, roles, culture, oversight committees, transparency) and a five-step risk/compliance cycle (identify, assess, mitigate, monitor, report).

## Key Points

- 8-segment GRC wheel: Governance, Risk Intelligence, Compliance, AI Governance, Cyber Resilience, Operational Resilience, Data Integrity, Integrated Assurance
- AI Governance segment specifically covers agentic AI oversight, model risk, and responsible AI use
- Governance framework: strategy & risk appetite, policies & standards, roles & ownership, culture & ethics, oversight committees, transparency & reporting
- Risk & compliance cycle: identify, assess, mitigate, monitor, report (continuous loop)
- Four outer callouts: governance alignment, regulatory fragmentation, policy architecture, executive oversight
- Positions AI governance as one segment within a broader enterprise GRC wheel rather than a standalone function
