# Agentic AI Strategy Playbooks: The 12 Reports Every Leader Should Read (May 2026)

![Infographic](public/img/notes/2026-05/agentic-ai-strategy-playbooks-may-2026.png)

## Summary
Curated by Stephanie Shills, this reading-list infographic ranks 12 major industry reports on agentic AI published between September 2025 and May 2026, grouped into Strategy, Build, and Leadership & Governance categories. Strategy picks include Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index (May 2026), Deloitte's State of AI in the Enterprise 2026 (January 2026), BCG's AI Radar 2026 (January 2026), and Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index Report (April 2026). Build picks cover Google Cloud's AI Agent Trends 2026, McKinsey's "Building the Foundations for Agentic AI at Scale" (April 2026), and IBM's Agentic AI Operating Model (May 2026). Leadership & Governance picks include McKinsey's State of AI Trust in 2026 (governance and risk controls for the agentic era), PwC's Agentic AI Reinvention, Accenture's Age of Co-Intelligence, BCG's The Emerging Agentic Enterprise, and McKinsey's The Agentic Organization.

## Key points
- 12 reports grouped into 3 categories: Strategy, Build, Leadership & Governance
- Strategy: Microsoft 2026 Work Trend Index, Deloitte State of AI in the Enterprise 2026, BCG AI Radar 2026, Stanford HAI 2026 AI Index Report
- Build: Google Cloud AI Agent Trends 2026, McKinsey 'Building the Foundations for Agentic AI at Scale,' IBM Agentic AI Operating Model
- Leadership & Governance: McKinsey State of AI Trust in 2026 explicitly flagged as covering 'why most organizations aren't ready for autonomous AI'
- Also includes PwC Agentic AI Reinvention, Accenture Age of Co-Intelligence, BCG The Emerging Agentic Enterprise, McKinsey The Agentic Organization
- Each entry includes publisher, exact publication month, and a one-line editorial summary of its unique angle
