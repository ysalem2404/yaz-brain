# Microsoft AI Stack for Project Managers: 10 Tools Mapped to Delivery Stages

## Summary

A project-management-focused reference maps ten Microsoft AI capabilities to specific stages of project delivery: Microsoft Copilot for everyday drafting/summaries/admin, Copilot in Teams for meeting notes and action capture, Copilot in Outlook for stakeholder email drafts, Copilot in Word for briefs and governance notes, Copilot in Excel for RAID log and budget analysis, Copilot in PowerPoint for steering packs and executive updates, Microsoft Loop for collaborative planning, Copilot Studio for building custom repeat-workflow assistants, Azure AI Foundry for enterprise AI solution design, and Phi models for lightweight, fast, focused tasks. The simple rule offered: Copilot for daily productivity, M365 apps for delivery outputs, and Copilot Studio/Azure AI Foundry for repeatable AI systems.

## Key Points

- Microsoft Copilot: everyday drafting, summaries, project admin support
- Copilot in Teams: captures meeting notes, decisions, actions, follow-ups
- Copilot in Excel: analyzes RAID logs, trackers, budgets, and trends
- Copilot in PowerPoint: builds steering packs, roadmap decks, executive updates
- Copilot Studio: builds custom assistants for repeat project workflows
- Azure AI Foundry: designs enterprise AI solutions, agents, and connected workflows
- Phi Models: lightweight AI for fast, efficient, focused tasks
- Simple rule: Copilot=daily productivity, M365 apps=delivery outputs, Copilot Studio/Azure AI Foundry=repeatable systems
